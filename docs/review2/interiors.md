# Review 2 (Nov 20, after round 2) — segment `interiors` (score 5/10)

Not amazing yet. The lit rooms now have natural colour and a full tonal range, but they read as oversized, under-dressed boxes under charcoal ceilings with blotchy halos. The blackout mostly fails its premise: the flashlight's bounce light and the battery units' own bounce lights wash the rooms almost as bright as with the power on (office mean luma 71 to 56), and in the one room where the beam does dominate (feed prep) it is a clipped white disc. Fix the flashlight bounce and the blackout exposure, light the ceilings evenly and triple the dressing, and this segment can go from a 5 to an 8.

Strengths:
- Natural, neutral-warm colour in the lit rooms: no sepia, teal or single-colour flood. Tonal range is full and nothing clips except lamp lenses (in the lit rooms the darkest 1 % sits at 7-18/255 and the median at 54-96).
- Believable fixtures: louvred troffers, return-air grilles, cornices, and wall-mounted twin-head battery units that throw warm scallops (corridor_dark, records_dark, office_dark).
- Strong story props and signs: a CCTV wall with night feeds that turns to static in the blackout, cork boards with printed notes, a CONTROL ROOM sign with a green card-reader LED, a RECORDS / ARCHIVE sign, branded merchandise and a hanging WELCOME sign, extinguishers, a CRT and filing cabinets.
- Lobby: warm wall grazers against the blue storm sky through the glass roof, and a polished floor with SSR reflections and a leak puddle. In the blackout, moonlight casts the roof-beam shadows across the left wall and the beam draws a soft shaft in the haze.
- breakroom_dark and the staff-exit collapse show the target: a soft pool reveals the cork board or the rubble, falls off naturally, and leaves readable silhouettes around it.
- The UI is clean and consistent: objective panel, light badge, captions and radio subtitles.

## Findings

- **[blocker] office_dark.png** (lighting): The blackout does not read as a blackout. The powerless security office is 79 % as bright as the lit one (mean luma 56 vs 71). The back wall is 3x brighter than with the power on (linear 0.34 vs 0.11), and the brightest thing in frame is a ceiling patch at the wall junction (sRGB ~211, peaks 255), not the beam. No cone or beam edge reads: the room is an even grey wash. The same happens in records_dark.png, where the ceiling (linear 0.21) is 8x brighter than the brick wall the player is looking at (0.026), and in corridor_dark.png, where the beam cannot be told apart from the emergency lights.
  - Likely cause: FlashlightController.cs: the bounce is a shadowless point light of BeamCandela x BounceShare 0.034 (up to ~245 cd). It is parked at hit.point + hit.normal x standoff (up to 1.4 m), i.e. at the height where the beam lands. Aimed at the top of a wall, it sits ~0.5 m under the ceiling and puts several hundred lux on the tiles, which is the 'bare point light under a ceiling' the bible bans. On the wall itself it adds ~110 lux to a beam capped at HotSpotMaxLux 75. On top of that, every room keeps 1-2 Light_*_Batt units (LookLights.Zone1.cs: 340 cd, Shadow.Low), and EnsureBounce gives each one a 153 cd floor bounce and a 37 cd wall fill. Finally, the night/storm exposure range (evMin 3.0 to evMax 5.0) lets the camera open ~2 stops when the power dies, which undoes most of the darkness.
  - Suggested fix: Set BounceShare to ~0.006-0.008 (40-60 cd), standoff to >= 0.3 x hit distance (min 1.5 m), and keep the bounce >= 1.2 m below the ceiling (one raycast up); count it inside the HotSpotMaxLux budget. Remove the floor bounce and wall fill from _Batt units (EnsureBounce wanted:false) and keep one unit per room, by the exit. Give enclosed blackout rooms evMin ~3.7. Targets: unlit walls 15-30/255, battery pools 60-100, beam pool 150-200, with the beam as the brightest thing in frame.
- **[major] office_on.png** (lighting): Charcoal ceilings with blotchy halos sit over evenly lit walls (also in lockers_on, breakroom_on, corridor_on). Between fixtures the tiles are sRGB 34-46 (25 in the staff corridor) while the walls are ~90-135. With a 0.65-albedo tile texture, the ceiling gets roughly a tenth of the light the walls get (~1/30 in the corridor). Each troffer has a bright oval round it and the corners go near-black, so it reads as a black lid, not a white drop ceiling. The control room (03_ch1_control_room_lit.png, ceiling ~120 over ~106 walls) shows what it should look like.
  - Likely cause: Zone1_VisitorCenterBuilder.TagWashReceivers (l.62-71) keeps ceiling slabs off the WashLayer, so the perimeter washes never reach the tiles. The only ceiling light is the floor bounce from LookLights.EnsureBounce. Its range is reach + 1.5 m, so it makes a hot oval over the lamp and gives ~1/6 of that 3 m away. The corridor has 4 CeilingWash lights for 80 m (l.646). Look_Indoor's indirectDiffuseLightingMultiplier of 0.20 at night (IndoorDiffuseNight) also scales HDRP's SSGI, so the real bounce off the lit floor is cut to a fifth.
  - Suggested fix: Aim for even ceilings at 35-50 % of wall luminance (sRGB ~70-90 next to ~110 walls). Either let the washes reach the tiles at half strength, or add one wide (170 deg) upward fill per ~25 m2 with range >= 2x the floor-to-ceiling height. Stretch the floor-bounce range to ~2.5x reach with a lower peak. Put a corridor ceiling fill every 6-8 m. Test SSGI with the indoor multiplier at 1.0 and a dark per-room probe as the fallback.
- **[major] breakroom_on.png** (geometry): The rooms read as empty greybox halls, not a working 1990s staff wing. The break room is 22 x 13 m with three tables. The gift shop is 22 x 20 m, the security office 14 x 12 m with one desk, and feed prep 20 x 32 m with two tables (giftshop_on, office_on, lockers_on, 12_ch3_feed_prep). Floors are bare, walls blank, and every surface is spotless: no scuffs, stains, cables, papers or clutter. In RE7 or Alien: Isolation every surface tells a story.
  - Likely cause: The BuildBoxRoomShell sizes in Zone1_VisitorCenterBuilder are 2-7x the floor area each room's function needs, and the Dress* functions furnish them as if they were small rooms.
  - Suggested fix: Without moving gameplay geometry: partition each room (counters, shelving runs, half-height walls, a kitchenette island) and roughly triple the furniture and wall dressing (whiteboards, rosters, calendars, key cabinet, radio charger rack, wall phone, switches and outlets, exit signs, sprinklers). Add floor clutter (boxes, carts, mop bucket, cable runs) and a grime decal pass (skirting scuffs, water stains under the ceiling, dirt in corners). Feed prep needs steel prep tables, sinks, floor drains, hoses, hooks, a freezer door and tubs. Re-run the route test afterwards.
- **[major] 12_ch3_feed_prep.png** (exposure): The flashlight on white tile is the 'white disc on black' the criteria rule out. The core is 252-255 and the tile texture is gone over ~80 px, ringed by a featureless round glow, while the rest of the room drops to 9-16/255 (the doors are at 9).
  - Likely cause: The beam is capped at <= 75 lux, but the bounce point light sits 1.4 m in front of the wall at ~200-225 cd and adds ~105-115 lux to the same spot (FlashlightController BounceShare 0.034, standoff clamp 1.4). That is about 3x white on 0.55-albedo tile at EV 3. HotSpotMaxLux 75 assumes the game's usual 0.05-0.30 albedos; the code comment already notes that white tile clips.
  - Suggested fix: With the bounce fix from the blocker, scale the hot-spot budget by the hit surface's albedo (~30 lux on white tile, 75 on dark concrete), or compute _adapt from beam + bounce together. Keep the core <= 220/255 so the grout still reads.
- **[major] corridor_dark_noflash.png** (exposure): With the flashlight off, the right wall and ceiling are crushed to sRGB 8-9 with no texture (std ~1), against a left wall at ~38 and a floor at ~55. A third of the frame sits at the black lift, and only the chair-rail highlight shows that a wall is there.
  - Likely cause: Nothing bounces light between surfaces. Battery heads are 20 m apart (Zone1_VisitorCenterBuilder l.648) and their floor bounces reach only ~6 m. The indoor multiplier of 0.20 also scales SSGI, so the lit floor and left wall send nothing onto the right wall.
  - Suggested fix: Give interiors a dim, cool ambient floor so unlit walls sit at 15-25/255 (see the colour finding), or run SSGI at full strength indoors.
- **[major] 08_ch1_staff_exit_collapse.png** (colour): The blackout interiors go amber-olive sepia, warmer than the lit rooms (also corridor_dark, records_dark, office_dark, breakroom_dark). Mean R/G is 1.16-1.25 with B/G 0.77-0.86, against 1.10-1.13 and 0.85-0.89 with the power on. Inside, the storm night has no cool counterpoint, and the staff-exit frame is olive-brown from ceiling to floor.
  - Likely cause: The 3300 K battery heads, plus their same-colour floor bounces and wall fills, light every surface. Look_Indoor sets a neutral white balance and cuts the blue sky ambient to 0.20, so nothing cool is left except the beam.
  - Suggested fix: Keep the battery heads warm but as islands (no bounce or fill). Give enclosed blackout rooms a dim blue-grey base of 2-4 lux at ~7500 K, e.g. indoor diffuse 0.4 under NightStormBlackout with the night white balance kept. Keep the beam and its bounce at 5600-6000 K.
- **[major] giftshop_on.png** (lighting): A bright oval on the ceiling just in front of the back wall (sRGB ~105, peaks 220) has no fixture in it. Around it, the centre of the ceiling is black with the power on (13/255). The result is light without a source next to a crushed void.
  - Likely cause: The oval sits exactly where the middle north perimeter wash aims (Washes(..., walls: "NNNSEW"), 450 cd pointed up at the junction). The gift-shop ceiling has evidently escaped TagWashReceivers' ceilingSlab test, so the wash hits the tiles. The 440 m2 room also has only 4 troffers 10 m apart (DressGiftShop fx[]), a quarter of the offices' density of one fixture per ~40 m2.
  - Suggested fix: Exclude the gift-shop ceiling from the wash layer (check the bounds test or the renderer name). Add a second row of troffers or a 3x3 grid so the ceiling and the merchandise tables are evenly lit.
- **[major] 08_ch1_staff_exit_collapse.png** (materials): The green peeling-plaster walls read as giant crackle wallpaper: the crack cells are person-sized, and the same red-brown blotches repeat in a regular grid down both walls, which fill most of the frame.
  - Likely cause: WorldMaterialPalette.PlasterPeeling90s uses a 3.0 m triplanar tile (l.120), with no second layer to break the repeat.
  - Suggested fix: Tile it at ~1.2-1.5 m and break the repeat with a random UV offset per wall segment and a large-scale variation mask or detail layer. Add decals: water streaks, scuffs and a few large peels.

- **[minor] corridor_dark.png** (lighting): The red emergency heads read as pink LEDs, not small red pools. The lens core clips to pink (sRGB 251,173,185) with a red halo, and no red light falls on the wall or floor around it.
  - Likely cause: Light_Corr_Emerg is 130 cd through the Red filter (1, 0.10, 0.06), only ~37 cd in luminance terms, i.e. ~6 lux on the floor. The lens emission is too hot for EV 3.
  - Suggested fix: Lower the lens to ~8-12 nits and a deeper red so it stays red. Raise the lamp to ~250-300 cd so a small red pool (~25-35 lux) reads on the wall under each head.
