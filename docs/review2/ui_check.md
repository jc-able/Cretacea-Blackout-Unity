# Review 2 second opinion — segment `ui`

First critic 7/10, second critic 7/10. Kept 12, dropped 1, added 3.

## Kept (confirmed in the image)

- **[major] ~/CretaceaBlackoutUnity/Screenshots/review2/route/29_end_screen.png** (lighting / end-screen plate): The sun on the horizon, right of centre, is a flat grey disc: every pixel is (194,194,194), crossed by a lemon band (194,194,4-86) inside a lemon halo. Its bottom edge (y≈293) sits on the stats card's top edge (y≈296), so the card looks like it is cutting the sun. Cause: 194 sRGB is 0.54 linear, which is clipped white × (1 − 0.46). The sun clips in the render (R and G at 1.0), and the end-screen plate (MenuController.BuildEndingOverlay → CreateBackdrop(root, "EndingRoot", 0.46f), blended in linear) turns every clipped pixel into dead grey. The DawnCoast key in AtmosphereController.cs (KeyLook default case) is 115000 lux at 5750 K, which is near white, even though its comment says 'peach, not lemon'.
  - Seen: I sampled the disc and band pixels directly: R and G are pinned at 194 and only B varies. At 4x zoom it reads as a grey sticker, duller than the orange clouds around it, and the dark card edge sits right under it.
  - Fix: Keep the disc under the clip at the source. Bring the DawnCoast key down toward 3200-3800 K and tint or dim the disc (HDAdditionalLightData surfaceTint about (1.0, 0.6, 0.38), a lower flareMultiplier, a peach flareTint). On the end screen, replace the flat 0.46 plate with a neutral gradient or radial plate: about 0.15 over the sun and its glint on the sea, about 0.5 behind the card and at the top. Use neutral rather than warm, because the frame is already all warm (see missed). Move the card down about 40 ref px, or reframe the camera, so the sun is not tangent to the card.

## Added by the second critic

- **[major] ~/CretaceaBlackoutUnity/Screenshots/review2/route/29_end_screen.png** (lighting / end-screen plate (palette)): The payoff frame is a monochrome dark-brown dusk, not the bible's DawnCoast ('peach to pale-blue sky, teal water'). Outside the UI, only 2.5% of scene pixels have blue above red. The scene's mean colour is (63,47,27); the upper sky is warm grey (47,41,39) and the sea on the left is brown (39,29,20). There is no blue or teal anywhere, which is exactly the 'colours are off / sepia' complaint. Two causes: the DawnCoast grade (LookAtmosphereProfiles.cs ~L288-311: temperature +1, tint +3, smhHighlights (1.06,1.0,0.95)) plus a near-white 115 klux key gilding the whole sea; and the end-screen plate of 0.46 plus a 0.55 vignette (MenuController.CreateBackdrop, blended in linear), which takes the centre to about 0.54× and the corners to about 0.25×, so the dawn reads as night falling.
  - Fix: Lighting side: let the zenith and the sea away from the sun read cool. Check that zenithTint (0.82,0.90,1.0) actually reaches the frame, push smhShadows bluer (about 0.92, 0.98, 1.12), drop the warm highlight bias, and warm only the sun and its glint path. UI side: use a lighter neutral gradient plate (about 0.2-0.3 over the sky and sea, about 0.5 only behind the card) with a vignette of at most 0.3, so the ending is the brightest, most open frame in the game.
- **[minor] ~/CretaceaBlackoutUnity/Screenshots/review2/ui/journal_tab2.png** (ui (tablet tabs)): The selected tablet tab scales 3% from its right edge (CretaceaButtonFx.cs L109: localScale = 1 + 0.03·t for hover or selected), so its left edge jumps about 6 px when chosen. The gap before it shrinks to 3 px against 9 px elsewhere: SYSTEMS ends at x=940 and the selected DOCUMENTS starts at x=943. MAP & OBJECTIVES starts at x=565 when selected, 571 otherwise. The row looks uneven and the tab twitches on every switch, and the scaled label is slightly soft.
  - Fix: Do not scale tabs; the chosen fill and amber border already mark them (use the 'wide' no-scale path). Or set the tab pivot to the centre and keep at least 8 px gaps.
- **[minor] ~/CretaceaBlackoutUnity/Screenshots/review2/ui/settings.png** (ui (key prompts)): No controls reference exists anywhere. The pause menu offers Resume / Restart Checkpoint / Settings / Main Menu, the settings 'CONTROLS & ACCESSIBILITY' column holds only sensitivity, invert and caption toggles, and the main menu footer shows only Enter/Esc (MenuController ~L576-581). The flashlight key is taught only by a chip that fades after 8 s. A new player who misses a prompt (flashlight, crouch, sprint, interact) has nowhere to look it up, which RE7 and TLOU both provide.
  - Fix: Add a CONTROLS page (keyboard and gamepad glyph list from the same key atlas) reachable from pause and settings, or a controls card on the tablet. Rebinding can come later.
