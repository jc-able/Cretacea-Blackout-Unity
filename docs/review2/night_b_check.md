## Kept (confirmed in the image)

- **[major] NightStormBlackout_yard_eye.png** (weather / lightning): The frame is mid-strike. The stacked containers become glowing white boxes: the green face is 181/181/169 here against 18/19/19 in play frame 24 from almost the same spot. The pad under the drum goes white (median 224), the pallets go white, and the wet asphalt on the left turns into a frost-like field of white glitter. The sky stays black (31/34/41) with no lit cloud. The ground flashes and the sky does not, so it reads as an exposure glitch rather than lightning. It happens every 6.5-16 s outdoors in chapters 2-6 (AtmosphereController.cs:353, rate 1.0 x lightningRateScale 1.0).
  - Seen: Seen in the frame and confirmed by sampling the regions. I downgraded it from blocker: the strike lasts about 0.25 s near peak (LightningFlashRoutine: 0.07 s at peak, 0.06 s at 20 %, 0.10 s at 85 %, then a 0.32 s fade), and a flash is meant to be bright, so it does not break the game. What makes it read as a glitch on every strike is the black sky and the glitter, which will shimmer in motion.
  - Fix: Lower the peak lightningLux from 190 to about 60-90 (AtmosphereController.cs:121). Make the sky carry the flash: the flash light has interactsWithSky = false (AtmosphereController.cs:400), so turn it on for the strike, or pulse the volumetric clouds' ambient or the sky exposure with _lightningLevel so the cloud deck glows. Kill the glitter: give the wet clones geometric specular AA, or drop the slab gloss while the flash is on (WetnessDriver slabSmoothnessMax 0.94, slabWetNormal 0.4).
