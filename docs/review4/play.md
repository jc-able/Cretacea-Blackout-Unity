# Review 4 (Nov 20, final state) — segment `play` (first critic 7/10, second critic 7/10)

Can a player finish the game, and does the story make sense?

## Verdict

Yes, a player can finish the game, and the story holds together. The bot played from the first gameplay room to the end screen on foot, with real input and live dinosaurs, in 507 s with 0 deaths. Every objective names the place and the action, and the chain is causal and well foreshadowed: memo, failed switchover, pens fail open, main gate sealed, staff exit collapses, 1996 manual, Substation 3, E-0, dawn.

It is not yet AAA-tight. The first lethal encounter (the Feed Prep raptor plus the slow dock crawl) comes a chapter before the game teaches crouch and light-off. A few voiced lines contradict the clock or the picture ('You're early', 'by the stream', a ranger log stamped 00:04), and 'the bent north leaf' asks for a compass the player doesn't have. The stalled New Game run is the bot's fault, not the game's, but the hand-off names the wrong cause, and no single run has yet gone from New Game to the end screen.

Review 3 had no route/story segment, so no majors were filed under it. Of the story beats it flagged in other segments: the T. rex reveal with no T. rex (night_a major) is fixed (frame 18); the thunder caption over the flashlight is fixed; the opening view that shows mostly ceiling is only partly fixed (New Game now looks down at the desk, but a Night-checkpoint start, as in 01, still looks up).

## Strengths

- The game can be finished on foot. final_playthrough_log.txt goes from the first gameplay room to the end screen in 507 s with 0 deaths and dinosaurs active; 29 shows 08:27 / 0 deaths. Main Menu, Continue and Restart Checkpoint all worked afterwards. Round 5 fixed the real blockers: the Control Room door, one-shot volumes spent by a refused beat, the early Yard checkpoint, chapter 1 beats replaying, and the missing objective after E-0 (27 now shows 'Follow the coast road east, away from the park').
- Who and why are clear within the first minute: J. Vance, night caretaker, clocks in; Dana (Ops, the D. Okafor of the memo) sends him on rounds. Every later step has a stated reason: the main gate is sealed fail-secure, the staff exit collapses (08 shows the EXIT sign behind the rubble), and the 1996 manual (11: a legible scanned page plus a transcript) says E-0 needs Auxiliary Circuit B from Substation 3. The keeper's note foreshadows raptors testing the latch; Halvorsen's log explains the E-0 hold.
- Objectives lead with landmarks and the action, show key caps inline ([C] in 16), and give the long post-manual route objective one leg at a time by player position (13, 17, 22).
- The big beats are signposted. Green bollards make the chase route readable (22). E-0 reads as closed: red signal heads under the green SERVICE EXIT sign (25). The T. rex reveal now shows the animal as a silhouette crossing the road on the strike, with the chevrons lit (18).
- The fairness systems are in place: checkpoints at each chapter; E-0 hold progress kept while you hide; the T. rex kept out of the substation until the lever is thrown; a rubber-banded chase with stamina drain at 0.15; feedback on generator pulls ('It coughs and dies...', 'Come on - catch!'); the end screen counts optional documents.
