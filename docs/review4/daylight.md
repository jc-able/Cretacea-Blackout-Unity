# Review 4 (Nov 20, final state) — segment `daylight` (first critic 7/10, second critic 7/10)

Prologue daylight, dusk and dawn (free camera, no HUD)

## Verdict

Daylight colour is finally natural, and the prologue reads as a lush green island: blue sky, green jungle, neutral shade beside warm-lit paving, and no sepia anywhere. The dusk plaza now has the cool shade, amber hill and storm deck the bible asks for. Of review 3's three majors, the 'dying' valley woodland (GoldenDay_valley_air) and the crop-like tall grass (GoldenDay_valley_eye) are mostly fixed, but the impostor hill mosaic is only partly fixed and is still the most visible flaw at dusk. Water is now the weakest element (the khaki ending sea is unchanged, the day sea is slate, the coast has no beach, the stream looks like a ditch and the dawn pond is the colour of the lawn), and the park's main building still has no silhouette, so the segment is good but not yet amazing.

## Strengths

- Natural day colour. Sky hue is 200-210 deg and the hill 98-113 deg. Sunlit paving is warm (0.528/0.434/0.311) against neutral shade (0.133/0.132/0.131). No frame has a sepia cast (plaza frame mean hue 70 deg, saturation 0.11). The eye-level day frames run from about 0.04-0.07 in their darkest 12 % to a 99th percentile of 0.74-0.78, with nothing clipped or crushed.
- DuskTransition_plaza_eye is now a strong frame. The floor is cool blue-grey (0.193/0.199/0.214, B/R 1.11; review 3 was warm brown at B/R 0.67), the hill catches the last amber light, and a dark storm deck hangs over a bright sunset gap: the bible's storm front.
- The dusk cloud in DuskTransition_valley_eye keeps its structure: clipping in the cloud box fell from 40.6 % to 13.1 %, and in the whole frame from 2.0 % to 0.8 %.
- From the air the valley woodland reads as forest (crown G/R 1.12 -> 1.21, hue 75.5 -> 80 deg, shade pools under the far crowns). The meadow pond is free of trunks and shrubs.
- The tall grass reads as wild grass with pale seed tips. At dawn the backlit blades and the low mist glowing into the sun are beautiful (DawnCoast_valley_eye).
- The valley bluff is no longer desert tan. The far tree line has no horizontal strips, and the 'pagoda' plates at the edges of the valley frames are gone. The plaza palm crown now shows green fronds.
- The dressing is believable. The plaza has bolted lamp posts, kiosks, banners, bins, benches and kerbed planters. The yard has containers, pallets, crates, vehicles and road markings. Everything sits on the ground with matching shadows, and nothing floats.
- The skies work. By day the volumetric cumulus has warm tops and grey bases, the dusk storm front is convincing, and at dawn the zenith is blue with gold-lit clouds and a soft sun disc that is not a blown-out blob.
