# Review 3 (Nov 20, after round 3) — segment `ui` (score 8/10)

The UI is now coherent and readable, close to AAA. One warm amber language runs through the HUD, pause, settings, the new controls page, tablet, reader and end screen, and the end screen finally works as a payoff: a light plate, a bright sun glint, the headline as the largest line, the credits moved off the card. Review 2's majors are fixed (the grey-disc sun under the 0.46 plate, the brown monochrome dawn) or nearly fixed (map collisions: only a faded 'AFF CORRIDOR' ghost remains under the YOU marker). It is not yet perfect for three reasons: the BLACKOUT wordmark dissolves into the gold sky at 1.15:1, the most-used keycaps (E, F, C, SHIFT) still use old art with smaller legends, and the menus are still generic flat cards with no 1996 park identity.

Strengths:
- End screen rebuilt into a payoff. A light neutral plate (0.12-0.30, vignette 0.28) keeps the blue-grey zenith (65,80,97), the teal sea and the bright sun path. The headline 'YOU ESCAPED CRETACEA PARK' is now the largest line (54 vs 42 ref px). Credits sit behind a CREDITS button, and the card sits on the sea well clear of the sun. Review 2's grey-disc sun and brown dawn are gone.
- New CONTROLS page, reachable from pause and settings. Its keyboard and Xbox columns match the bindings in CretaceaInput exactly, and it uses the same card, footer and accent button as every other menu.
- Tablet spoilers fixed: unfound documents read 'UNKNOWN DOCUMENT / Not yet found', the MASTER KEY row is hidden, and SYSTEMS is locked and dimmed instead of showing an empty page. The tab row no longer twitches: edges sit at x=573/748/756 whichever tab is selected.
- Map: the target label RECORDS ANNEX is now cream bold on amber at 6.5:1 (was 3.3:1). The beacon sits in the room's lower right, clear of its label. STORAGE and VET fit their rooms, STAFF EXIT is clean, and the YOU dot has a dark outline.
- Objective copy now leads with what the player can see: '...at the far end of the Staff Corridor past the Control Room (east)'. The corridor frame shows the CONTROL ROOM sign, so the words and the world agree.
- Subtitles now break into two even lines with no orphan word, keep the amber speaker tag and stay readable over the lit floor. The objective banner holds over the bright office ceiling (02) and the dark control room (06).
- The flashlight chip names the action ('F FLASHLIGHT') and lets the lamp carry the state, so it teaches the key on pickup.
- Consistency fixes landed: the pause chapter line uses the tablet's amber Barlow Condensed, the footer reads 'TAB / ESC', settings has an ESC BACK footer, the bipolar brightness slider reads '+0' with no misleading fill, and the TAB/ESC legends are larger. The transcript's hanging indent is aligned (wrapped lines at x≈724, under the item text).

## Findings

- **[major] 29_end_screen.png** (ui): Top centre: the wordmark sits on the brightest band of the dawn sky with no backing of its own. 'BLACKOUT' (amber Stardos Stencil) measures (245,176,71) against sky of about (193,174,147), a luminance contrast of 1.15:1. It reads by hue alone and nearly vanishes in a greyscale check, so it fails for colour-blind players and on desaturated TVs. 'CRETACEA' (cream) holds only because of its down-right drop shadow. Every other text element on this screen measures 9-13:1. The game's name washes out on its payoff frame.
  - Likely cause: MenuController.BuildEndingOverlay places EndTitle/EndSub 236-392 ref px from the top, where EndingPlateSprite is at its lightest (alpha 0.12-0.22). That is right for the sky, but the text gets nothing behind it. The text uses the font's Shadow preset (StardosStencil-Bold SDF Shadow.mat: underlay offset 0.35/-0.55, dilate 0.25, softness 0.45, alpha 0.72). That preset is a directional drop shadow, not a halo, so the letter faces sit directly on the bright sky.
  - Suggested fix: Give the badge and wordmark block its own soft dark pool: reuse EndingPoolSprite at about 0.35-0.4 alpha, about 760x300 ref px, centred on the wordmark. Add a dedicated end-title material with a centred underlay (offset 0,0, dilate about 0.45, softness about 0.8, black at 0.55). Target at least 3:1 luminance contrast for BLACKOUT. Alternatively set BLACKOUT in cream and keep amber for a thin rule under it. Leave the rest of the sky plate as light as it is.
- **[minor] controls.png** (ui): Keyboard column. The SHIFT legend is 7 px tall at 720p, against 11-12 px for WASD, MOUSE, CTRL, TAB, ESC, SPACE and ENTER, so the sprint key looks like a placeholder. E, F and C (interact, flashlight, crouch: the most-used keys) have 9 px legends, smaller than TAB/ESC. Two keycap styles also sit side by side. The atlas caps (C, E, SHIFT, LB, L3) have a bevelled base and no outline; the code-drawn text caps (CTRL, WASD, MOUSE, L/R STICK) are flat with a light outline. The 'C / CTRL' row shows both at once. The hand-in's claim of 'one 40 px legend for every key' does not hold for these four keys.
  - Likely cause: docs/handin/ui_tools/make_key_atlas.py copies e, f, c and shift from Assets/Art/UI/HUD/key_*.png, the old art with smaller legends. Shift is a 128x64 sprite whose small legend shrinks again when MenuController.PlaceKeys fits it to capH 38. Only the other keys are drawn with cap(px=40). The text caps come from a different drawing path, the CretaceaUiFactory.Keycap text fallback.
  - Suggested fix: Draw e, f, c and shift with cap() at the same 40 px legend (cap('SHIFT') comes out about 110 px wide) and regenerate key_atlas.png/json. Add WASD, MOUSE, CTRL, L STICK and R STICK to the atlas with cap() as well, or make the text fallback copy the atlas look (base step, no light outline). Then re-check the HUD flashlight chip (F) and the reader's CONTINUE button (E).
- **[minor] controls.png** (ui): The alternating row bands render as solid tan-grey (56,51,43) on the (13,13,13) card, about four times stronger than designed, so the page reads like a spreadsheet. Keycaps on the banded rows (face 63,68,73) nearly merge into the band and are held apart only by their 1-2 px border: SHIFT, E, SPACE, LB/L3, X.
  - Likely cause: MenuController.BuildControlsModal uses band Color(1, 0.92, 0.78, 0.035). The canvas composites in linear space: 3.5% over 13/255 blended in linear gives exactly 56,51,43, while an sRGB blend would give about 21,20,19. Every translucent UI fill tuned by eye as sRGB comes out about 3-4x heavier.
  - Suggested fix: Lower the band alpha to about 0.010-0.012, use an opaque band of about (0.075, 0.068, 0.058), or replace the bands with 1 px BorderSubtle rules between rows. Keep keycaps clearly brighter than anything they sit on.
- **[minor] hud_corridor.png** (ui): Bottom-left chip (same in 02_ch1_flashlight_in_hand.png): the 'beam on' lamp looks like an off-centre eclipse, not a lamp with a glow. The 8 px amber dot is pushed against the right edge of its 16 px halo (centres at x≈162.5 vs 157.5). The halo is a hard-edged flat disc (R=120 at every pixel inside), so the whole thing reads like a toggle switch or a moon phase.
  - Likely cause: HudController.BuildBottomLeftStatusStrip creates Glow (24 ref px) and Dot (12 ref px) with CreateAnchoredPanel(anchor (1,0.5), pivot (1,0.5), position (-16,0)), so both are right-aligned on the same edge. Glow uses the solid GetCircleSprite at alpha 0.30.
  - Suggested fix: Use pivot (0.5,0.5) and anchoredPosition (-28,0) for both so they share a centre. Draw the glow with a radial falloff sprite (alpha 1 at the centre to 0 at the rim) at about 0.35, so it reads as light, not as a second disc.
- **[minor] document_reader.png** (ui): Transcript column (same in 11_ch2_manual_open.png). The hanging indent is fixed, but the numbered items still have no space between them. Items 7.1-7.4 start 20-21 px apart, against 18-19 px between wrapped lines, about 2 px extra. The transcript stays denser than the paper beside it, which spaces its items clearly.
  - Likely cause: MenuController.cs line 420: _docTranscriptText.paragraphSpacing = 12f. TMP paragraph spacing is in hundredths of an em, so 12 is 0.12 em: about 2.8 ref px at the 23 px size, about 1.8 px at 720p.
  - Suggested fix: Set paragraphSpacing to about 50-60 (0.5-0.6 em, about 8 px at 720p). Check it against the longest page so auto-size does not shrink the text to make it fit.
- **[minor] journal_tab0.png** (ui): Map, west end of the corridor (same in journal_tab1.png). The YOU dot and its view cone still sit on the first letters of STAFF CORRIDOR. The near-player fade now works, but the faded label still reads as a ghost 'AFF CORRIDOR' right beside the marker, and the YOU tag covers the corridor's south wall line. Review 2's other map collisions are fixed, so this item is only partly fixed.
  - Likely cause: ParkMapView keeps the corridor label at a fixed point at the corridor's west end. The round-3 fix fades the label within 30 px of the player instead of moving it.
  - Suggested fix: Put the corridor label in the half of the corridor away from the player (or at its midpoint between the Control Room and Records Annex doors), or hide it completely while it is overlapped. Place the YOU tag where it does not cover walls, for example above the dot when the dot is next to a south wall.
- **[minor] journal_tab2.png** (ui): Documents tab. The spoilers are gone, but known-but-unfound entries use the status 'NOT FOUND' as their title, so the tablet never names the evacuation manual the objective sends the player to find. Its hint still steers by compass ('Records Annex, east end of the Staff Corridor'), while the objective now says '...at the far end of the Staff Corridor past the Control Room (east)'. The locked tab reads 'SYSTEMS · LOCKED' with no hint of how to unlock it. journal_tab1 shows the map again because the tab is locked, so the unlocked Systems page has still not been seen in any capture.
  - Likely cause: JournalController.cs around line 1013: known ? "NOT FOUND" : "UNKNOWN DOCUMENT". The ArchiveDocs location strings around line 108 were not updated to match ObjectiveGuide.
  - Suggested fix: For known entries, show the title with a muted status tag ('1996 EVACUATION MANUAL', then 'NOT FOUND' in small caps). Rewrite the location strings landmark-first, matching ObjectiveGuide. Give the locked tab a one-line reason on focus or in the footer, for example 'Read the evacuation manual to load the schematic'.
- **[minor] pause.png** (ui): Pause, settings, controls and the tablet are still the same flat near-black cards with hairline borders. They are clean but generic, closer to a modern app than a 1996 park-security kit. The park badge and the stencil face appear only on the end screen. The hand-in lists this item as not done.
  - Likely cause: Round-3 item 10 (badge in the headers, panel grain) was left out for time.
  - Suggested fix: Carry the park identity into the menus: the badge or stencil wordmark in the pause, controls and tablet headers, the stencil face for chapter titles, faint paper or CRT grain in the panels, and a rugged device bezel around the field tablet.
