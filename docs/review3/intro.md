# Review 3 (Nov 20, after round 3) — segment `intro` (score 7/10)

Much closer, but not amazing yet. The opening now does its job: exposure is natural and controlled from day to night, with no white-outs and no black frames at the cuts. The herbivores and a sunlit T. rex are actually shown, the park has a real crowd and the title cards are clean. Review 2's blocker (the white mirror on the night plaza, intro_16) is fixed, and so are the crowd, sauropod, night-exterior, title-card and kiosk majors. Still visible to any viewer: the rail trikes merge into one two-headed animal (09); the roar puts a mullion through the rex's face (13, partly fixed); the shot-3 herd stands unlit and cropped in the closing aerial (14-16; the aerial's dressing is partly fixed); and the first gameplay frame is a flat beige office built around cube-and-stick desk props (19-20, still there).

Strengths:
- Exposure across the day-dusk-night arc is controlled. The mean frame brightness steps down 81-88, then 76, 57, 43, 35 (/255). Outdoors almost nothing clips (at most 0.11 % of pixels, in intro_07). The darkest 1 % never drops below about 10/255, and no cut lands on a black or white frame. The only clipped pixels are the office's troffer lenses (19-20).
- The herbivores are finally on screen. The title glide (01-03) puts a front-lit brachiosaurus and grazing trikes in the middle of the frame with the Visitor Center behind. In 09 a large brachiosaurus walks in profile, its neck breaking the skyline against the cumulus (review 2 major: fixed).
- The T. rex is now a real money shot. It arrives large and sunlit in the middle pane (12) and roars with its jaw open, with visitors' heads framing the bottom corners (13).
- The park reads as open (review 2 major: fixed). intro_05 and intro_07 show a queue at the rails, a guide's tour group, families and strollers in recoloured clothes. About a dozen visitors stand in groups at the gallery glass (11).
- Every title card now uses one lower-left plate with an amber edge bar: legible, the same style everywhere, never over the subject (01, 14, 17, 19). 'CLOSING TIME' is a proper heading (review 2 major: fixed).
- The night exterior (17) is now a believable building (review 2 major: fixed). It has three warm wall lamps with pools on the wall, a door, an AC unit, a downpipe, conduit, an electrical box, a wet apron with a puddle, a barbed-wire fence and a red roof beacon, under a blue-grey sky (R/B 0.72) with a clear roofline.
- Review 2's blocker is fixed: the night aerial (16) has no white mirror rectangle. The plaza paving tops out around 104/255 (99th percentile) with no clipped pixels.
- The jungle above the plaza is green and lush. At dusk, amber last light catches the ridge with blue haze between the trunks (14-15).
- The kiosk back seen from shot 2 is dressed: a door with a handle, a louvred vent, a poster and a double-sided sign (07; review 2 major: fixed).
- Day colour is natural: blue sky, white cumulus, saturated greens, neutral paving (R/B 1.04-1.07) and cool shade. The gallery balances a dim interior against a bright exterior with nothing blown (11).
