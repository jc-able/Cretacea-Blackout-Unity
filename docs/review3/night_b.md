## Findings

- **[major] 25_ch6_e0_gate_closed.png** (materials): The closed climax gate does not read as closed. Inside the portal, the left ~60 % of the opening (x 380-760) shows the coast road, a lamp and the jungle perfectly sharp, with no wire at all. A hard vertical line at x~760 separates that from the nearer leaf, which renders as a halftone grid of dots, not diamond mesh (x 760-940, y 95-470). Fine detail in the green channel measures 2-3.7 left of the line and 5-8 right of it. The leaves show no stiles, brace or motor; only one rail crosses the opening at y~320. In 26 the only change is that the dot panel and the rail vanish, so the gate-opens payoff barely registers. Review 2 status: the frosted-glass veil is gone everywhere (fences in 19-21, the far E-0 in 24, the coast road in 27), but E-0 is now over-corrected.
  - Likely cause: Zone23Dressing.ChainlinkSeeThrough() multiplies the wire alpha by 0.52 and swaps in ChainlinkFadeTexture (mip 3 alpha x0.55, mip 4 and smaller capped at 0.12). That sits on top of MAT_ChainlinkFence's alpha cutoff of 0.27 (WorldMaterialPalette.cs, BuildChainlinkMaterial). From mip 4 the effective alpha is at most 0.06, so the wires vanish all at once at a mip boundary; the oblique far leaf, 8-12 m away, reaches mip 4 at 720p. At mips 2-3 only the wire crossings pass the cutoff, which is why the mesh shows as dots. The E-0 LeafA/LeafB go through ThinChainlinkBox (Zone3_SubstationAndYardBuilder.cs lines 45-51), and BuildFramedDoor gives them no frame.
  - Suggested fix: Take E-0 off the see-through material and build a real gate leaf: a 60 mm galvanised tube frame (stiles, top and bottom rails, a diagonal brace) with modelled palisade or welded-mesh infill. For example, 20 mm bars every 12-15 cm, or 50 mm weld mesh at full opacity with coverage-preserving mips. Add a motor housing on the hinge post and a beacon that switches from red to green as the gate opens. Keep the vanishing material for long perimeter spans only. Re-capture 25 at 720p and at 1080p: the whole opening must read as barred, and the change from 25 to 26 must read as the gate swinging open.
- **[major] 25_ch6_e0_gate_closed.png** (materials): The yellow-and-black hazard stripes painted across the gate threshold are printed onto the player's glove and flashlight (bottom right, x 800-950, y 540-650): the stripes run straight across the hand as if it were part of the floor. In 26 the same stripes show faintly on the shadowed glove. This is the hand the player looks at all game, and it happens at the climax gate.
  - Likely cause: ViewmodelCustomPass (Assets/Scripts/Rendering/ViewmodelCustomPass.cs) clears depth and draws the viewmodel's Forward pass before post-processing. By then HDRP has already built the decal buffer (DBuffer) from the world's depth. The viewmodel materials still receive decals (_SupportDecals: 1 in M_FPS_GlovedArm.mat, M_FPS_JacketSleeveExt.mat and every M_Flashlight_*.mat), so they sample the decal buffer of whatever floor lies behind them on screen. Any floor decal behind the hand can do this: Hazard_E0Threshold (Zone3_SubstationAndYardBuilder.cs line 1032), stencils, grime, oil and puddles (about 500 along the route).
  - Suggested fix: Turn decals off on every viewmodel material (set _SupportDecals to 0, then call HDMaterial.ValidateMaterial so the _DISABLE_DECALS keyword is set). Have the Look Pass enforce this for every renderer on layer 12, so a new viewmodel material cannot bring it back. Re-capture 25, plus a frame with the hand over a puddle decal.

  - Likely cause: The island has a single broadleaf canopy model: LookVegetation.TreeStems is jungle_tree, palm_tree, palm_tree_b and tree_fern, and Assets/Art/Foliage holds no other tree. The yard tree belt (Zone3_SubstationAndYardBuilder.cs around line 1170) places jungle_tree at scale 14 + 3*hh (14-17 m), at a near-constant setback of 7.5 m + 3*hh from the wall, so the trunks line up at one height.

Break the trunk band with palms, tree ferns and scaled-up shrub_01-04 so no two pale trunks stand side by side, and darken the trunks with distance. For this shot, add a low band of local volumetric fog in the valley and a blue-grey dawn haze over the far ridge. Dress the warehouse roof (ridge vents, skylights, gutters, downpipes, rust streaks) and break up the wall with buttresses, stains and signage, or raise the crane so the jungle and the sea dominate the frame.
