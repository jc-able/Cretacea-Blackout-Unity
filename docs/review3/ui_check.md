# Review 3 second opinion (Nov 20) — segment `ui` (first critic 8/10, second critic 8/10)

HUD, menus, journal, document reader, end screen

The second critic looked at the same captures, tried to refute each finding of the first critic, corrected severities and added what was missed.

## Confirmed (8)

- **[major] ~/CretaceaUnityClones/vfx/Screenshots/review3/route/29_end_screen.png** (ui (end-screen wordmark)): Top centre: now that the plate is light, the wordmark has no backing of its own. 'BLACKOUT' (amber Stardos Stencil) sits on the brightest band of the dawn sky and reads by hue alone. It disappears in a greyscale or colour-blind check. 'CRETACEA' (cream) holds only because of its drop shadow. The game's name washes out on the payoff frame. Cause: MenuController.BuildEndingOverlay puts EndTitle and EndSub straight on the scene. EndingPlateSprite is only about 0.14 alpha at the BLACKOUT line (the ramp runs 0.12 to 0.22 above v=0.55), and the vignette is 0.28.
  - Why it is real: I sampled it myself. The BLACKOUT glyphs average (238,173,76) against the sky around them at (203,183,152), about 1.0:1 in luminance (0.486 vs 0.488). The first critic's 1.15:1 is generous. CRETACEA's glyphs (237,230,214) measure 1.6:1 against the same sky. At 3x the stencil letters dissolve into the cloud band. This is a regression from the round-3 plate fix: in review 2's capture the 0.46 plate gave the same word a dark-brown ground. Review 2's majors for this frame: (1) the flat grey sun disc under a flat 0.46 plate is FIXED. The plate is now a light 0.12-0.30 gradient, the sun is a bright soft glow behind cloud with its glint on the sea, and the card sits low on the sea, clear of the sun. (2) The brown monochrome dusk is FIXED: blue-grey upper sky, gold-lit clouds, green-teal sea. The headline is now the largest line on the card and the credits have their own card.
  - Fix: Give the badge and wordmark their own soft dark pool: reuse EndingPoolSprite, black at about 0.35-0.45 alpha, about 800x330 ref px, centred on the wordmark. And/or give EndTitle and EndSub a TMP underlay material (centred, dilate about 0.4, softness about 0.7, black at about 0.6). Target at least 3:1 luminance contrast for BLACKOUT. Alternatively set BLACKOUT in cream and keep amber for a thin rule under it. Do not bring back a full-frame dim.
- **[minor] ~/CretaceaUnityClones/vfx/Screenshots/review3/ui/controls.png** (ui (keycaps)): Keyboard column: the SHIFT legend is 7 px tall at 720p. The legends of E, F and C (interact, flashlight, crouch: the most-used keys) are 9 px, against 11-12 px for WASD, MOUSE, CTRL, TAB, ESC, SPACE and ENTER. Two keycap styles sit side by side, both in the 'C / CTRL' row: the project-art caps have a bevelled base and no outline, the code-drawn text caps (WASD, MOUSE, CTRL, L/R STICK) are flat with a light outline. The hand-in's 'one 40 px legend for every key' does not hold for e, f, c and shift.
  - Why it is real: Measured with a brightness threshold: SHIFT 7 px; C, E and F 9 px; CTRL, WASD, MOUSE and L STICK 11 px; TAB, ESC, SPACE, ENTER and LB 12 px. At 3x the SHIFT legend is visibly about half size. make_key_atlas.py line 58 loads e, f, c and shift from the old key art. The same art gives the HUD chip an F legend of 7 px (hud_corridor.png and 02), the smallest legend in the game on its most-used chip, and the reader's CONTINUE an E of 9 px. Footer caps measure 8-9 px for ESC (pause, settings, controls) and 10 px for TAB and ESC (tablet): review 2's '6-7 px TAB/ESC glyphs' is only partly fixed.
  - Fix: In docs/handin/ui_tools/make_key_atlas.py, draw e, f, c and shift with cap() at the 40 px legend (not from Assets/Art/UI/HUD/key_*.png) and regenerate key_atlas.png and key_atlas.json. Add WASD, MOUSE, CTRL, L STICK and R STICK to the atlas with cap(), or make the text-cap fallback copy the atlas look (base step, no light outline). Draw footer keycaps large enough that every legend is at least 10 px at 720p. Re-check the HUD flashlight chip (F) and the reader's CONTINUE (E).
- **[minor] ~/CretaceaUnityClones/vfx/Screenshots/review3/ui/controls.png** (ui (controls table)): The alternating row bands render as solid tan-grey (56,51,43) on the (13,13,13) card. After the text they are the strongest tone on the page, so the page reads like a spreadsheet. Keycap faces (63,68,73) on banded rows nearly merge with the band and are held apart only by their 1-2 px rim (SHIFT, E, SPACE, LB/L3, X).
  - Why it is real: Sampled the bands at (56,51,43) and the card at (13,13,13). The code asks for (1, 0.92, 0.78) at alpha 0.035, which an sRGB blend would put at about (21,21,20). The UI blends in linear, so the band lands about 5x brighter than designed. Cap faces on the band measure about 1.2:1.
  - Fix: Lower the band alpha in MenuController.BuildControlsModal to about 0.008-0.012 (allowing for the linear blend), or replace the bands with 1 px BorderSubtle rules between rows. Keep keycaps clearly brighter than anything they sit on.

## Missed by the first critic (3)

- **[minor] ~/CretaceaUnityClones/vfx/Screenshots/review3/ui/journal_tab0.png** (ui (tablet map)): Map, right of the Feed Prep block (same in journal_tab1.png): 'LOADING', the top line of LOADING DOCK, touches the room's top wall.
