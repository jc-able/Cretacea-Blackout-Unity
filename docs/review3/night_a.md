# Review 3 (Nov 20, after round 3) — segment `night_a` (score 6/10)

Not amazing yet, but closer. The architecture half now looks like a shipped game: the service path (14, 16) is dressed, wet and lit by real fixtures, the calm plaza is still the hero frame, and lightning now lights the sky and turns the jungle into silhouettes. Tyrant Valley, the game's signature space, is almost unchanged from review 2: between strikes it sits in a murky 6-90/255 band, the gravel road looks dry, the T. rex reveal shows no T. rex, and lone lollipop trees are the focal points. The loading dock has sunk into near-black, and the 'blackout' plaza capture still shows the power on. Review 2's majors: storm-valley tonal range, dry road, missing T. rex and the blackout-plaza capture are still there; the bare service-path slabs are fixed in 14 and 16 and partly fixed in 15; the cloned valley tree is partly fixed.

Strengths:
- Service path start (14) and gate (16) now read as a real park service route: PADDOCK 4 SERVICE GATE sign, conduit with junction boxes, a warm bulkhead with its fixture, a guard booth, jungle hanging over the coping, a soaked lane that mirrors the lamps, and a puddle with rain rings (16).
- The gate in 16 reads as perforated metal, not frosted glass, with legible DANGER / PADDOCK 4 plates. Review 2's amber orb is gone: the beacon now has a visible fixture and a small glow.
- Lightning works as intended: in valley_strike the cloud deck carries the flash, every tree cuts out as a textured silhouette, grass and road stay readable, and nothing clips except the bolt (p99 145).
- No frame is crushed: the black floor is 6-7/255 everywhere, 0 % of pixels are under 3/255, and there is no banding and no teal or red wash; the night palette stays blue-grey with warm practicals.
- NightCalm_plaza_eye is still the hero frame: a sodium cobra-head pool, lit shopfronts and sign, palms, the hill silhouette and wet reflections in the mid-ground.
- Rain reads as rain in every Play-mode frame (slanted streaks against the sky and in the beam), and none falls under the dock canopy.
- The valley treeline is denser and more varied than in review 2 (leaning trees, palms, banana and fern understorey, vines), and the tall grass along the road is excellent.
- The overturned jeep now sits on the shoulder with a contact shadow, and its amber interior glow and red jerry cans give the valley entry its one warm accent.
- The storm sky has lost its lone lit wisp and gained a slightly brighter horizon band, so the treelines stand out as silhouettes (trunks 15/255 against 30 for the low sky).
- One HUD style across the segment: objective panel, captions and the flashlight chip.

In Play mode with the flashlight on, 18 measures p1 6, median 28, p99 72 and p99.9 91 outside the HUD; only 0.02 % of pixels exceed 100/255. The darks did deepen (canopy trunks 15/255, was 23; target 12 or less) and the treeline now separates from the low sky (30), but there is still no glint, no lamp and no lit sky anywhere in the valley. It reads as murk, not a storm. Only valley_strike has a full range, and only for the length of a flash.
  - Likely cause: LookAtmosphereProfiles.cs lines 307-308: storm.indirectDiffuse 1.7 and contrast 9 (review 2 asked for about 1.3 and 12-16) darkened everything evenly. storm.fogColor (0.34,0.42,0.56)*3.0 at a 110 m mean free path is unchanged (lines 298-300), so the mid-ground still lifts to the sky's level. No light source is in view in the valley between strikes, and gravel cannot glint (TerrainWetnessDriver.ProfileFor Gravel: smoothness max 0.42).
  - Suggested fix: Add highlights instead of darkening further. (1) Put one permanent lamp in the valley's view, e.g. a red aviation beacon or a warm 3000 K lantern on a mast at Substation 3 at the far end of Service Road 3 (lens 14-22 nits, volumetric 0.4). The player gets a goal and the frame gets a highlight. (2) Make the road wet so it catches the moon (azimuth 115, ahead-right of the eastbound route); see the road finding. (3) Make the cloud deck faintly bright low toward the moon (about 45-60/255 behind the treeline), using the cloud lighting rather than the fog constant, then trim fogColor x3.0 to about x2.3 so trunks and grass separate from the haze. Target: darks under the canopy at 12/255 or less, and something at 120/255 or more in every valley frame without lightning.
