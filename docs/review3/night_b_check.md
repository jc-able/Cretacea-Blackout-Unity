## Confirmed (8)

- **[major] 25_ch6_e0_gate_closed.png** (materials / E-0 gate): The closed climax gate does not read as closed. In the left ~60 % of the opening (x 380-745) the coast road, the lamp and the jungle are sharp, with no wire at all. Right of a hard, near-vertical line at x~745-760, the nearer part of the leaf shows as a regular halftone dot grid (x 760-940, y 95-470), not as diamond mesh. One rail at y~320 is the only solid member: there are no stiles, brace, bars or motor. From the yard (24, yard_before_strike, yard_strike) the leaves vanish completely, so the goal reads as an open, lit doorway. In 26 the dots and the mid rail disappear, and only a thin bar or two of the swung leaves shows at top left, so the gate-opening payoff barely registers.
  - Why it is real: The 2x crop of 25 shows a dot grid on the right and nothing on the left, with the boundary at x~745-760. The 3x crop of the far gate in 24 shows a pale, empty rectangle. The cause is by design in WorldMaterialPalette.BuildChainlinkMaterial: alpha clip at _AlphaCutoff 0.27, commented 'just above the texture's mean alpha: far mips fall under it and vanish', with PlainMips (mipMapsPreserveCoverage false, bias -1). The E-0 LeafA/LeafB get this material through ThinChainlinkBox (Zone3_SubstationAndYardBuilder). At 5-12 m the leaf straddles a mip switch: the nearer mip keeps only the wire crossings (the dots), the next mip falls under the cutoff (empty), and the switch is the hard line. Review 2 status: the frosted veil is gone everywhere, but the gate is still not believable, now with a different symptom.
  - Fix: Keep the first critic's fix: a real gate leaf with a 50-60 mm tube frame, stiles, top and bottom rails, a diagonal brace and opaque palisade or weld-mesh infill, a motor housing on the hinge post, and a beacon that turns from red to green. Give the E-0 leaves their own mesh material (coverage-preserving mips, cutoff ~0.5) instead of the vanishing perimeter material. Commit 3fb4102 (12:18, after these 11:32 captures) adds 3 cm bars every 25 cm plus stiles and a brace. Check them from the yard: at ~40 m a 3 cm bar is under a pixel at 720p and will shimmer, so use 5-6 cm bars or a solid lower kick plate. Re-capture 24, 25 and 26; the change from 25 to 26 must read as the leaves swinging out.
- **[major] 25_ch6_e0_gate_closed.png** (viewmodel / decals): The yellow-and-black hazard stripes on the E-0 threshold are printed onto the player's glove and torch (x 790-950, y 540-650). They continue the floor pattern in perspective and end at a hard vertical edge at x~948. They show again, faintly, on the shadowed glove in 26.
  - Why it is real: The 3x crop shows bold yellow bands across the black glove and the torch body, with a straight cut-off at the decal box edge. Because the stripes continue the floor's pattern exactly, the hand is reading the floor's decal buffer. So any floor decal under the hand (puddles, grime, stencils) will print the same way anywhere in the game, not only at E-0. It happens at the climax, on the hand the player watches all game.
  - Fix: As the critic says: turn Receive Decals off (_SupportDecals 0, then HDMaterial.ValidateMaterial) on every renderer on viewmodel layer 12, and have the Look Pass enforce it by layer, not by folder. Commit 3fb4102 only covers Assets/Art/Materials/Props/Flashlight, and this is not verified. Re-capture 25 and 26, plus a frame with the hand over a puddle decal and the E-0 stencil.

  - Fix: As the critic says: vary the tree scale (about 9-22 m), height, crown, tilt and setback. Break the trunk line with palms, tree ferns and big shrubs, and darken the trunks with distance. For this shot, add local volumetric fog in the valley and a blue-grey haze on the far ridge: the dawn look's fogMeanFreePath 2600 m and fogMaxHeight 24 m (LookAtmosphereProfiles) now leave no aerial perspective. Dress the warehouse roof and the wall, or raise the crane so the jungle and the sea dominate.
