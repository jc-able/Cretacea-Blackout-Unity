- **[major] GoldenDay_valley_eye.png** (vegetation): New in round 3: the impostors' horizontal crown caps show edge-on from eye level as flat plates. On the far slope at the right edge (x 1150-1280, y 265-300), the trees look like stacked flat shelves. On the hill in GoldenDay_meadow_eye and DuskTransition_meadow_eye, the caps make a terraced, blocky mosaic of flat ledges over dark vertical trunk stripes: obvious at 2x, a horizontal striation at 1x.
  - Likely cause: LookVegetationImpostors.SaveMesh adds a horizontal cap quad at capY (the mean height of the crown's top surface) across the full crown bounds. It is drawn from every angle with sky-facing normals and a flat lit shade (Lerp 0.85-0.70). Nothing fades it when the camera is level with it, so its rim pokes out of the crossed cards as a hard horizontal line.
  - Suggested fix: Show the cap only in views from above. Option 1: an impostor Shader Graph that dither-fades the cap below about 20° of view elevation (and the crossed cards above about 60°). Option 2, with no new shader: inset the cap to about 70 % of the crown footprint and lower it to about 65 % of the crown height, so the leafy crossed cards hide its rim from the side. Long term: hemi-octahedral impostors (8x8 views with albedo, normal and depth). Check: no straight horizontal edges on the ridge in GoldenDay_valley_eye and DawnCoast_valley_eye, and the aerials stay as green as they are now.
