# Hand-in: TERRAIN, VEGETATION, WATER (branch `look/terrain`, base 941668e)

Captures: `Screenshots/handin/` (16 files + `extra/`, see `INDEX.md`). Everything below is generated by
`cretacea build_all_scenes`; no scene was hand-edited.

## What changed and why

### 1. Ground that reads as jungle (goal 1)
- **8 terrain layers** (`LookTerrainLayers.cs`), bound by exact file name: 0 Mud, 1 Grass (tropical green),
  2 Gravel, 3 RockyCliff (dark basalt), 4 JungleFloor (leaf litter), 5 MossyRock, 6 Moss, 7 Sand.
- **New derived texture set** `Assets/Art/Textures/terrain_jungle/` (no downloads; script
  `Assets/Art/Terrain/Source~/derive_terrain_textures.py`): grass_ground straw -> wet tropical green (hue spread and a
  few brown leaves kept), backdrop_jungle_aerial -> moss, rock_cliff -> basalt, mud -> damp volcanic sand. Every
  layer's colour is "flattened" (low-frequency drift removed, wrap-around) so 5-22 m tiling no longer shows as a
  checkerboard from the air. Masks are rebuilt from each set's **dry** `_MaskMap` (G = AO, B = height, A = smoothness)
  at 1024 px; normals are the source sets' own.
- **Splat by rules** (no more `y > 18/22 => rock`, no `x > 546 => rock`): rock only where slope (+ noise) > ~40 deg,
  basalt on the steepest / sea cliffs, mossy rock elsewhere; mud on stream banks, worn ground right next to anything
  zones 1-3 built (physics check against their colliders) and small valley patches; gravel on Service Road 3; sand at
  the sea waterline; mud/sand ring at the lake; macro / mid / fine noise mixes grass, leaf litter and moss.
- `PlayerController.terrainLayerSurfaces` follows the new order: {Mud, Grass, Gravel, Concrete, Grass, Concrete,
  Grass, Gravel} (footsteps: leaf litter/moss = Grass, rock = Concrete, sand = Gravel).
- **Rain-wet ground at runtime**: `Assets/Scripts/Rendering/TerrainWetnessDriver.cs` (on the main terrain) reads
  `_GlobalWetness` and feeds every tile a splat MaterialPropertyBlock (smoothness up, albedo x0.8 at full wetness;
  rock/gravel/sand less). TerrainLayer assets are never modified. Verified in Play (storm): wet sparkle on the
  ground near the substation, toggled on/off.
- Height-based blending stays off: HDRP TerrainLit only supports it with <= 4 layers.

### 2. Shape (goal 2)
- `EvaluateWorldElevation` = the old function **unchanged inside X -125..560, Z -215..195** (renamed
  `EvaluateLegacyElevation`, its own -42..136 clamp kept), blended over 70 m into `LookTerrainShape.OuterElevation`
  outside it: domain-warped ridged + fBm hills that grow with distance from the play area, Mount Tormenta (195 m,
  broken crater, radial gullies) north behind the Visitor Center, a 150 m cone in the south-west, a north-east
  shoulder, the NE/SE headlands continued from the old ones. Measured outside the play area on land: mean slope 24 deg,
  12.7 % above 40 deg (summit/crater flanks).
- **The island ends in coast in every direction**: a warped elliptic coastline (W -720, N +830, S -730) smooth-min'd
  with an east coast that runs under the overlook at x ~628 and bulges out on the headlands; beach profile at the
  waterline, then a shelf and seabed down to ~-98 m. Eight **render-only neighbour tiles** (1024 m each, no
  TerrainCollider -> the NavMesh still covers the main tile only) carry the rest of the island and the seabed;
  neighbours are linked with `SetNeighbors`.
- Main tile height range is now Y -100..280 (was -42..138) so the seabed and the volcano fit; heights inside the play
  area are the same function, re-quantised at 1.2 cm instead of 0.55 cm (not visible; props sit at hard-coded Y).

### 3. Vegetation, much denser and cheaper per object (goal 3)
- `LookVegetationPrefabs.cs` builds 25 **LODGroup prefabs** in `Assets/Prefabs/Foliage/` from the LOD0/_LOD1/_LOD2
  FBX files (pivot at the base, Environment layer, shadows only on near LODs, small plants never cast). Trees get two
  generated far LODs (vertex clustering of LOD2: 108-340 triangles, `Assets/Prefabs/Foliage/Meshes/`), logs get one.
  Plants cull early (ferns ~20 m, shrubs ~40 m, banana ~100 m). GPU instancing comes from the LookMaterials materials;
  the GPU Resident Drawer instances the GameObjects (draw calls stay 340-1400 with ~36,000 plants).
- `LookVegetation.cs` (seeded, deterministic) places
  - play area + 40 m: **2,767 trees** with trunk CapsuleColliders (NavMesh holes come from the bake; **no carving
    NavMeshObstacles**), **9,773 understory** plants (banana, monstera, shrub, pachira, fern, anthurium, calathea,
    tree fern), **~700 rocks / logs / roots / stumps** with box colliders. Density field: clumps and clearings,
    open grassland along Service Road 3 in Tyrant Valley, open Titan Meadows, dense belts right behind walls/fences
    (physics check), lush forest edges.
  - the rest of the island (all 9 tiles): **22,037 hill trees** (palms by the coast, jungle trees on the hills,
    thinning towards the summits; capsule colliders within 160 m of the play area on the main tile).
  - coast: ~390 boulders along the whole waterline, rock shelves (coastal_cliff_01) under the headlands.
  - Titan Meadows lake shore: rocks, logs, palms and tree ferns leaning over the water.
  - Old placement: 165 trees, 225 tufts, 36 boulders -> now ~36,000 placed plants/rocks (~150x the trees).
- Exclusions: real footprints + ~2 m (`LookVegetation.Footprints`: plaza, VC wings, raptor pen sightline, service
  paths, paddock gate, jeep, substation compound, old yard, coast road) plus physics checks against every zone 1-4
  collider (trunk + 2 m; crowns of big trees must not cut into tall buildings), Service Road 3 (5.5 m + trunk pad),
  the stream channel, slope limits, and the intro/ending camera lines (StoryDirector glide shots, ending corridor).
  The coast road is now built before the jungle so plants avoid it too.
- **Ground cover** (`LookVegetationDetails.cs`): instanced terrain details on the main tile, meshes made of 3-6
  crossed cards cut from the grass_bermuda / fern_02 atlases (6-12 triangles each): short tufts over grass, **tall
  grass in the 7 TallGrassZoneVolume patches** (triggers kept, see capture 14), reeds at the lake, fern fans on leaf
  litter; 831k instances, drawn to 85 m; never through slabs/roads (fresh physics mask); ending foreground kept low.
- NavMesh (final build with bake): both agents baked; raptor and T. rex paths complete across the valley and the stream
  (95,0,-2) -> (245,1,0) and back, at z -60 / 6 / 60 (`NavMesh.CalculatePath`).

### 4. Water (goal 4) - HDRP Water System (`LookTerrainWater.cs`)
- **Sea**: `WaterSurface` OceanSeaLake, Infinite, Y -40, teal shallows / deep blue, simulation foam, caustics.
  `Assets/Scripts/Rendering/WaterWeatherDriver.cs` eases swell, chaos and whitecaps with `_GlobalWetness`
  (calm by day/dawn, storm sea at night; writes only in 2 % steps because each write rebuilds the spectrum).
- **Stream**: River surface on a ribbon mesh that follows `EvaluateStreamX` (z -176..176, smoothed level 0.42 m above
  the bed, `Assets/Art/Terrain/Meshes/StreamRibbon.asset`), tannin-brown water.
- **Titan Meadows lake**: Pool surface (96 m quad) at Y -7.25 in the existing hollow (level chosen so no water shows at
  the quad edge).
- Removed: the unlit exposure-scaled ocean grid (`DawnOceanGrid`, `MAT_DawnOcean` no longer used by Zone 4), the 29
  stream cubes' MeshRenderer/MeshFilter. **Kept**: their Trigger-layer BoxColliders with SurfaceTag Water, the rescue
  boat and its lights, all anchors and triggers. Looked at in GoldenDay and DawnCoast captures; at night I did not
  get a frame where the water is clearly in view, and DuskTransition / NightCalm were not captured (the surfaces are
  lit by the scene, so they should follow the presets, but that is not verified).
- Shore foam: not done (HDRP 17 water has no depth-based shoreline foam; would need water decals along the coast).

### 5. Wind (optional) - not done.

## Findings and what I did

### 1. (high) Ending capture showed a death screen
**Reproduced and explained; not caused by the terrain.** Logged every dino each 3-6 s with `script eval`:
- `jump_chapter Escape` puts the player at (495.5, 1, -47.5) inside the Old Yard; `yard_raptor_b` patrols to
  (486, -52) and **kills the player there after ~12 s** (logged: raptor at (495.19, -47.34), `IsDead=True`). That is
  normal gameplay (the player is standing still next to a patrol).
- The following `jump_chapter DawnCoast` revives the player (`IsDead=False`) **but the MenuController death panel
  stays on screen** - the "YOU WERE KILLED" frame at the coast is stale UI from the Escape death. Reproduced twice
  (capture `Screenshots/probe_dawn_after_escape_death.png`).
- Without a preceding death, DawnCoast x2 + 40 s: player alive, the two yard raptors keep patrolling inside the yard.
  Their NavMesh paths to (543, 1, -46) are **partial and end at the closed E-0 gate (504.3, -46)**: the yard is closed,
  they cannot reach the coast road.
- New capture 16: DawnCoast x2, 15 s after the second jump, player alive.
- Request for the lead: hide the death panel on `ReviveForCheckpoint` / chapter jump (MenuController, not my file).

### 2. (high) Valley lost its walls; neighbour tiles had no collider
- New `LookTerrainBounds.cs`: an **invisible collider ring** (310 BoxColliders, 1.6 m thick, from 4 m below the ground
  to 30 m above it) on a rounded rectangle **46 m outside the play area**. No renderer, so the dawn overlook view and
  the cutscene cameras are untouched. It blocks the player and, because the NavMesh is baked from colliders, cuts the
  dino NavMesh off from the outer island. The jungle belts close in along it (trees are placed densely next to walls).
- Neighbour tiles keep their `TerrainCollider` and get `NavMeshModifier.ignoreFromBuild = true` (NavMesh stays on the
  main tile; nobody falls through anywhere).
- Proof (final build, `NavMesh.CalculatePath`, both agents): from the valley (200,1,0), Titan Meadows (-40,0,-150),
  north of the yard (450,1,120), (140,0,-150) and (0,0,60) to (150,0,320), (-300,0,-60), (300,0,260), (300,0,-300),
  (-200,0,-300), (650,-38,-46): **no complete path** for Agent_Raptor or Agent_TRex. (The probe's (600,0,120) point
  is inside the ring - the headland east of the yard - so its two "complete" results are expected.)
- Route still works: raptor and T. rex paths complete for paddock gate -> ford -> Substation -> yard gap and valley
  north <-> south; player-size capsule checks every ~2 m along Service Road 3: 0 hits on any plant/rock collider.
  (The Humanoid agent has no baked NavMesh, so the player route is proven with the capsule checks, not a path.)
- Terrain heights were not raised: the outer hills stay as they were (walls by collider, not by geometry).

### 3. (high) Sea: no shore foam, no shallows, black from above
- Seabed (`LookTerrainShape.OuterElevation`, dc < 0) as suggested: `beach - 3.5*SS(0,140,-dc) - 48*SS(120,460,-dc)
  + ripple*SS(100,300,-dc)` - a long sandy shelf (~2.6 m deep 40 m out, ~4 m at 100 m), then the drop.
- Sea: refraction (0.10, 0.55, 0.55), absorption 14 m, max refraction 2.5 m, scattering (0.03, 0.26, 0.25).
- **Shore foam**: one `WaterDecal` under the sea (Water Decal Sample shader graph, `_TYPE` = Texture, `_AffectFoam` 1,
  `_AffectDeformation` 0), region 2048 m over the whole island, atlas resolution 1024 (the HDRP asset's water decal atlas
  is 1024, so 2048 does not fit). Its mask `Assets/Art/Terrain/ShoreFoamMask.asset` is generated each build from
  `CoastDistance`: a wash band at the waterline (2-22 m, broken by noise, running a few metres up the sand) plus a
  broken surf line 14-34 m out; R = surface foam, G = deep foam. Sea: `decalRegionSize` 2048, `foamResolution` 2048.
- At night `WaterWeatherDriver` now also eases the scattering colour to a dark teal and the foam to grey with the
  storm value (Play only; the edit-mode NightCalm preview still shows a bright cyan sea, see rough edges).
- Verified: 08 (teal shallows + foam line from the air), 13 (foam band and sand under the water at dawn), extra
  `after_sea_dusk`, `after_play_sea_nightcalm_overlook`, `after_play_sea_nightstorm_overlook`.

### 4. (medium) Frozen headland step at x = 520

### 5. (medium) Hill jungle read as an orchard of lollipops
- **Hill jungle is now instanced** (`Assets/Scripts/Rendering/InstancedFoliageRenderer.cs`): 59,413 canopy trees in
  clumps (4.5 m cells, 0-3 trees per cell from a clump field: closed canopy in dense patches, clearings between) +
  51,814 mid-layer plants (tree fern, banana, pachira, shrub, monstera; also on slopes up to 50 deg so steep faces are
  not bald). jungle_tree scale 0.9-1.6; more palms (40 %) fill the band between the mid layer and the high crowns.
  Baked to `HillJungleInstances.bytes` (generated, git-ignored), drawn with `Graphics.RenderMeshInstanced` per camera
  (beginCameraRendering): cells frustum-culled, per-instance LOD within 260 m, one LOD per cell beyond, lists rebuilt
  only when the camera moves > 1 m or turns > 1 deg.
- **Impostors** (`LookVegetationImpostors.cs`): the trees' far LOD is now two crossed quads whose texture is the tree,
  rasterised on the CPU from LOD1 (front + side view, alpha-tested like the leaves, mip levels keep the alpha coverage).
  Replaces the vertex-clustered FarLOD/FarLOD2 blobs (deleted). Trees: LOD0 -> LOD1 -> LOD2 -> impostor -> culled.
  The instanced hill set switches to the impostor at 35 % of the LODGroup distance (jungle tree ~45 m x scale), because
  the full distances put ~10 M triangles on screen from the Visitor Center.
- **Cross-fade NOT enabled, on purpose**: measured at Substation, `fadeMode = CrossFade` took draw calls from 1,145 to
  3,416 (GPU Resident Drawer stops instancing cross-fading LODGroups; trees only: 3,152). LODGroups stay `None`.
- Hill ground under the canopy: more leaf litter (JungleFloor 0.42), less moss.
- Valley (critic's "park"): play-area trees now follow sharp groves (`ForestDensity` floor 0.07 instead of 0.25) -
  groves and open grass instead of evenly spaced palms (10, 06).
