# Round 4 critic report: polish (independent art director, read-only)

## Overall

Not amazing yet. Goals 2, 3, 4, 8 and 10 hold up in the captures, and the trikes no longer merge. But the new shot-3 staging walks the rail brachiosaurus onto the visitor promenade: its front feet stand on the paving, straddling the glass balustrade beside the guests, for the whole shot. The kit dressing adds the badge and stencil, but its uniform grain lifts the cards from 13 to ~20 and flattens the controls bands, and the screws and bevel barely read. The storm flash is random and unproven, the 'misty island' caption is still there, perf was not measured, and three screens were never captured. Re-stage the brachiosaurus first.

- **[medium] Kit grain breaks the controls bands and lifts every card**
  - Evidence: 01_controls_after: the band rows are a flat L 22 (opaque, so they hide the grain), while the plain rows run 13-32 (mean 19-22). The bands read as smooth strips on a cloudy card (the MOUSE row is mottled, WASD and SHIFT are flat). The card went from a flat 13 (r4_a0, before the grain) to a mean of 20, p1-p99 15-28: not 'a few sRGB levels'. The goal-1 band check predates the grain.

- **[medium] The intro's one lightning beat is random every run**
  - Evidence: IntroStormFlash calls TriggerLightningFlash(0.9f), which picks a random azimuth 0-360 and elevation 38-72 (AtmosphereController.cs:893-894).   - Fix: In IntroStormFlash, call atmosphere.TriggerLightningFlashFrom(new Vector3(-1f,-1.6f,-0.5f), 0.9f, true). The strike then sits about 22-27 deg left of the shot-6 lens, inside the 65 deg cone, so the roofline reads as a silhouette on a flashing sky. Ask the lead for a forced bolt: ForceBolt is public, but Cretacea.Story does not reference Rendering. Capture shot-6 frames at 1.42-1.5 s.
