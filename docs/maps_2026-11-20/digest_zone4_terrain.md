# zone4_terrain

## Summary
Zone 4 (Assets/Editor/WorldBuilder/Zone4_TerrainJungleAndDawnCoastBuilder.cs) builds the terrain and splat, 165 trees, 225 grass tufts, 36 boulders, a 29-cube stream and a custom ocean. `cretacea build_all_scenes` rebuilds all of it. Why the island reads as desert:
(1) The splat rules `y>22 => rock` and `x>546 => rock` paint the 58-136 m smooth-dome ring (44 % of the map) and the coast brown. A numpy copy of the rules gives the live 59/34/6.5 % shares.
(2) The 'grass' texture is tan straw. A binder bug makes all 4 layers use the wet mask (smoothness 0.68-0.89). The project has no green or sand ground texture.
(3) About 5 trees per hectare, none on the ridge. The '3,600 objects' are 225 tufts, each split into 4-21 sub-meshes. Plant materials have no mask maps and very likely no alpha clipping.
(4) Ocean and stream use an unlit shader multiplied by 2600 x exposure: blown out at dawn (EV 8.9), about 1000x too bright at night (calculated). The seabed is only 2 m deep, the headland cliff meshes are likely buried, and HDRP Water is off.
The notes give exact lines, coordinates, triangle counts and the code hooks for each change.

## How to
- Zone 4 runs inside `cretacea build_all_scenes [bake_navmesh=true]` (CretaceaWorldAssembler.cs:170-180; menu Cretacea/World/Build All Scenes). BuildMainWorldScene creates a NEW empty scene (line 270), then builds Zones 1-4 (274-283), then bakes the NavMesh (352). There is no per-zone command; bake_navmesh=false makes test rebuilds faster. Hand edits to MainWorld.unity are always lost.
- Assets Zone 4 updates in place: CretaceaTerrain.asset (heights and splat fully rewritten); TL_*.terrainlayer (tileSize, smoothness, metallic and texture refs overwritten every build; normalScale, diffuseRemap and maskMapRemap are left alone); MAT_CretaceaTerrain.mat (created only if missing, Zone4:188-195, so manual edits survive); DawnOceanGrid.asset (rebuilt). MAT_DawnOcean.mat is never written.
- Height is EvaluateWorldElevation(x,z) in world Y (Zone4:288-347). The heightmap is normalised with TerrainOriginY -42 / HeightRange 180 (Zone4:23,26) and clamped to [-42,136] (346). The plateaus (341-344) hold the Visitor Center/plaza at -0.05, Substation pad 2.26, Old Yard 0.96 and coast road 0.96. Zones 1-3 hard-code prop heights to these values (e.g. Zone3_SubstationAndYardBuilder.cs:55,71,322) and never sample the terrain, so keep the plateaus and valley heights when reshaping.
- The splat is painted only in Zone4:110-172; layer order is Mud, Grass, Gravel, RockyCliff (BuildTerrainLayers 201-210). Footsteps map the dominant layer index through PlayerController.terrainLayerSurfaces {Mud,Grass,Gravel,Concrete} (PlayerController.cs:53-59); any index past the array plays Grass. Keep that array in sync when adding or reordering layers. HDRP TerrainLit draws up to 8 layers in one pass, and the engine sets _TERRAIN_8_LAYERS/_MASKMAP itself (TerrainLit.shader:59-62).
- Terrain layer textures are bound by a substring search over Assets/Art/Textures (Zone4:229-245). The mask line (240) takes the last match, so every layer gets *_MaskMap_Wet. Filter out `_wet` or bind explicit paths. Mask maps have B=0 because CretaceaPbrImporter.PackMaskMap writes B=0 (CretaceaPbrImporter.cs:428-436). Pack each set's *_Height.png into B before setting _EnableHeightBlend/_HeightTransition on MAT_CretaceaTerrain.
- Every plant is a GameObject made by WorldMaterialPalette.InstantiatePropOrFallback(stem, parent, pos, euler, targetSize, grounded, addCollider, useMeshCollider, fallbackMat, customName) (WorldMaterialPalette.cs:351-406). It loads the LOD0 FBX by exact filename stem, scales uniformly so the LARGEST bounds axis equals max(targetSize), puts it on the Environment layer, adds no LODGroup and leaves shadows on. Materials are embedded FBX materials (materialImportMode 2, no postprocessor), so fix them with a remap or AssetPostprocessor using HDMaterial.SetAlphaClipping/SetAlphaCutoff/SetDiffusionProfile/ValidateMaterial (HDRP package Runtime/Material/MaterialExtension.cs:369-513).
- Exclusion data already available in Zone 4: FoliageExclusions rects (Zone4:51-60) with IsExcludedFromFoliage (679-687); the Road 3 polyline with DistanceToRoad3 (31-36, 373-389); EvaluateStreamX (371); TallGrassPatches (39-48); the plateau rects (341-344); td.GetSteepness; SampleTerrainY (689-696). Zone 1-3 colliders already exist when Zone 4 runs, so Physics.CheckBox/CheckCapsule can mask buildings and paths. Call Physics.SyncTransforms() first, because the assembler only syncs at CretaceaWorldAssembler.cs:351, after Zone 4, and skip the terrain's own collider.
- The NavMesh is baked after Zone 4 from PhysicsColliders on Default|Environment|Door (CretaceaWorldAssembler.cs:1262-1270), so every trunk or rock collider already cuts a hole. The carving NavMeshObstacles on each tree and boulder (Zone4:498-503, 523-527) are redundant; drop them before raising density.
- Trees and boulders share one System.Random(90421) stream (Zone4:463, 469-528). Changing the number or order of random calls in the tree loop moves every boulder. Each grass patch has its own seed, 199600+i*31 (436).
- HDRP Water is off: supportWater: 0 in HDRP High Fidelity.asset:234, Balanced:236 and Performant:235. The WaterRendering override is already on in the default volume profile. WaterSurface API: surfaceType OceanSeaLake/River/Pool; geometryType Quad/Custom/InstancedQuads/Infinite; meshRenderers list for Custom (HDRP Runtime/Water/WaterSurface/WaterSurface.cs:149-159). The ocean today is Zone4:571-581 at Y -40; the stream is Zone4:400-417.
- Keep the gameplay pieces when replacing visuals. Each stream cube carries the Trigger layer, a trigger BoxCollider and SurfaceTag Water for water footsteps (Zone4:408-416). The 7 TallGrassZoneVolume trigger spheres (Zone4:425-433) drive stealth visibility (PlayerController.cs:568-572). The ending anchors coast, ending_view and dawn_coast are at (543, 1.0-1.05, -46) yaw 90 looking +X; dawn_crane_peak is at (536, 15.2, -42); the coast_ending trigger is at (536.5, 3.5, -46) (Zone4:561-565).
- GPU Resident Drawer is on (HDRP High Fidelity.asset:526-531 mode 1; GraphicsSettings.asset:58 m_BrgStripping 2), so identical MeshRenderers are instanced automatically. No LODGroups exist yet (env.txt lodGroups=0). The *_LOD1/_LOD2.fbx files (triangle counts in section 6 of the notes) are ready to be built into LODGroup prefabs.
