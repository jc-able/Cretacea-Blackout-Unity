# story_flow

## Summary
I mapped the whole story flow in StoryDirector: the 8-shot prologue (~52 s), the Ch1 blackout timeline, the Ch2-6 beats with trigger boxes and anchors in world coordinates, and the ~16 s dawn crane ending. I also mapped the end state of all 8 jump_chapter targets, the CLI hooks, the death and end screens, and the save path. For the look pass: plain Play starts at Ch1 (Night). Every chapter start snaps the atmosphere preset and all 13 light groups through ApplyNightFacilityState. The preset numbers come from the builder table (CretaceaWorldAssembler.cs:802-919), not from the AtmosphereController defaults. Exposure is fixed per preset and does not change indoors. Look risks: the ending shot puts the sun dead centre and is about 4 stops over. The intro and ending blends interpolate lux linearly but EV in log space, so mid-blend frames blow out and the sun sweeps across the sky. The DawnCoast jump plays the ending cutscene (confirmed in the lead's capture). Flow risks: the Ch1 flashlight pickup can vanish after a restart or jump (soft-lock), CLI jumps leave fired triggers and the death/end panels in place, and every Play press overwrites the owner's save file.

## How to
- Plain Play in MainWorld starts at Chapter 1, because the builder sets editorStartCheckpoint=Night (CretaceaWorldAssembler.cs:1054-1055). The intro only plays from Menu NEW GAME or `unity command cretacea jump_chapter -- --chapter Intro`.
- A Play-mode jump calls StoryDirector.StartFromCheckpoint(cp, freshPreset:true) without reloading the scene (CretaceaWorldAssembler.cs:1631-1693). Paddock has no jump target; use `Cretacea.Story.StoryDirector.Instance.StartFromCheckpoint(Cretacea.Core.CheckpointId.Paddock, true)`. OneShot StoryTriggerVolumes stay fired, so stop and restart Play to re-test a beat.
- What is lit when: ApplyNightFacilityState(blackout) (StoryDirector.cs:294-357) snaps the preset and all 13 light groups at every chapter start. Scripted lighting changes happen only in the intro (SD:440-531), the blackout (SD:686-782), the transfer lever (SD:1193-1215) and the ending (SD:1448-1494).
- The preset numbers (EV, lux, sun Euler, fog) that end up in the scene come from SerializeAtmospherePresets (CretaceaWorldAssembler.cs:802-919). The AtmosphereController defaults (:163-254) only apply if fewer than 5 presets are serialized. Only AtmosphereController writes exposure (Fixed mode, :407-412); InteriorZoneVolume changes audio only.
- The scenes are generated. Trigger boxes and anchors live in the Zone1-4 builders (WorldArchitectureKit.BuildStoryTrigger / BuildWorldAnchor); edits to StoryDirector code survive build_all_scenes. The objects the flow toggles (lobby_shutter_closed, lobby_damage, staff_exit_collapse, sub_blocker, generator) must be built active so they register (SD:160-166).
- Cutscene cameras use CameraDirector.GlideCutsceneShot(from, to, lookFrom, lookTo, seconds, fovStart, fovEnd, abort) (CameraDirector.cs:204-250). The intro shot coordinates are at StoryDirector.cs:460-531. The ending crane is at CameraDirector.cs:258-282, around ending_view (543, 1.05, -46).
- CLI drive: PlayerController.Instance.Teleport(pos, yaw, pitch); PlayerController.Instance.Flashlight.SetHasFlashlight(true, true, false). WorldRegistry.Instance.GetInteractable(id).OnInteract(null) completes holds instantly (ids: terminal, control_console, gate_box, substation_Breaker1/2/3, substation_FuelPump, substation_GeneratorStarter x3, substation_TransferLever, doc_*). Also GetDoor(id).Open(null, true); GameEvents.RaiseStoryTriggerEntered(id); Cretacea.AI.DinoSpawner.Instance.DespawnAll(); CameraDirector.Instance.SwitchToCutsceneCamera(pos, look, fov).
- Save file: ~/Library/Application Support/DefaultCompany/CretaceaBlackoutUnity/cretacea_save.json. It is written by every Ch1 start (including plain Play), checkpoint triggers, first document reads and deaths. Back it up before testing and restore it afterwards.
- The DawnCoast jump spawns inside the coast_ending box, so it plays the ending cutscene the first time in a Play session; jump twice for a static dawn view. The OldYard jump spawns inside reach_yard, which causes a camera shake and a save write (from reading the code).
- After a death or the end screen, a CLI jump leaves the panel up unless the target chapter saves a checkpoint (Night, OldYard). Domain reload on Play is disabled (EditorSettings.asset:29-30), GameState is DontDestroyOnLoad, and death/restart reload the scene "MainWorld".
