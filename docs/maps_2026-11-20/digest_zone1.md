# zone1

## Summary
Zone 1 (Visitor Center) is fully generated by Zone1_VisitorCenterBuilder.cs from WorldArchitectureKit cube boxes and WorldMaterialPalette. Mapped: 14 rooms plus plaza, dock and gallery (bounds, materials, props, 48 lights in live candela, signs, decals, anchors, ASCII sketch). The look problems are mostly systemic:
(1) Every surface is a Unity cube carrying one texture copy per face, with materials at uv0 and tile 1, so textures stretch over 8-131 m.
(2) PlasterClean (walls and 8 ceilings) has no texture. DarkPaintedMetal binds a mustard-yellow paint (501 renderers). The wet floors get dry mask smoothness, and the lobby floor is brown cracked exterior concrete.
(3) Lamps were tuned for GoldenDay (11.5k-85k cd) but stay on at night. The blackout is lit only by saturated red points, and 6 rooms are fully dark.
(4) 26 lights have no fixture. All signs are blank green boxes, although 20 sign textures exist.
(5) Placeholders: the gift shop is empty with no west wall, the stairs dead-end into the mezzanine rail, and the dock canopy and gallery roof float. Smaller defects: floating CCTV bank and note, corridor lights inside a pipe, misplaced puddles and sparks, 0.25-0.34 m ceiling slots.

## How to
- Edit code, never the scene: `cretacea build_all_scenes` -> CretaceaWorldAssembler.cs:167-189 -> BuildMainWorldScene (NewScene from scratch, Zone 1 at :274, NavMesh bake :352, save :361). Zone1_VisitorCenterBuilder.Build() (Z1:33-46) runs 6 section builders in a fixed order.
- Every coordinate in Zone1_VisitorCenterBuilder.cs is a world-space literal. Groups are created at the origin, so CreateBox localPos == world pos (WorldArchitectureKit.cs:926-952). Exceptions: CCTV bank children are local to (-65,1.85,33.62) (Z1:199), the collapse group is at (-64.5,0,-10) (Z1:522), and flashlight children are local.
- Walls: BuildWallWithOpenings(start,end,baseY,height,thickness,mat,OpeningSpec[],skirting,dado,palette,name,includeInOcclusion) (WorldArchitectureKit.cs:94). The wall is centred on the line with ONE material on both faces; openings use centerAlong in metres from start. Skirting/frames = DarkPaintedMetal and dado = BrushedSteel are hard-coded (WorldArchitectureKit.cs:122-124). Rooms (BuildBoxRoomShell, :445) are floor + ceiling + RainBlocker only.
- Lights: BuildHdrpLight (WorldArchitectureKit.cs:657-704) writes Light.intensity raw, which the live scene reports as Candela although the parameter is named intensityLumens. It forces useColorTemperature at 5500 K by default and creates no fixture mesh. Groups: VCMain is on before the blackout and off after; VCEmergency is on only in the blackout; VCExit is always on (StoryDirector.cs:297-309). LightGroupMember caches the authored values as 100 % and fades via SetLightDimmer; pairedEmissives are scaled via an MPB _EmissiveColor (LightGroupMember.cs:76-161).
- Materials: WorldMaterialPalette ctor (WorldMaterialPalette.cs:91-130) picks texture sets by substring folder hints (:526-550, first match wins). CretaceaPbrImporter.CreateOrUpdateHdrpLitMaterial (:460-539) reuses existing .mat assets and only rewrites maps, metallic/smoothness and remap ranges (reset to 0..1 when a mask exists). It never sets _UVBase or tiling, so add planar/triplanar + _TexWorldScale in GetOrBuildPbr. MAT_* are shared by all 4 zones.
- Props: InstantiatePropOrFallback (WorldMaterialPalette.cs:351) scales UNIFORMLY to max(targetSize) and seats bounds.min.y at worldPos.y when grounded. All 27 stems Zone 1 uses resolve to FBX in Assets/Art/Props. Unused ready assets: lamp_bulkhead, switch_cabinet, breaker_box, power_transfer_switch, gate_control_box, trash_bin, park_bench, lamp_post; textures ceiling_tiles, drywall_paint, paving_stone, carpet_office, all *_Wet maps, metal_painted_worn_grey.
- Signs: make a material with p.CreateTextureQuadMaterial(name, stem, emissive, intensity, tint) (WorldMaterialPalette.cs:188; example Zone2_ServicePathsBuilder.cs:168). Stems in Assets/Art/UI/Signs include security_office, control_room, records, feed_prep, emergency_exit, staff_only, lobby_welcome, rex_banner, park_logo, raptor_poster and fire_assembly. BuildIlluminatedSign/CreateBox is a cube, so the texture repeats on all faces; use a Quad for the face.
- Keep story ids: door ids, trigger ids and WorldAnchor ids (start, spawn_records, dock, terminal, control_console, status_wall, manual, lobby_damage, lobby_shutter_closed, staff_exit_collapse, wing_*, dock_door) are consumed by StoryDirector/WorldRegistry (WorldRegistry.cs:30-60). Objects under named-anchor groups (shutter Z1:598, lobby_damage Z1:636, collapse Z1:525) get SetActive'd by story beats.
- Rain and probes: every roofed room keeps a layer-13 RainBlocker cube above its ceiling (rendered by StormRainOcclusionController.cs:170). The 62 DefaultHDMaterial renderers are exactly these cubes (59 in Zone 1). InsetRoomCeilingAndRainBlocker (Z1:932-946) shrinks the ceiling and blocker together. BuildRoomReflectionProbe (WorldArchitectureKit.cs:746) is Realtime/OnEnable with multiplier 0.45 and no culling mask, and also creates the room's InteriorZoneVolume audio trigger.
- Exposure math for re-authoring the lamp table: white point = 1.2 x 2^EV100 nits (1.8 nits at EV 0.6). Today a 620 cd corridor lamp puts about 17 nits on the plaster wall (about 10x over white), and 10-14 nit emissives are 5-8x over. Reference targets: troffer about 4,000-5,000 lm (about 400 cd point or a 1.2x0.3 m area light), emergency twin-head 200-400 lm, exit sign 50-150 nits. Z1:281 uses unseeded Random.Range; seed it for stable rebuilds. Verify with JumpChapterCli (CretaceaWorldAssembler.cs:1593): Night, Records, Dock.
