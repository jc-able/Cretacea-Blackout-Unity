# zone23

## Summary
Zones 2-3 are generated by Zone2_ServicePathsBuilder.cs and Zone3_SubstationAndYardBuilder.cs from WorldArchitectureKit.CreateBox primitive cubes with default 0..1 UVs and no tiling anywhere, so every wall, slab, pad and fence span stretches one texture tile over its whole face. The tall flat path walls are 14 single-cube 5 m x 0.35 m ConcreteExterior walls (16-23 m) standing in a bare field that Zone4 keeps free of all scatter; only 9 trees and 2 vines dress them. The flat walled lot is one 110 x 146 m slab cube plus four 104-145 m walls, about 75 % empty. Lights are applied as candela (2200-36000 cd = 50-230 lx pools vs 10-20 lx real) and almost none has a fixture; in blackout the paths show only the kiosk and one lamp, and the yard entry is unlit. The Talon pen hero view is a rock-textured 110 x 90 m box with 5 trees; the Substation has no transformers or structures. Coplanar floors risk z-fighting; valley props use hard-coded Y. Six sign PNGs, several decals and all prop mask maps are unused.

## How to
- Rebuild path: `cretacea build_all_scenes` -> CretaceaWorldAssembler.BuildMainWorldScene (Assets/Editor/WorldBuilder/CretaceaWorldAssembler.cs:267-284) regenerates MainWorld.unity from code in this order: Zone1, Zone2 (:277), Zone3 (:280), then the Zone4 terrain (:283), then the NavMesh bake (:350-354). Edit the builders, never the scene. WorldMaterialPalette rewrites Assets/Art/Materials/World/*.mat on every run, and GetOrBuildPbr re-applies metallic/smoothness each time.
- Position conventions: the zone roots sit at the origin. These helpers take WORLD positions: InstantiatePropOrFallback, BuildHdrpLight, BuildDecal, BuildWorldAnchor, BuildStoryTrigger, BuildConduitRun, BuildWallWithOpenings, BuildChainlinkFenceSpan, BuildFramedDoor, BuildBoxRoomShell, BuildRoomReflectionProbe. These take LOCAL positions: CreateBox, BuildIlluminatedSign, BuildRainBlockerSlab and Zone3.CreateDinoBlockerSlab. Local matters inside sub-roots such as OverturnedRangerJeep (104,0,-7.8), StreamBridge_Culvert (201,0,4), the BreakerPedestal racks and door roots.
- CreateBox (WorldArchitectureKit.cs:926-952) is the single choke point for all architecture: a scaled primitive cube with 0..1 UVs per face. Fix texture scale there once (a cached cube mesh with UV = face size / tile, which keeps batching) or in WorldMaterialPalette.GetOrBuildPbr (:132-161) via HDRP Lit planar/triplanar (_UVBase, _TexWorldScale, then HDShaderUtils.ResetMaterialKeywords; confirm the enum values for HDRP 17). Chainlink needs its own ~1-1.5 m tile.
- Zone2/3 run before the terrain exists, so every Y is hard-coded (paths 0, Substation SubY 2.3, Yard YardY 1.0). To seat props on the ground, call Zone4_TerrainJungleAndDawnCoastBuilder.EvaluateWorldElevation(x,z) (public static, Zone4:288-347). Plateaus: VC/paths/Talon Y -0.05 (X -104..92, Z -96..130, margin 18); Substation 2.26 (X 248..288, Z -19..19, margin 20); Yard 0.96 (X 398..508, Z -97..52, margin 18).
- Zone4 FoliageExclusions (Zone4:51-60) remove all scatter from: the paths (X 20..94, Z -16..36), the jeep pad (98..112, -15..-1), the Substation (244..294, -22..22), the Yard (396..510, -98..54) and the Talon pen (-92..22, 40..132). To get jungle around these zones, shrink those rectangles or place vegetation in the Zone2/3 builders with InstantiatePropOrFallback(stem, parent, worldPos, euler, size, grounded:true, addCollider:false).
- InstantiatePropOrFallback (WorldMaterialPalette.cs:351-406) finds an asset by file stem under Assets/Art/Props, Foliage and Rocks. It scales uniformly so the largest bounds axis = max(targetSize), puts bounds.min.y at pos.y and sets the Environment layer. A missing asset becomes a grey cube proxy. FBX prop materials ignore the *_MaskMap.png and *_MaskMap_Wet.png files that sit in each prop's Textures folder.
- Lights: BuildHdrpLight (Kit:657-704) writes light.intensity raw, and the runtime reads it as candela (lights.txt). It also forces colorTemperature 5500 K and contact shadows. StoryDirector.cs:302-317 switches lights on and off by LightGroupId: Paths and Talon are OFF in blackout; VCExit, PaddockBattery, SubBattery and YardBattery are ON; AuxSub, AuxRoute and AuxYard turn on after aux_power. Put new always-on readability lights in a battery group. AuxRoute lights need sequenceOrder + pairedEmissives.
- Do not move or rename gameplay pieces: the crawl gap (NorthLeaf_BentUp, bottom lip Y 1.24; fence_gap and paddock_entry anchors); the 1.6 x 3.2 m squeeze arch at (401,-22.8) and its SqueezeGap_DinoBlocker; the sub_blocker ring; the talon_gate, e0_gate_leaves, workshop_door_e and office/tool door DoorControllers; the Interact_* boxes; the SubstationStation powerConduitRenderers (they turn from red to green); PathLampSparksVfx and every anchor that WorldRegistry/StoryDirector look up by name; the story triggers at the coordinates in notes section 2.
- NavMesh bakes from PhysicsColliders only (CretaceaWorldAssembler.cs:1262-1270). Collider-less dressing does not affect AI. Anything with addCollider:true inside the 4.6 m paths, the 1.6 m squeeze gap or the 1.22 m crawl changes the navmesh and the player's route.
- Coplanar tops: BuildBoxRoomShell floors (Kit:466) top out exactly at baseY, which is also the yard slab's top (Y 1.0, Zone3:322). All service-path road segments top out at Y 0 and overlap at the bends. Fix by lowering room floors by ~2 cm or cutting the slab under buildings, and by giving each road segment a unique ~1 mm offset or merging them.
- The previous AI's blueprint and QA are stale in places. The live jeep is at (104,-7.8), not (182,-22). The WH B roof holes and the e0 asphalt stencil were never built. Values commented as 'lm' are applied as candela. The light-grey roofs in aerial shots are most likely the RainBlocker slabs: they have no material (DefaultHDMaterial) and sit on layer 13, which the gameplay camera excludes (CretaceaWorldAssembler.cs:597).
