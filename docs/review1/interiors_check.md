# Review 1 — second opinion on segment `interiors` (score 4/10)

A second, sceptical critic looked at the same captures: which findings hold (with what was measured), which do not, and what the first critic missed.

## Kept (confirmed)

- **[blocker] breakroom_on.png** (lighting): In every power-on room, the top 0.4-1 m of each wall is a near-black band with a wavy lower edge, under a lit ceiling, so the ceiling looks like it floats over a dark gap. The band is navy, not neutral. The corridor has the same fault as near-black wedges at the top of the near walls.
  - Fix: Give troffers a real light distribution: HDRP rectangle area lights about 1.2 x 0.6 m, or at least spotAngle about 179 with innerSpotAngle about 0.5 x outer, so intensity falls off like a cosine. Raise the wall-top fill: bake APV for the Visitor Center (PowerOn and Blackout scenarios), or lift the '_Wash' rule to about 250-300 cd and give every troffer a bounce share. Add a neutral ShadowsMidtonesHighlights and white-balance override to Look_Indoor so indoor shadows are not graded blue. Accept when the top 30 cm of every wall is at least 60% of the mid-wall luma with R >= B.
  - Why it is real: Measured. breakroom_on: band luma 13 against 71-76 on the wall and 66 on the ceiling. giftshop_on: 22 against 104/83. office_on left wall: RGB 21/28/32 (luma 27) against 100. lockers_on back wall: RGB 24/40/50 (37) against 79. corridor_on near walls: luma 22-31 against 120-133 on the lit wall, worse than the first critic's 71. Each scallop in the edge is one lamp's cone. Code: LookLights.cs:53 Wide=164 and :177 innerSpotAngle = 0.94 x outer for Throw.Down, so each downlight cuts off hard 8 degrees below the ceiling plane. A wall 3-6 m away therefore gets no direct light on its top 0.4-0.85 m. Below the cut-off the only light is the 90 cd '_Wash' spots (LookLights.Zone1.cs), the shadowless Bounce lights (only on shadowed lamps) and the ambient term, and Look_Indoor scales the ambient to 0.42. The night and storm grades push shadows blue everywhere, interiors included (LookAtmosphereProfiles.cs night.smhShadows 0.90/1.0/1.12, storm 0.86/1.0/1.14, temperature -4/-6), and Look_Indoor only overrides the IndirectLightingController, so the gap renders navy.
- **[major] records_dark.png** (geometry): The rooms are oversized and nearly empty, so they read as test chambers, not a working park building. Almost no small detail is in view.
  - Fix: As the first critic proposed: dress each room to real density without moving colliders, or add non-structural partitions. Records: steel shelving, archive boxes, labelled drawers. Office: CCTV console, filing cabinets, key box, radio chargers, a whiteboard. Break room: counter, sink, fridge, microwave, coffee machine. Gift shop: stocked gondolas with modelled products. Give corkboard sheets printed memo, roster and map textures. Add grime decals along skirtings and doors, plus ceiling detail (smoke detectors, sprinklers, vents).
  - Why it is real: Builder sizes (Zone1_VisitorCenterBuilder.cs:66-70, 634, 695): security office 14x12 m, lockers 10x12, records 18x12, break room 22x13, gift shop 22x20. In the frames: records has one desk, a CRT, one cabinet and a chair, yet the objective is to search it for a manual. The break room is three tables on a vast floor. The lockers are one row and two benches. In the gift shop the middle is empty floor, a bracket shelf has nothing on it, and the back shelf holds flat, untextured colour blocks. The office and break-room corkboards carry blank sheets: CorkBoard() builds Sheet_i from plain mPaper/mPaintYellow boxes, which breaks the bible's 'no blank sign plates'.
- **[major] breakroom_on.png** (colour): The break room and gift shop are sepia: amber troffer lenses, brown ceilings and orange floors. The same fixture is blue-white in the office, lockers and control room. This is the owner's 'colours are off' again.
  - Fix: Set every troffer rule to one fluorescent white (4000-4300 K). Build each lens colour from LensColor(l) instead of the two fixed tints. Put warmth in practicals instead: the vending machine, a pendant over the checkout, display-case lights.
  - Why it is real: Mean R/B over the frame: break room 1.70, gift shop 1.68, office 1.15. A zoom shows the break-room lens as saturated yellow-orange. Code: LookLights.Zone1.cs sets ^Light_Break_Main$ to 3600 K and ^Light_GiftShop$ to 3400 K. LookFixtures.LensFor (lines 811-813) gives every lamp from 2800 to 4000 K the fixed MAT_Lens_WarmWhite (1.0/0.86/0.66). LensColor() already computes the lamp's CCT colour, but LensFor ignores it. The bible reserves 4300-5600 K for fluorescent tubes.
- **[major] lobby_on.png** (lighting): With the power on, the lobby atrium already looks blacked out. Walls, glass, banners, the logo and the mezzanine are unlit navy. Only a floor pool and the fossil uplight are lit, so the power cut barely changes the game's showpiece room.
  - Fix: Add wall grazers along the long walls and under the mezzanine fascia. Put accent spots on Lobby_Banner_RexE/RaptorE and Lobby_ParkLogoN, light the reception desk with task lights, and add a glowing welcome sign. Aim for 60-110 lux on the walls so the blackout is a visible event. Give the lobby a neutral indoor grade.
  - Why it is real: lobby_on walls: RGB 29/37/42 on the left and 20/24/26 on the right (luma 22-36). The framed sign on the right wall (x 1155-1235, y 115-225) is at luma 22. The floor pool is about 107. In lobby_dark the walls are at luma 15-33, almost the same values. Part of the navy is the global blue shadow grade described in finding 1.
- **[major] office_dark.png** (materials): Switched-off fixtures read as black wire grids on a lit ceiling. The corridor fixtures turn into dark slabs.
  - Fix: Make the frame, louvres and spine white enamel: dielectric, albedo about 0.75, added as a new palette surface. Keep the corridor diffuser visible and switch only its emission, with the 0.85 opal base, so a dead fixture reads as a pale panel in the beam.
  - Why it is real: In the office_dark zoom, the dead troffer shows a black outline and a black cross with ceiling-coloured panes. The same grid appears in breakroom_dark, records_dark, 08 and 12. Code: LookFixtures.PanelAt builds the Frame from DarkPaintedMetal and the louvres and spine from BrushedSteel (metallic remap 1). In an unlit room the metal reflects the dark probe, so it renders black. The corridor diffuser is a toggle object (Zone1_VisitorCenterBuilder.cs:586, BindLightGroupToggleObject) that is hidden in the blackout, leaving only the DarkPaintedMetal housing. In corridor_dark_noflash (x 610-670, y 110-180) it measures luma 3.5 against about 9 for the ceiling.
- **[major] 12_ch3_feed_prep.png** (exposure): On the white tile, the flashlight hot spot clips to a white disc with a bloom halo and the tile grout disappears. This is exactly the 'white disc' that the bible's acceptance criterion 4 rules out. Lighter surfaces in other rooms clip the same way.
  - Fix: Let exposure rise toward EV 6 when the beam's sphere-cast hit is close. Alternatively, cap the hot spot by the albedo of the surface it hits. Bring HotSpotMaxLux down to the bible's 70, set the bloom threshold to about 1.0 and soften the night/storm tonemap shoulder so the hot spot rolls off and texture survives.
  - Why it is real: Feed prep: a 100x80 px core (x 600-700, y 335-415) is 96% at 254 or above, and the zoom shows no grout. Elsewhere: the records_dark ceiling corner is 83% clipped, the office_dark corner 92% in an 80x20 core, and the breakroom_dark far wall about 14 m away 40%. Code: FlashlightController.HotSpotMaxLux = 95. Its own comment says white tile at 7 m is 'still clipped and bloomed at 240', while the bible's target is about 70 lux at 10 m. The storm preset caps exposure at evMax 4.7, so it cannot adapt, and baseLook.bloomThreshold = 0 blooms everything.
- **[major] 03_ch1_control_room_lit.png** (materials): The control-room windows are a lit, milky teal film. A window onto the night should be the darkest surface in a lit room.
  - Fix: Use Premultiply blend with a base colour of about 0.02-0.04 and low alpha, keeping smoothness at 0.96, so only reflections and the exterior show. Re-check the lobby and gift-shop glass.
  - Why it is real: All five panes measure luma 63-83 (about RGB 65/80/77), the same as the ceiling (81). The paddock outside shows only faintly. Code: GlassArchitectural ends with _BaseColor (0.70, 0.82, 0.84, alpha 0.14) in Alpha blend mode (WorldMaterialPalette.cs about line 367), so the room lights a diffuse pale-teal layer over the exterior.
- **[major] 03_ch1_control_room_lit.png** (story): All six desk monitors are black in a powered control room, while the radio tells the player to log the camera feeds there.
  - Fix: Add an emissive screen per monitor bound to VCMain, using camera-feed images or cyan status pages at 30-45 nits. Switch it to static or off in the blackout.
  - Why it is real: The monitors measure luma 15-20. The subtitle reads 'log the Talon Ridge and Tyrant Valley feeds from the Control Room', and the console's interact prompt is 'Log Talon Ridge & Tyrant Valley Feeds'. Code: the control_console props (Zone1_VisitorCenterBuilder.cs about line 344) get no screen material. The office CCTV bank does have one (CctvScreenLive), and in office_dark it correctly switches to static.
- **[major] records_dark.png** (lighting): Light leaks through the records desk. A bright amber pool sits on the floor under the desk, directly below the beacon standing on top of it.
  - Fix: Set Shadow.Low (cached, 256) on that rule, or aim the cone up or sideways.
  - Why it is real: In the knee space at x 415-495, y 545-562 the floor measures RGB 220/149/71 (luma 158) with the red channel 40% clipped, against luma 71 on the beam-lit floor beside it. Code: LookLights.cs:78 gives ^Light_Records_ManualBeacon Shadow.None, and it is a downward 52-degree spot at 1.95 m (builder line 474), so the desk top blocks nothing.
- **[major] 08_ch1_staff_exit_collapse.png** (geometry): The staff-exit collapse, a story beat, is one clean tilted concrete box on a spotless floor.
  - Fix: Turn the slab into an invisible collider so the route stays blocked, and let debris_pile be the visible mass. Add fractured slab chunks, fallen ceiling tiles, a bent troffer hanging from a ragged hole, dangling cables, a dust decal fanned across the floor and dust in the beam.
  - Why it is real: In the frame: straight edges, even boardform texture, a few thin rods above it, no rubble or dust. Code: the 'FallenSlab' CreateBox (2.8x2.3x3.6 m ConcreteBoardform, euler 14/8/-18) sits over and hides the debris_pile mesh (Zone1_VisitorCenterBuilder.cs about line 685). 'BrokenRebar_0-4' are box sticks, and the 'jagged hole' is a straight gap between the StaffExit_CeilEast and StaffExit_CeilWest boxes.
- **[major] corridor_dark_noflash.png** (lighting): With the flashlight off, the near walls are crushed to black. The warm battery pools are islands with nothing around them.
  - Fix: Give the warm _Batt units a bounce of about 0.2-0.25 share, and keep the red heads without one, so walls 4-6 m from a pool sit at about 3-6 lux. With the flashlight off, near walls should stay above about luma 15.
  - Why it is real: The right wall (x 1000-1250, y 150-400) is RGB 2/4/4, 100% below luma 6. Over the whole frame, 44% of pixels are below luma 6 and 66% below 10, rising to 59% below 6 in the right half. The pools peak at luma 94-101. This matters more than it seems: the HUD stealth meter reads SHADOWED with the light off and EXPOSED with it on, so players will spend a lot of time in this state. Code: the '_Batt' rule is Shadow.None, and LookLights.Apply only creates a bounce when shadow != None (line 217), so the warm units have no bounce at all. Look_Indoor cuts the remaining ambient to 0.42.
- **[major] corridor_dark_noflash.png** (lighting): Every warm blackout pool starts from bare wall, with no unit or glowing lens: a lamp without a fixture. Upgraded from minor.
  - Fix: In LookFixtures.Build, send '_Batt' to Emergency(l) as well, so each unit gets a twin-head body with glowing lenses. Aim the heads along the corridor instead of straight down.
  - Why it is real: Bright apexes on plain plaster at x 695, y 312 and x 580, y 335 in corridor_dark_noflash, and at x 775, y 240 in corridor_dark; the zoom shows only plaster. The same sourceless washes appear on the left walls of office_dark and records_dark. Code: LookFixtures.Build sends only names containing 'Emerg' to Emergency(), which builds the twin-head body. The warm 'Light_<Room>_Batt' units (Zone1_VisitorCenterBuilder.cs BatteryLight) match no fixture family and return null. These units are the main light in every blackout interior (chapters 2-6), and bible criterion 2 says 'no lamp without a fixture'.
- **[minor] corridor_on.png** (lighting): Near lit fixtures clip into featureless shapes. The nearest corridor fixture is a glowing blob, not a lens in a frame. (Narrower than reported: the far troffers do show their louvres.)
  - Fix: Swap mTubeGlow in ExistingTroffer, or build the corridor tube with LensFor. Raise the bloom threshold to about 1.
  - Why it is real: The corridor fixture (x 620-660, y 235-275) is 55% clipped with a starburst. Near troffers are 88% clipped in lockers_on and 93% in 03, and their louvres are lost. The far troffers in office_on do show the 4x2 louvre grid. Code: the corridor diffuser is mTubeGlow at 70 nits (Zone1_VisitorCenterBuilder.cs:1684). LookFixtures.ExistingTroffer swaps only EmissiveCoolWhite/WarmWhite, so this lens never gets DiffuserNits = 25, the level LookFixtures' own measurements found to keep the louvres readable. Bloom threshold is 0.
- **[minor] 08_ch1_staff_exit_collapse.png** (colour): Under the beam, the staff exit becomes a lime-green tunnel. The crack and rust pattern is oversized, and the lower wall panel (dado) differs between the two walls.
  - Fix: Desaturate the PlasterPeeling90s tint by about 30% toward an institutional sage, tile the cracks at real scale, and give both walls the same lower panel.
  - Why it is real: Left wall RGB 83/94/56. Near the camera, the crack cells are roughly 30-50 cm across. The right lower wall is blue-grey (RGB 39/47/41) and the left is green (30/38/25). Code: the north liner has mWainscot, while Wall_StaffExit_South is PlasterPeeling90s full height with only a dado rail (Zone1_VisitorCenterBuilder.cs about lines 661-663).
- **[minor] 12_ch3_feed_prep.png** (geometry): Both doorways are flat black rectangles cut into the tile, with no frame, no door and no depth cue.
  - Fix: Add architraves and a door leaf standing ajar, plus a faint light or a visible surface beyond each opening.
  - Why it is real: x 272-362 measures luma 6.6 and x 903-993 measures 9.0, with no architrave or leaf visible.
- **[minor] lobby_on.png** (geometry): A blank two-tone box stands between the T. rex's feet, right at the hero prop's focal point.
  - Fix: Replace it with a real exhibit plaque with text and a graphic, or remove it.
  - Why it is real: A zoom of x 622-655, y 303-335 shows a plain white-over-pale-blue panel behind the support rod, with no text or graphic.
- **[minor] breakroom_dark.png** (weather): Bright dust specks hang in unlit air and read as stars or snow, not dust in a beam.
  - Fix: Fade the motes by beam or volumetric light so they glitter in the flashlight and disappear in the dark.
  - Why it is real: Specks at x 583, y 130 and x 780, y 130 peak at 126-147 against a background of 40-50. More appear in office_dark, in corridor_dark and in the dark band of breakroom_on. The dot at the exact screen centre (640, 358) in several frames is the crosshair, not a mote.

## Dropped (not real, overstated, or a capture artefact)

- Finding 13, sub-claim: the troffer lenses in office_on clip so badly that their louvres do not read.
  - Why not: A zoom of office_on's far troffer (x 325-505, y 160-215; 49% clipped) shows the full 4x2 louvre grid. Only the near troffers (lockers_on, 03) and the corridor tube lose their structure. The finding is kept, with this narrower scope.
- Finding 4, sub-claim: 'No fixture is in view', implying the lobby's main light has no body.
  - Why not: LookFixtures.Build gives ^Light_Lobby_Main a HighBay body. In lobby_on it is simply above the frame; the floor pool falls from above and behind the camera. The real fault is that the walls, banners and logo get no light, not a missing fixture. The finding is kept.

## Missed by the first critic

- **[minor] lockers_on.png** (UI): The see-through objective panel lets the lit troffer behind it show through, and the 'TAB JOURNAL & ROUTE MAP' line becomes unreadable. Text at the edge of the frame becomes unreadable whenever the player looks up at a lit ceiling.
  - Fix: Raise the panel's backing opacity to about 0.85, or put a fixed dark plate behind each text line, so legibility does not depend on what is behind the panel. Measured: in lockers_on the line sits on luma 126-134 and the text peaks at about 160, roughly 1.2:1. In office_on the same line sits on luma 66.
- **[minor] office_on.png** (materials): Every office-type room is built from the same recipe: FloorTileClean (large beige industrial tile), CeilingTiles, beige paint and a black skirting (Zone1_VisitorCenterBuilder.cs:66-70 and 634). The security office, locker room, control room, records annex and break room read as one recycled box. The locker room is not treated as a wet room.
  - Fix: Give each room its own surfaces: CarpetDark in the security office, control room and records annex; LabTileWhite walls with FloorTileWet in the locker room (the bible's wet-room rule); vinyl or VCT in the break room; and a different wall paint per room.
- **[minor] breakroom_on.png** (materials): The suspended ceiling is at double scale. CeilingTiles repeats every 2.4 m (WorldMaterialPalette.cs:120), and the ceiling_tiles texture holds a 2x2 grid (T-bar lines at about 1000 of 2048 px), so each tile is a 1.2 m square instead of 600 mm. The 1.22x0.62 m troffers (LookFixtures.TrofferGrid, 3.0x2.4 m spacing anchored on the first lamp) do not sit in the grid. The ceiling fills the top third of every room shot, so this makes the oversized rooms feel larger still.
  - Fix: Set CeilingTiles tileMeters to 1.2 (600 mm tiles) and snap troffer centres to the world tile grid, so each troffer replaces one 600x1200 tile.
- **[minor] 12_ch3_feed_prep.png** (materials): Large black flake blots that read as ink splats cover the feed-prep ceiling (y 60-240). PlasterClean is the white variant of the peeling-plaster texture at 3.0 m per repeat (WorldMaterialPalette.cs:117), and Room_FeedPrepWing uses it for its ceiling (builder line 1221). Every surface meant to be 'clean' therefore peels and cracks.
  - Fix: Give PlasterClean a genuinely clean plaster texture, or tone the flake contrast down in its tint and mask, and keep the peeling for PlasterPeeling90s only. A wet-room ceiling should be clean painted plaster or tiles.
