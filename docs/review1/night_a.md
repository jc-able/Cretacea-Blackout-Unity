# Review 1 (Nov 20, before the lead's look fixes in 07fd73d) — segment night_a

Images: Screenshots/review1/ (same camera positions as Tools/lookdev/capture_set.sh and route_test.py).

### Critic segment `night_a` (score 4/10)

Not amazing yet, and not consistent. The best route frames (14, 16 and especially 17 at the valley entry) finally look like the Jurassic Park storm: real rain streaks, warm practical lights reflected in wet ground, and a torch that shows texture. But the loading dock (13) is crushed to black with the torch on, the night palette has slid from blue moonlight into a teal two-tone with almost no red, and the outdoor presets show a bone-dry 'rainy' plaza with unlit lamp heads, a flat lawn for a meadow, frost-white backlit grass and a storm sky that is a haze-free black void.

- **[blocker] 13_ch3_loading_dock.png** (exposure): Crushed to black with the flashlight ON: the median pixel is 8.6/255, 48 % of the frame is below 8/255 and 67 % below 16. The white utility truck on the left (mean about 10/255) and the underside of the canopy (about 6/255) are invisible. Only the doorway to the moonlit jungle and the torch hot spot on the pillar read. A brightened crop shows the truck, wet asphalt and wall are all there, just unlit, so the player reaches the Dock checkpoint and sees a black screen.
  - Likely cause: In the blackout the DockMain light group (Light_Dock_Main, Light_Dock_Wash) is switched off (StoryDirector: SetGroup(DockMain, !blackout)). The only lamp left is the battery light 'Dock' at (8.6, 3.35, -13.6) in Zone1_VisitorCenterBuilder (rule ^Light_Dock_Batt, 520 cd, in LookLights.Zone1.cs). It sits behind and to the right of the player at the roller door and puts only about 5 lux on the pad ahead; the bible targets about 35 lux for emergency pools. The auto exposure (LookAtmosphereProfiles: CenterWeighted metering, histogramPercentages 40-88, storm EV 3.0-4.7) exposes for the bright jungle opening and the hot spot in the centre, which pushes it toward EV 4.7.
  - Suggested fix: Add a second battery unit facing the player as they arrive, for example Light_Dock_Batt_1 on CanopyCol_E around (21.3, 3.4, -10.0) aimed west with the same 520 cd rule. Or keep one Light_Dock_Main on emergency power at about 30 %. Either way the truck and pad should sit in a pool of about 35 lux. Then lower the meter's top percentile (88 to about 78), so one bright opening cannot drive the exposure to the dark end of its range. Target: under 10 % of pixels below 8/255 in this view.
- **[major] NightStormBlackout_meadow_eye.png** (colour): The night is not blue moonlight but a teal two-tone: outside the lamp pools the red channel is close to zero. Measured sRGB means: storm meadow grass (4,17,17), aerial meadow (3,22,22), aerial jungle (3,14,16), storm valley grass (3,14,13), valley-entry grass in 17 (5,18,19), NightCalm plaza sky (2,23,52). Next to the orange sodium pools the palette becomes teal-and-orange, the same 'colours are off' problem in a new hue, and the jungle reads as teal murk instead of dark green under blue light.
  - Likely cause: Every cool push stacks on the others, and two of them lean green. In LookAtmosphereProfiles.AllLooks(): WhiteBalance tint is -1 at night and -2 in the storm (negative means green), temperature -4 and -6, the shadows colour (SMH) (0.90,1,1.12) and (0.86,1,1.14), and the sky zenith and horizon tints (0.72,0.86,1.0) and (0.70,0.90,1.0). In AtmosphereController.GetKeyLook the moon is 7400-7800 K multiplied by the filters (0.80,0.90,1.0) and (0.76,0.88,1.0).
  - Suggested fix: Keep the blue and lose the green: tint +2 to +4, SMH shadows about (0.95,0.97,1.08), moon filter about (0.86,0.89,1.0) at about 6500 K, sky tints about (0.80,0.86,1.0), and storm saturation about -10. Aim for moonlit grass near (12,18,24) and concrete near (30,34,42), with red at roughly 40-60 % of blue in the shadows. Foliage then reads dark blue-green, and the warm pools stand out without looking like a colour grade.
- **[major] NightStormBlackout_plaza_eye.png** (weather): The storm does not read as a storm. Apart from the sky this is the NightCalm frame again: the jungle hill about 200 m away is crisp with no rain haze, there is no lightning, and the sky is a black void (2,12,18). One cloud edge is lit electric blue at upper right (about (7,37,62) where measured in the storm meadow) and looks like a hole punched in the cloud cover. The storm meadow and valley share the same black sky. Two caveats: rain cannot be judged in these frames, because the rain emitter follows the player, not the free camera; and the lamps are on only because the preset capture does not switch light groups.
  - Likely cause: LookAtmosphereProfiles.BuildProfile sets fog.colorMode = FogColorMode.SkyColor, and the storm profile inherits the night fogTint (3,3,3) and fogDepthExtent of 150 m. Beyond 150 m the fog takes the near-black colour of the storm sky, so distant terrain gets darker, not hazier. The Stormy volumetric clouds are rim-lit by the too-blue 19 lux moon.
  - Suggested fix: Give the storm a glowing veil: constant-colour fog in a dim blue-grey, or fogDepthExtent of about 400 m with fogAlbedo about 0.75, so the far hill and treeline stand as silhouettes in front of the haze. Reduce the moon's light on the clouds (cloudSunDimmer about 0.4) and desaturate them, so the cloud cover reads as a textured overcast. Let lightning do the sky reveals.
- **[major] NightCalm_plaza_eye.png** (materials): The plaza is bone dry in presets where it is raining (NightCalm wetness 0.75 and rain 0.45, storm wetness 1.0): the lamp heads and bollards do not reflect in the paving, there are no puddles, and there is no sheen under the pools. The storm plaza is the same. The reference is the wet concrete of Jurassic Park, but the hero exterior reads as a dry summer evening.
  - Likely cause: Other outdoor surfaces do react to the rain (the streaked reflection in 14, the jeep light on the gravel in 17), so the plaza slab seems to be left out. Check that MainPlaza_Paving (mPlazaPaving, Zone1_VisitorCenterBuilder around line 1674) gets a wet copy of its material in WetnessDriver and passes LookWeather.IsPuddleSurface. WetnessDriver also raises the lower end of the smoothness range by only 0.05 (wetSmoothnessMinRise), so mostly rough paving never builds a film of water.
  - Suggested fix: Make sure the plaza slab gets its wet material and puddles. For flat outdoor slabs, allow a bigger smoothness rise (about 0.25 at full wetness), so every lamp throws a long streaked reflection toward the camera. Check by eye in NightCalm that each bollard and lamp has a visible streak on the paving.
- **[major] NightCalm_plaza_eye.png** (lighting): The head of the hero lamp post is dark: there is no glowing diffuser under the lamp head, so the light seems to come from mid-air. Just under the arm the pole has hot orange flecks where the lamp lights its own pole. The pool is centred on the foot of the pole instead of under the head. With 120-degree cones the pools merge into one even orange carpet over the whole foreground (about 108/255, with no hot spot or falloff), so the lamps do not read as islands.
  - Likely cause: LookFixtures.Build() has no branch for ^Light_PlazaLamp. Only ^Light_PathLamp goes to PathLamp(), which moves the light under the head and adds a glowing lens. Light_PlazaLamp_* therefore stays where Zone1_VisitorCenterBuilder (around line 1530) puts it, at the post's own XZ (lx, 4.25, lz), with no lens. LookLights.Zone1 gives it a 120-degree downward cone (Throw.Down) at 1900 cd.
  - Suggested fix: Send Light_PlazaLamp (and Light_Canopy_Down and Light_Gallery_Down if they sit on posts) through PathLamp(), or an equivalent that finds the head of the plaza post, so the light moves under the head and gets a 170-nit lens. Narrow the cone to about 90-100 degrees, so each pool falls from about 130 lux at its centre to about 20 lux between posts.
- **[major] NightCalm_valley_eye.png** (vegetation): On the moon side (right half) the tall grass and gravel turn pale cyan-white. The brightest 5 % of pixels average (45,69,74) and cover every blade and pebble, so the grass reads as hoar-frost or snow, not wet tropical grass. The left half sits in teal-black (grass about 15/255). The storm valley and 18 show the same split (right gravel up to 111/255, left grass 15).
  - Likely cause: The terrain grass cards (MAT_grass_bermuda_01) use the plant smoothness range 0.10-0.62 (commented 'waxy tropical leaves, wet', LookMaterials.cs around line 185) plus light transmission through the leaves. The moon sits low, ahead and to the right (azimuth 115 degrees, elevation 29-31 degrees in AtmosphereController.GetKeyLook), so every card catches a grazing sheen in the moon's cyan-white. TerrainWetnessDriver makes every pebble glint as well.
  - Suggested fix: Give grass cards their own low gloss (_SmoothnessRemapMax about 0.30-0.35, specular occlusion on) and green-tinted transmission, so backlit grass glows dark green at the edges instead of silver. Keep crisp glints for puddles and broad leaves only. Add a little fill on the shadow side (night indirectDiffuse 1.5 to about 2.0) to lift the left half off black.
- **[major] NightCalm_meadow_eye.png** (vegetation): The lower 45 % of the frame is one flat dark-green carpet: no blades, no clumps, no tonal change (grass 18.8 ± 4.9 out of 255; in the storm version 14.3 ± 4.1, with 44 % of that frame below 16/255). It reads as a golf-course lawn or a flat fill, not a tropical meadow, and it is what the player sees from the plaza's viewing rail.
  - Likely cause: LookVegetationDetails.Paint fades the grass tufts out with distance from the play area (factor 1 - SS(40, 90, outside)), and Terrain.detailObjectDistance is 55 m. Beyond that only the grass terrain layer shows, and that layer has no large-scale variation.
  - Suggested fix: Give the grass layer large-scale variation: broad hue and brightness noise, darker clump patches, and mud and moss blends. Scatter tall grass, ferns, shrubs and a few rocks or logs across the part of the meadow seen from the rail; flat billboard stand-ins are fine beyond 55 m. Break up the lawn with an animal trail or a paddock fence.
- **[major] 13_ch3_loading_dock.png** (geometry): The object the torch lands on, in the centre of the frame, is a featureless pale-mint box: the dock dumpster. To its left, a flat untextured white wall panel with vines reads as a blank board. In the only lit spot of the frame the player is looking at primitive boxes.
  - Likely cause: Zone1_VisitorCenterBuilder.DressLoadingDock builds the 'Dumpster' from Box() primitives: a 1.9 × 1.2 × 1.1 m body in PaintedMetalGreen, a black lid slab and four 10 cm wheel boxes.
  - Suggested fix: Use a dumpster or skip model from Assets/Art if one exists. Otherwise model the parts that make it read as a dumpster (side ribs, fork pockets, a hinged two-part lid, wheels), and give it a darker green with rust and grime decals and a stencil. Texture the wall panel with the palette's concrete or plaster, so the beam has detail to reveal.
- **[major] 16_ch3_service_gate.png** (materials): The Paddock 4 chain-link gate does not read as chain-link. The closed leaf (centre, with the DANGER sign) looks like a solid grey steel sheet with evenly spaced polka-dot stains. The bent north leaf (left) looks like a halftone screen, and the amber lamp paints a solid pool on it. You cannot see the valley through the 'fence'. The leaf's bottom edge is a fully saturated glowing bar (255,208,9) that reads as a neon tube.
  - Likely cause: In Zone2_ServicePathsBuilder (around lines 184-188), SouthLeaf_Closed and NorthLeaf_BentUp are 14 cm thick CreateBox slabs using MAT_ChainlinkFence (alpha-clipped holes, mapped in world space at 1.8 m, which gives 5.6 cm diamonds). Front and back faces overlap, and the wires are thinner than a pixel, so anti-aliasing (TAA) averages them into an opaque veil. NorthLeaf_BottomLip uses p.EmissiveAmber.
  - Suggested fix: Build the leaves like the Talon fence (BuildChainlinkFenceSpan: one alpha-clipped plane in a pipe frame), with a texture bias so the mesh stays visible and see-through at 5-10 m. Make the bottom edge a bent rusted pipe with an amber reflective-tape decal. If the crawl gap needs a guide, the beacon above already provides one.
- **[minor] 15_ch3_path_raptor_crossing.png** (water): The puddle in the junction reads as a milky-white stain: mean (153,145,134) ± 20, brighter than the wet slab around it, with nothing mirrored in it (neither the lamp on top of the wall nor the base of the wall). The puddle in front of the gate in 16 is the same (122 ± 15).
  - Likely cause: The puddle decals (WxPuddle, MAT_WetPuddleDecal_Atlas, placed by LookWeather) show a strong but blurred reflection, taken from the reflection probe or the screen-space-reflection fallback, over a light base colour, instead of acting as a dark, near-perfect mirror.
  - Suggested fix: Darken the puddle base colour, raise smoothness to about 0.97 with a flat normal (ripples only as a faint normal), and reduce the share of ambient reflection, so screen-space reflections of the lamp and wall dominate. A puddle should be darker than the wet concrete around it, except where it mirrors a light.
- **[minor] 13_ch3_loading_dock.png** (weather): Round white specks hang in the air under the dock canopy, in front of the truck and against the ceiling. Under a roof they read as snow or dust, not rain. Outside, in 14, 16, 17 and 18, the rain correctly reads as streaks.
  - Likely cause: Either the rain particles (streaks or splashes) do not respect the canopy's rain blocker (Dock_Pad, addRainBlocker: true) right after the teleport, or a separate dust effect is lit by the torch. The frame does not show which.
  - Suggested fix: Under a rain blocker allow only drips and splashes along the roof edge. If the specks are dust motes, keep them inside the beam cone and make them much sparser.
- **[minor] 14_ch3_service_path_start.png** (lighting): The right third of the corridor is a black slab (22 % of the frame below 8/255). The right-hand wall and the container get neither moonlight nor lamp light, and the torch is on but shows no visible hot spot anywhere down the path. Only the vines read, as silhouettes.
  - Likely cause: n/a
  - Suggested fix: Put a second wall lamp or battery bulkhead on the right-hand wall further down; it also adds a second reflection on the wet path. Alternatively let the moon graze the top of that wall. Also check that the torch hot spot is visible at corridor distances: at 20-25 m the 7200 cd beam gives only about 12-18 lux.
- **[minor] NightStormBlackout_aerial_sw.png** (water): From the air the storm island is a black-green mass (24 % of pixels below 8/255). The Titan Meadows lake is a perfectly round, perfectly flat black disc (10.8 ± 0.9 out of 255) that reads as a hole in the ground. The sea is flat teal (26 ± 3.6) with no storm whitecaps. The plaza is one flat grey rectangle, and the meadow trees are evenly spaced like stamps, which reads as savanna.
  - Likely cause: The lake is a Pool water surface (LookTerrainWater.BuildLake) under a black storm sky, with dense reeds on only about a third of its edge (LookVegetationDetails). The storm swell and foam on the sea do not show at this distance.
  - Suggested fix: Break up the lake outline (reeds all round, an irregular bank, the fallen trunks), let it mirror some haze, and raise ocean foam and whitecaps in the storm. Low priority: this is not a gameplay view.
- **[minor] NightCalm_plaza_eye.png** (geometry): The visitor centre behind the plaza is a plain two-storey box with a flat roof. Its glass curtain wall is a flat mid-grey (42 ± 6 out of 255): it does not reflect the warm lamps, and no lit lobby shows behind it, even with the power on. As the backdrop of the hero exterior it reads as a warehouse, not the park's flagship building.
  - Likely cause: n/a
  - Suggested fix: Let the lobby glow through the glass (lower the base alpha and reflection of GlassArchitectural, or add emissive interior cards), make the plaza lamps visible in the glass, and add a roof fascia, an entrance canopy light and a lit park sign, so the silhouette has a top and a focal point.
- **[minor] 17_ch4_valley_entry.png** (ui): In every route frame the first-person hand is a dark, lumpy glove silhouette across the lower-right quarter. The torch body is invisible apart from a glint on the lens, so it does not read as a guard holding a flashlight, and it is the largest black shape in the frame.
  - Likely cause: n/a
  - Suggested fix: Show the torch body with a rim highlight from its own spill or bounce light, and lighten the glove and sleeve base colour slightly (LookMaterials already fixed the glove once). Aim for the hand at about 15-25/255, not black.
- **[minor] 18_ch4_trex_reveal.png** (creatures): Neither creature beat frame contains a creature, even brightened three times. And in 15 there is no raptor in the junction 2.5 s after the paths_corner2 trigger. The '[THUNDER CRACKS OVERHEAD]' caption shows over an unchanged sky.
