# Review 1 — second opinion on segment `night_b` (score 4/10)

A second, sceptical critic looked at the same captures: which findings hold (with what was measured), which do not, and what the first critic missed.

## Kept (confirmed)

- **[blocker] 28_ending_3.png** (geometry): The last shot of the game is framed on the Coast Guard rescue boat (centre bottom, x≈515-630, y≈530-630), and the boat is a stack of untextured boxes: a dark box hull, a tan stepped box deckhouse, and a stick mast with a crossbar. It sits on a flat pale slab that sticks out past the hull on every side like a raft. There is no bow shape and no wake, and no beacon or nav light reads, so nothing tells the player this is a rescue boat. It reads as a blockout toy at the payoff of the whole game.
  - Fix: Cause: Zone4_TerrainJungleAndDawnCoastBuilder.cs lines 529-547 build it from CreateBox parts: EvacWakeFoam is a 32 x 0.18 x 9.6 m PlasterClean slab, and the hull, deckhouse, funnel and mast are all boxes. Its beacon is a 32-nit EmissiveAmber cube, and the 85,000 cd and 140,000 cd evac lights are invisible at dawn EV ~12. Assets/Art has no boat model. Options: (1) delete EvacWakeFoam, push the boat 1.5-2 km out into the dawn haze so only a silhouette reads, and give it a beacon bright enough to read at dawn exposure (thousands of nits). (2) Build a real procedural hull with a tapered bow mesh. (3) Cut the boat and end on the sun glint. In every case re-aim finalSeaCam (CameraDirector.PlayDawnCoastCraneShot, overlook + (3, 9.5, -3), which frames the boat 170 m away) so boxes are never seen up close.
  - Why it is real: At 5x zoom it is plainly a box hull, a stepped box deckhouse and a mast with a crossbar, on a flat light-tan plank wider than the hull. The mast top is dark (no beacon). The boat is the focal point of the final frame.
- **[major] 20_ch5_generator_running.png** (materials): Chain-link fence fabric turns into solid panels beyond the nearest span. In 20 and 21 the 4.6 m substation fences fill the background as opaque tan panels with a fine diamond print and brown blotches, so the substation looks walled in stained plywood. In 19 the west fence spans either side of the gate gap read as two blank tan buildings lit by bulkheads, and the north span reads as a concrete wall. In 27 only the nearest span on each side is see-through; every span beyond is a grey wall. In 26 the fence run left of the road through the gate is opaque while the panel above it is mesh.
  - Fix: Cause confirmed in WorldMaterialPalette.KeepAlphaCoverage(): it sets mipMapsPreserveCoverage = true with a 0.4 cutoff on chainlink_albedo, which has 32 diamonds per 1.8 m repeat. By mip 3-4 the wire and the holes average into one uniform value, and coverage preservation lifts it over the cutoff. Turn preserve-coverage off, or author the low mips so their alpha stays under 0.4, so distant fabric thins out and the posts and rails carry the silhouette. If some fabric must stay visible far off, fade it with distance (dither) rather than letting it go opaque.
  - Why it is real: A 2x zoom of 20 shows the diamond mesh printed on a fully opaque tan panel, with the jungle visible only above the fence top. 27 and 26 show the same material see-through up close and solid farther away.
- **[major] 22_ch5_green_route.png** (weather): The rain reads as snow or sleet in 19, 22, 23, 24, 26 and 27: short, fat white dashes and same-size dots spread evenly over the dark sky, far from any light. 22, 23 and 27 are the worst. Only 20 and 21 have streaks long and diagonal enough to read as wind-blown rain. The tropical storm looks like a winter night.
  - Fix: Cause in StormRainOcclusionController.ConfigureParticleSystems(): startSize is 0.012-0.02 m with velocityScale 0.022, which makes dashes about 0.24 m x 1.5 cm. minParticleSize 0.0026 holds every distant streak at 2 px or more, so far drops become uniform dots. MAT_RainStreaks uses alpha 0.7, _MaxNits 70 and _SkyLight 1, so drops lit only by the sky glow everywhere. Changes: set _SkyLight to about 0.3, _MaxNits to about 20 and alpha to about 0.35, so only lamp and beam cones show bright streaks. Set velocityScale to about 0.05 for longer streaks. Cull or strongly fade streaks beyond about 12-15 m instead of clamping them to 2 px. Add a distant rain-sheet layer (scrolling streak cards or a noise-masked local fog) for depth.
  - Why it is real: A 3x zoom of the sky in 22 shows mostly 2-3 px dots and 3-8 px dashes of equal brightness on dark blue, with no lamp nearby.
- **[major] 19_ch5_substation_dark.png** (materials): Puddle decals show as hard-edged light-grey parallelograms on the ground. In 19 there are two right of the hand (x≈806-943, y≈466-491 and x≈914-1034, y≈509-537). In 23 there is one on the road (x≈860-1000, y≈450-472). They read as primitive quads stuck to the floor.
  - Fix: Cause confirmed in LookWeather.ConfigurePuddleMaterial(): _NormalBlendSrc = 1 and _MaskBlendSrc = 1 (mask-map blue), but _MaskMap is null. As a result the ripple normal and the 0.96 smoothness cover the whole projector box. Set both to 0 (base-colour alpha) so the puddle shape in the atlas masks them. Mask the lightning emission the same way, or set _AffectEmission to 0. The problem is game-wide: every placed puddle (up to 400) and the builders' puddles share this material.
  - Why it is real: The zooms show straight-edged pale quads, each with the same ripple rings inside, on both the concrete pad (19) and the asphalt (23).
- **[major] 28_ending_1.png** (exposure): The first ending shot is overexposed toward the sun. The red channel is clipped on 61 % of the right half of the sky (green on 27 %) and on 18 % of the frame overall. The big cloud at upper right is a flat cream blob (mean RGB 244/220/163), and 50 % of the sun glint is clipped in all channels, so it reads as a white bar. ending_3 faces the same sun, with the sun in frame, and clips 0 %. So the cause is metering or adaptation timing, not the scene. This is the least severe of the majors, but 'nothing blown out' is an explicit acceptance criterion.
  - Fix: Switch to DawnCoast under a short fade or title card with SetPreset(DawnCoast, 0) and hold about 1 s, or lock the cutscene to a fixed exposure matching ending_3 for the first glide. Separately, the custom tonemapper (LookAtmosphereProfiles baseLook: TonemappingMode.Custom, shoulderStrength 0.75) lets bright yellows go flat in R well before they reach white. A longer shoulder or highlight desaturation would keep the cloud form.
  - Why it is real: Measured as above. To the eye, the right third is a flat glowing cream band with soft, shapeless clouds.
- **[major] 25_ch6_e0_gate_closed.png** (ui): The E-0 header, the target of the whole climax, is shredded (x≈470-620, y≈95-175, same in 26). 'SERVICE EXIT' is an unreadable spray of green pixels, and 'E-0' and the arrow are broken into fragments with holes.

Keep the letters just above scene white so they read instead of blooming.

  - Why not: LookFixtures.BollardCollar() already disables AuxGreenCap (cap.enabled = false) and adds its own Collar and Dome lenses. Those two lenses are the stacked discs, so the fix is to keep one lens. The neon-colour finding itself stands.

## Missed by the first critic

- **[minor] 19_ch5_substation_dark.png** (viewmodel): The first-person glove shows ghosting from the background. In 19 the floor conduit's dark band runs straight across the glove, although the hand is in front of it. At rows 537-556 the glove pixels drop from ~60 to a flat 17, exactly matching the band beside the hand, and they repeat its lighter stripe at rows 557-559. In 25 the yellow hazard stripes and the chain-link faintly show through the palm. In motion this reads as a smeared, see-through hand.
  - Fix: Likely cause (unverified): the viewmodel is drawn by a Custom Pass at CustomPassInjectionPoint.BeforePostProcess (ViewmodelCameraConfig.cs line 73) without motion vectors, so TAA and motion blur reuse the world's history and motion under the hand. Write motion vectors for the viewmodel pass, or exclude it from motion blur and TAA history. Check by panning with the light on.
- **[minor] NightStormBlackout_yard_eye.png** (lighting): The storm has a clear-sky moon. Crates cast crisp, dark moon shadows in yard_eye and 24, and the coast rail posts cast hard shadows on the asphalt in coast_eye, where the sky shows only a few wisps and a moon glow on the horizon. Under 'heavy rain haze' it reads as a calm moonlit night, which feeds the owner's 'it doesn't look right'.
  - Fix: In AtmosphereController KeyLook NightStormBlackout (19 lux, 29°, angularDiameter 0.9°, shadowDimmer 0.9), drop shadowDimmer to about 0.3-0.4 so the moon acts as diffuse light through the cloud deck. Keep crisp shadows for the lamps and the flashlight, and let lightning supply the hard moments.
