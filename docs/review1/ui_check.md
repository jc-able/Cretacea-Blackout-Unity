# Review 1 — second opinion on segment `ui` (score 6/10)

A second, sceptical critic looked at the same captures: which findings hold (with what was measured), which do not, and what the first critic missed.

## Kept (confirmed)

- **[major] journal_tab2.png** (Journal, Documents tab): The right detail card (x 530-1128, y 178-617) is one flat beige fill. It is the largest and brightest block on the screen, it is nearly empty, and it reads as a placeholder. The document reader's page is stained and textured, so the game shows two different 'paper' treatments. The body text is thin grey-brown Courier Prime Regular.
  - Fix: Collected entries: reuse the reader's paper texture, 9-sliced with some edge wear, with the body in CourierPrime-Bold at 24-26 ref px. Uncollected entries: use a dark PanelCard with a faint page outline and the hint in TextMuted, so the screen stays dark. The values are in JournalController.cs:549 (DocDetail colour 0.86, 0.80, 0.68, 0.97) and :558 (Mono, 22 px).
  - Why it is real: I sampled the empty part of the card (x 545-1120, y 300-600): it is a single colour, std-dev 0. The frame's mean luminance is 65/255, about 25%. The reader page in document_reader.png varies (std-dev 14-18).
- **[major] journal_tab2.png** (Journal copy): All five list rows read like debug output: '1. [UNCOLLECTED - Security Office Desk]' through '5. [UNCOLLECTED - Old Yard Foreman's Office]', in brackets and caps with hyphens. Every row has the same shape, so there is no hierarchy. The detail card says 'UNCOLLECTED INTEL / Locate this document at: ...'. Together with the flat card, the tab looks unfinished.
  - Fix: Make each row two lines: a muted 'NOT FOUND' (or a redacted bar) with a page or lock icon, then the location hint in small TextMuted. Change the detail to 'NOT YET RECOVERED' plus one hint line. Keep brackets out of UI copy (JournalController.cs:743 and :767).
  - Why it is real: All five rows show it. The string is built at JournalController.cs:743.

- **[major] journal_tab0-2.png** (HUD / horror pacing): Upgraded from minor. The objective group's alpha is set to 1 and never lowered.

## Dropped (not real, overstated, or a capture artefact)

- #16: in every modal capture the right-most pixel column shows the scene undimmed, a tan line on the end screen.
  - Why not: It cannot be pinned on the game. Every game_view capture (ui, route, rooms, intro) is a nearest-neighbour upscale of a 1155x696 editor Game view. Output column 1279 is the only one sampling source column 1154, and the overlay canvas stops one source pixel short of the camera image, which points to a fractional Game-view width. The free-camera 'presets' captures, rendered at native 1280x720, show nothing like it. Check once in a 1920x1080 player; I moved it under missed item 1.
- Part of #8: the CONTINUE button in the document reader 'has the same problem' as SAVE & BACK.
  - Why not: CONTINUE is the only control on that screen, so nothing competes for focus and a lit default button is correct.
- Part of #6: 'Throw Main TRANSFER Lever' mixes capitalisation.
  - Why not: It matches the lever's in-world TRANSFER label and the manual's 'Throw the TRANSFER lever'. The caps are deliberate emphasis, not a slip.

## Missed by the first critic

- **[major] *)** (Capture pipeline (review validity, not the game)): The frames are not native 1280x720. Each has exactly 125 duplicated columns (one every ~10.2 px) and 24 duplicated rows (one every 30 px), so each is a nearest-neighbour stretch of a 1155x696 (1.66:1) editor Game view. Everything is about 7% wider than it is tall: the round end-screen emblem measures 105x96 px. Glyph stems double or thin ('WAREHOUSE B' and 'FEED PREP DOCK' on the map). The 3D world in the route frames is stretched the same way. The UI was laid out on a 1855x1118 reference canvas, not 1920x1080. The pixel measurements behind #12, #13 and #16 were taken on these distorted frames. The free-camera 'presets' captures (exact 1280x720) have zero duplicates.
  - Fix: Set the Game view to a fixed 1920x1080 (or 1280x720) resolution instead of Free Aspect before 'capture game_view --source screen', or capture Play mode through a RenderTexture at the exact size, as the presets capture does. Re-shoot the UI and route sets, and tell the world-look reviewers that their frames are stretched too.
- **[minor] 12_ch3_feed_prep.png (HUD; outside this list, objective panel only)** (Objective copy): After the manual, the objective runs to three lines with ASCII arrows: 'Restore auxiliary power at Substation 3 (Paddock 4), then reach Exit E-0. Route: Feed Prep -> Loading Dock' / '-> Service Path.' The last line starts with an arrow. This is the objective the player carries out of chapter 2, so it is one of the most-read HUD strings. By contrast, route/14 ('Follow the concrete service path east to the Paddock 4 service gate.') shows the good pattern.
  - Fix: Give one leg at a time, for example 'Go through Feed Prep to the loading dock.', updated as each leg is reached, and keep the full route on the map. Fix the string at StoryDirector.cs:964. If arrows are wanted, add U+2192 to the Barlow SDF atlas rather than relying on HudController.SanitizeUiGlyphs, which turns arrows into '->'.
- **[minor] document_reader.png** (Copy and typography): A spaced hyphen stands in for a dash throughout: the subtitle 'The gates failed OPEN - the pens are open! Get out - main entrance, now!', the speaker tag 'RADIO - DANA (OPS)', the Systems footer 'SERVICE EXIT E-0 GATE - OFFLINE (RESTORE ...)', the transcript 'SECTION 7.4-7.5 - AUXILIARY CIRCUIT B', and the toast 'Master key acquired - unlocks Feed Prep'. The same screens use a real em dash elsewhere ('CHAPTER 2 — RECORDS ANNEX', 'EVACUATION PROCEDURES (1996) —'), and the fonts include it.
  - Fix: Replace the spaced hyphen with an em dash in the StoryDirector, JournalController and MenuController strings. Use an en dash for ranges (7.4–7.5) and a middle dot in speaker tags ('RADIO · DANA (OPS)').
- **[minor] settings.png** (Settings menu): DISPLAY & AUDIO offers only Brightness, FOV and Fullscreen. There is no resolution, quality preset, V-Sync or frame cap, no camera-shake option, and no key rebinding. A PC player looks for these first, RE7 and TLOU Part I ship all of them, and this game shakes the camera hard (StoryDirector.cs:905, trauma 0.82 on the ceiling collapse).
  - Fix: Add Resolution, Quality (Low/Medium/High), V-Sync, Camera Shake (0-100%) and a Controls page with rebinding. The right column has space below Gamepad Rumble.
