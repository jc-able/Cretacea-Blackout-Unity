# Review 1 — second opinion on segment `intro` (score 4/10)

A second, sceptical critic looked at the same captures: which findings hold (with what was measured), which do not, and what the first critic missed.

## Kept (confirmed)

- **[blocker] intro_01.png (every frame 01-20)** (capture / ui): Every intro frame is covered by the '1996 PRESERVE EVACUATION MANUAL' reader (DOCUMENT · PAGE 2, CONTINUE [E]). The paper (x 112-604) and the column (x 643-1166) hide about 75 % of each frame. The 0.86 backdrop plus the 0.55 vignette darkens what is left by 3.3-3.8 stops, and the HUD overlay group, which holds the title cards, is at alpha 0 while a modal is up. Composition, crowd, dinosaurs and the four title cards cannot be judged. This is a state the capture harness creates, not one a player can reach, so it blocks the review but is not a bug in the game.
  - Fix: In Tools/lookdev/capture_set.sh ('ui' part), call MenuController.CloseDocumentModal in a loop until GameState.Instance.IsDocumentOpen is false (DocumentPickup.ShowPageRecursive clears it after the last page), or capture 'intro' before 'ui'. Then recapture. The cadence is about 2.75 s per frame, so frames 07-15 are one shot later than their labels. A game-side change is optional hardening only: StartFromCheckpoint could hide an open reader without running its page chain and call SetDocumentOpen(false).
  - Why it is real: All 12 listed images (and 16, 18) show the reader. I measured the overlay against the one column it does not cover (x=1279): x=1278 passes only 0.07-0.10 of the world. Cause in code: capture_set.sh calls CloseDocumentModal once. doc_manual has several pages, so the close callback opens page 2. jump_chapter → StartFromCheckpoint clears the letterbox, subtitles and cutscene but never the reader. A player cannot get here, because MenuController.Update returns before the Pause handling while _documentModalRoot is active. What shows at the edges agrees with the critic's label shift: 07 is still the facade (WELCOME CRETACEA sign), 09 the viewing rail with a visitor, 13 the T. rex in the gallery, 15 the end of the dusk aerial.
- **[blocker] intro_17.png** (exposure / camera blocking): Shot 6, the '23:04' push-in on the Security Office west window, opens on a black frame. The column at x=1279 shows the true world: linear mean 0.001, max 0.002, 80 % of rows at or below sRGB 8, and that includes the sky rows. The centre strip, where the lit 3 m window should sit (about x 540-775 at 14 m, FOV 50), is black. The settled NightCalm preset captures have a dark-blue sky (top rows sRGB 6-28 / 16-52 / 19-80) and under 7 % near-black pixels, and intro_18 shows the lit office at a normal level. So the camera start is blocked by something (foliage or terrain), not just underexposed. Nothing in shot 6 fades the screen, and x=1279 lies outside all UI.
  - Fix: Add the shot-6 camera line to LookVegetation.CameraLines, e.g. (new Vector2(-89f,27f), new Vector2(-72f,28.5f), 7f). The list at lines 24-33 has no shot-6 entry, and the VC-wings footprint only starts at x=-74, so x -86..-74 is open to scatter. A Footprints entry (-92,-74,22,34) would also work. Rebuild. Also add an editor check that raycasts from each GlideCutsceneShot start to its look target and checks terrain height at the start, so this cannot come back. Check frames at about 46.5 s and 48 s: the window must read from the first frame. A warm wall-pack over the window (a LookLights rule, about 2700 K) would give the push-in a key light.
  - Why it is real: I saw an all-black frame. The uncovered edge column shows the black is in the world and not caused by UI. intro_18, one capture later, has a normal interior (edge mean 0.067). In StoryDirector.OpeningCutsceneRoutine, shot 6 only raises a title card and turns lightning on. I could not identify the occluder: MainWorld.unity is stored as binary.
- **[major] intro_16.png** (exposure / day-night transition): In the middle of shot 5b, where the park lights come on, the picture dips to near-black. Edge column: linear mean 0.003, against 0.081 in intro_15 and 0.067 in intro_18, so about 4.5-4.75 stops darker. The sky rows read sRGB (1,3,9): the dusk sky turns black before night arrives. With the reader removed, only a faint tree canopy and one small warm lamp remain at the edges.
  - Fix: AtmosphereController.BlendRoutine takes the 'dip' path for DuskTransition→NightCalm, because the lux ratio is 12.2 stops (>6) and the light direction changes by about 130°. Instead of scaling the key light (which also drives the PBR sky) to 0.0001 lux at k=0.5, fade the sun's lux down in log space to the moon's 24 lux while keeping its direction, swap direction at equal intensity, then settle on the moon. That gives no black and no pop, and the sky stays at a blue-hour level. Second-best option: start the NightCalm blend 2.25 s before the 5b→6 cut so the bottom lands on the cut, or make a deliberate short dip on that cut.
  - Why it is real: Frame 16 falls about 44 s in, which is 2.25 s into the 4.5 s NightCalm blend that starts with shot 5b: exactly the bottom of the dip. The critic's 'about 5 stops' is close; I measure about 4.5 on the uncovered column.
- **[major] intro_20.png (also intro_19)** (interior lighting / colour): The first room the player stands in has a cyan ceiling over warm walls. True edge values: ceiling sRGB (42,59,70) to (87,111,132), linear R:G:B about 0.5:1:1.45; walls (96,88,73), about 1.2:1:0.66. The filing cabinet has a neutral grey albedo (texture median sRGB 58,59,60) but renders navy (reconstructed 0.69:1:1.22), so the room's fill is the night sky, not its lamps. Frames 19 and 20 are about 3 s apart and identical, so this is not a transient before the indoor volume blends in. Corrections to the critic: Light_Security_Main is 3500 K, not 4000 K, and an indoor override already exists (Look_Indoor with IndoorDiffuse 0.42 and IndoorReflection 0.30, plus a LampBounce at BounceShare 0.45). It is too weak, so the job is to strengthen what exists, not add a new volume.
  - Fix: In LookAtmosphereProfiles, make the indoor dimming depend on the preset: at night about 0.1 indirect diffuse and 0.15 reflection, because night.indirectDiffuse is 1.5. Give Look_Indoor a neutral SMH and white balance so the night grade's blue shadow push (SMH shadows 0.90/1.0/1.12, WB -4) does not tint lit rooms. In LookLights, raise the bounce for Light_Security_Main or add a 3500 K CeilingWash in the office, so the ceiling sits 1-1.5 stops under the walls in a warm-neutral tone. Recheck the vc_offices reflection probe.
  - Why it is real: Visible at the top of both edge strips and in the uncovered column. The cabinet result rules out the ceiling-tile albedo as the explanation.
- **[major]  intro_07.png (also intro_06)** (colour / palette continuity): Upgraded from minor. Shot 2, the facade glide, reads sepia. With the reader removed, the hill behind the plaza comes out sRGB about (110-120, 96-104, 71-78): hue 37°, saturation 0.21, a tan/khaki slope with flat dark tree cut-outs and vertical banding. The sky near the top is cream (234,214,182) and the paving peach (210,180,138). The shots on either side have green/teal hills and blue skies: (82,121,156) in shot 1, (42,84,124) in shot 3. Confirm on a clean capture.

## Dropped (not real, overstated, or a capture artefact)

- #4 sub-claim: the troffers sit on the ceiling as flat, clipped white slabs
  - Why not: The reconstruction shows the prismatic lens grid on every troffer. Clipping (about 8 % of the edge column) is limited to the emitters themselves, which is normal for a lit fixture at night exposure. The flaw is the cyan ceiling around them, not the fixtures.
- #3 fix option: keep a floor of at least 0.25 on the dimmer
  - Why not: At k=0.5 the dip swaps the key from the sun (115,000 lux) to the moon (24 lux). With a 0.25 floor the light would jump from about 28,750 lux of sun to 6 lux of moon at the swap: a hard pop, not a fix. Fade the sun's intensity down to the moon's level before swapping direction instead.
- #1 framing: the owner's first minute is a spoiler page that needs a StoryDirector fix
  - Why not: Only the capture script reaches this state. While the reader is open, MenuController.Update returns before Pause, so a player cannot jump chapters with it up, and the intro starts from New Game. The finding stands as a review blocker; the game-side change is optional hardening.

## Missed by the first critic

- **[major] intro_11.png (also intro_10, intro_12, intro_13)** (interior lighting / crushed blacks): The viewing gallery interior in the T. rex beat (shot 4) is crushed. At the uncovered edge, the gallery ceiling reads sRGB (10-14, 11-15, 3-6), a dead olive-black, while sunlit foliage through the glass clips at (255,229,122). In the reconstructed strips, 85-100 % of the top band (rows 62-200, about the top quarter of the picture) is below sRGB 12, and 32-46 % of all edge pixels are. That band merges with the 61 px letterbox, so the frame looks top-heavy, and the side walls are mud. Cause in code: the six gallery downlights (Light_Gallery_Down_0..5, Zone1_VisitorCenterBuilder.BuildPlazaAndViewingGallery) are in LightGroupId.Plaza, which OpeningCutsceneRoutine switches off until shot 5b. They also have no LookLights rule and no ceiling wash. On top of that, AtmosphereController's enclosure test raises Look_Indoor (sky diffuse ×0.42, reflections ×0.30) inside the gallery in broad daylight.
  - Fix: Keep the gallery lit in the afternoon: move Light_Gallery_Down_* to VCMain or turn them on in the intro, add a LookLights rule, and add a warm-neutral fill from the glass wall (a CeilingWash or a wide shadowless area light facing in, about 10-15 % of the exterior level) so the ceiling, visitors and mullions read as shapes. Scale Look_Indoor's strength by preset (about 1.0, i.e. no dimming, in GoldenDay, where a 24 m glass wall should flood the room).
- **[minor] pause.png, settings, journal)** (ui / letterbox): At 1280x720 the letterbox bars and the full-screen modal backdrops stop 1 px short of the right edge, and the screen fade probably does too, since it is on the same canvas. Column x=1279 shows the raw world: (255,255,255) inside the top letterbox bar in intro_18, sky (64,104,146) inside it in intro_02, and about 4x the brightness of x=1278 under every modal capture. During the cinematic this is a thin bright line beside the black bars, and black fades are never fully black.
  - Fix: Inflate the full-screen panels by a couple of pixels (offsetMin (-2,-2), offsetMax (2,2)) in HudController.BuildLetterboxBars, the fade panel and MenuController.CreateBackdrop. Verify at 1920x1080 and 4K, since the gap is a canvas rounding effect and depends on resolution.
- **[minor] intro_01.png (frames 01-18)** (ui / cinematic composition): The '[Hold Space] Skip Intro' plate is shown at 0.95 alpha from the first frame to the end of the 52 s cinematic (HudController.OnCutsceneSkipPromptChanged, raised once at intro start). AAA cinematics (RE7, TLOU) show a skip prompt only after a button press. Here a permanent framed plate sits in the corner of the letterbox over the whole opening.
  - Fix: Show the skip plate on the first key or button press (or for the first 3 s only), then fade it out after about 3 s without input.
