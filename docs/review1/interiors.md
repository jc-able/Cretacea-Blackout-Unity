# Review 1 (Nov 20, before the lead's look fixes in 07fd73d) — segment interiors

Images: Screenshots/review1/ (same camera positions as Tools/lookdev/capture_set.sh and route_test.py).

### Critic segment `interiors` (score 4/10)

Not yet. The blackout frames have real horror atmosphere: the moonlit atrium with mullion shadows, the beam reaching down the corridor, troffers that truly die, CCTV static. The power-on rooms fail the brief: a black band under every ceiling, a sepia break room and gift shop, a lobby that already looks blacked out, and oversized, nearly empty rooms with placeholder set pieces (the collapse box, black-grid fixtures, flat black doorways, milky control-room glass). Next to Alien: Isolation or RE7 this reads as a well-lit prototype, not a shipped interior.

- **[blocker] breakroom_on.png** (lighting): Black band under every lit ceiling. In all power-on rooms the top 0.5-1 m of each wall is near-black while the ceiling above is lit, so the ceiling seems to float over a dark gap. breakroom_on (y 240-390): band luma 13 against 36 for the ceiling and 68 for the wall below. giftshop_on: 22 against 80/104, with a wavy edge. office_on left wall: 27 and blue-tinted (RGB 21/28/32) against 115 on the wall. lockers_on: navy 37 against 73-80. corridor_on: hard dark wedges on the near walls (71 against 129). 03: above the windows. The band is also in the game's first frame (route/01).
  - Likely cause: LookLights.cs Apply (line 177) turns every Throw.Down lamp into a spot with Wide = 164 deg (line 53) and inner = 0.94 x outer = 154 deg, hung 7 cm under the ceiling (LookFixtures.cs line 143). The cone edge drops only 0.14 m per metre, so the top of each wall gets no direct light, and the troffer grid scallops the edge. Up there only two things arrive. One is the under-floor Bounce (BounceShare 0.45, 1.4 m below the floor, lines 222-223), at a grazing angle: about 9 lux against about 60 on the mid-wall. The other is the sky ambient indoors (Look_Indoor IndoorDiffuse 0.42, LookAtmosphereProfiles.cs line 126), which reads blue, and night.smhShadows (0.90, 1.0, 1.12) at line 231 pushes it bluer. The same 0.42 Indirect Lighting Controller multiplier also scales the SSGI (enabled at line 394) that should fill the band.
  - Suggested fix: Make troffers emit like troffers: HDRP rectangle area lights sized to the lens (0.6 x 1.2 m), or at minimum spotAngle 179 with innerSpotAngle about 0.5 x outer, so the light falls off like a cosine instead of cutting off. Give companion lamps their share of the bounce. Stop scaling SSGI indoors: bake APV for the Visitor Center (the High Fidelity asset already uses Probe Volumes; turn on supportProbeVolumeScenarios and bake PowerOn and Blackout scenarios), and make Look_Indoor remove only the sky term. Keep shadows neutral indoors. Accept when the top of every wall is at least 60% of the mid-wall and not blue.
- **[major] records_dark.png** (geometry): Rooms are oversized and nearly empty, so they read as test chambers, not a 1990s park building. Security office 14 x 12 m: one desk, a chair, one locker stack, a corkboard of blank notes. Locker room 10 x 12 m: one locker row and two benches. Break room 22 x 13 m: three tables. Gift shop 22 x 20 m: one 2 m back shelf in view, stocked with flat coloured boxes. Records annex 18 x 12 m: one desk, one cabinet, one chair, in an objective room that should be stacks of files. Almost nothing small is in view: door frames, switches, outlets, smoke detectors, exit signs, pipes, grime.
  - Likely cause: Zone1_VisitorCenterBuilder.cs BuildBoxRoomShell sizes (lines 66-70, 634, 695) are 3-6x real rooms, and the Dress* passes are light. WallShelf and the display tables stock Box() primitives in mVendRed, mBinderBlue and similar colours.
  - Suggested fix: Dress to real density without moving colliders, or add non-structural partitions. Office: CCTV console with a CRT bank, filing cabinets, key box, radio charger rack, park map or whiteboard, water cooler. Records: rows of steel shelving with archive boxes and labelled drawers. Break room: counter, sink, fridge, microwave, coffee machine, a cluttered notice board. Gift shop: modelled plush, shirts and books on the gondolas. Every room: grime and leak decals (DecalGrimeLeak) along skirtings and doors, and ceiling detail (smoke detectors, sprinklers, vents).
- **[major] breakroom_on.png** (colour): The break room and gift shop (giftshop_on.png) are sepia: brown-orange monochrome with orange-glowing panels and brown ceilings. The red/blue ratio over the frame is 1.71 and 1.69, against 1.16 in the security office; the break-room floor is RGB 152/116/78. The same troffer is blue-white in the office, lockers and control room. This is the owner's 'colours are off' again.
  - Likely cause: LookLights.Zone1.cs sets ^Light_Break_Main to 3600 K (line 58) and ^Light_GiftShop to 3400 K (line 51). LookFixtures.LensFor (lines 811-813) gives every lamp under 4000 K the MAT_Lens_WarmWhite lens (1.0, 0.86, 0.66) and every lamp at or above 4000 K MAT_Lens_CoolWhite (0.86, 0.93, 1.0). That cool tint is about 8000 K over 4000-4500 K lamps, and it is also why the troffers in office_on, lockers_on and 03 have cyan halos.
  - Suggested fix: Use one fluorescent colour for every troffer (4000-4300 K; the bible allows cool white for tubes). Derive each lens colour from Mathf.CorrelatedColorTemperatureToRGB(lamp kelvin) instead of the two fixed tints. Put the warmth in practicals instead: vending-machine glow, a warm pendant over the checkout, display-case lights.
- **[major] lobby_on.png** (lighting): With the power on, the lobby already looks blacked out. Walls and glass sit at luma 22-36 in navy; the only lit things are a floor pool (about 105) and the fossil uplight. No fixture is in view, and the banners, park logo and mezzanine are unlit. When the power dies (lobby_dark) the atrium hardly changes, so the blackout loses its punch in the showpiece room.
  - Likely cause: Two Light_Lobby_Main spots (LookLights.cs line 71: 9500 cd, 118 deg; builder lines 1119-1120, 9.8 m up) light a 38 x 51 m atrium 11 m high. The cones cover about 16 m of floor radius, so the walls get almost nothing.
  - Suggested fix: Add wall grazers along the long walls and under the mezzanine fascia, accent spots on Lobby_Banner_RexE/RaptorE and Lobby_ParkLogoN, lit task lights on the reception desk and a glowing welcome sign. Make the high-bay or pendant bodies visible. Aim for 60-110 lux on the walls so the lights going out is a visible event.
- **[major] office_dark.png** (materials): Dead fixtures read as black wire grids. In every blackout room the switched-off troffers show as black frames with a black cross grid against the lit ceiling: office_dark x 320-515, y 150-220; breakroom_dark x 527-652, y 193-212; records_dark x 495-660, y 150-220; 08 x 592-688, y 143-198; also 12. The corridor fixtures turn into flat dark squares (corridor_dark x 625-655, y 245-265; corridor_dark_noflash x 610-670, y 110-180).
  - Likely cause: LookFixtures.PanelAt (line 153) builds the frame from DarkPaintedMetal, 4 cm larger than the lens, with BrushedSteel louvres and spine; the metal goes black when there is nothing bright to reflect. The corridor diffusers (ExistingTroffer) keep the palette emissive's dark base colour instead of the 0.85 opal that CustomLens sets.
  - Suggested fix: Use a white enamel frame and louvres (dielectric, albedo about 0.75, as a new palette surface). Give every lens the 0.85 opal base, the corridor's included, so a dead fixture reads as a pale panel in the beam.
- **[major] 12_ch3_feed_prep.png** (exposure): The flashlight hot spot clips to a white disc with a bloom halo on the white tile (centre about x 560-740, y 300-440). The core is luma 254, with 64% of a 100 x 80 px area at 255, and the grout and tile texture vanish. In office_dark the hot spot on the ceiling corner is 40% clipped. This is exactly the 'white disc' the bible rules out.
  - Likely cause: FlashlightController.cs line 154: HotSpotMaxLux = 95 lux on any surface; its own comment says 'white tile at 7 m still clipped'. Storm exposure is capped at EV 4.7 (LookAtmosphereProfiles.cs line 239). 95 lux on a 0.8-albedo tile is about 24 nits, which is 0.8-2.5 x white between EV 4.7 and 3.0.
  - Suggested fix: Make the cap depend on the surface's albedo: about 30 lux on white tile, using base-colour luminance cached per hit material. Or let histogram exposure reach about EV 6 when the beam fills the view. Also soften the night/storm tonemap shoulder so the hot spot rolls off and the tile pattern survives.
- **[major] 03_ch1_control_room_lit.png** (materials): The control-room windows are a lit milky film. All five panes are a uniform pale teal (luma 73-76, RGB 65/80/77), as bright as the ceiling (71) and the wall above them (65). The storm outside is washed out and the glass looks frosted; a window onto the night should be the darkest thing in a lit room.
  - Likely cause: WorldMaterialPalette.BuildGlassMaterial (lines 354, 367): alpha-blended HDRP Lit with _BaseColor (0.70, 0.82, 0.84, 0.14), i.e. a bright diffuse layer at 14% coverage that the room lamps light up. Every window in the game uses this material.
  - Suggested fix: Use Premultiply blend (it keeps the specular) with a base colour near 0.02 and low alpha, so only reflections and the exterior show. Keep the tint very slight. Re-check every window against a dark exterior.
- **[major] 03_ch1_control_room_lit.png** (story): All six desk monitors are black (luma about 20) in a powered control room, while the radio subtitle tells you to log the camera feeds from this room. A dead control room with the lights on breaks belief.
  - Likely cause: n/a
  - Suggested fix: Give the screens emissive feed or status images on VCMain (cyan per the palette, 30-45 nits) with camera-feed textures. Switch them to static or off when the power dies, as the office wall monitors already do.
- **[major] records_dark.png** (lighting): Light leaks through the desk. An amber pool lies on the floor under the records desk (x 410-500, y 545-565; luma 145, RGB 199/137/71), twice as bright as the beam-lit floor beside it (72), although the beacon stands on top of the desk.
  - Likely cause: LookLights.cs line 78: ^Light_Records_ManualBeacon is Shadow.None, and LookFixtures.DeskBeacon puts the lamp 0.26 m above the desk top (line 566).
  - Suggested fix: Set Shadow.Low (cached 256) on that rule, or use a shadow-casting cone aimed up or sideways, so the desk blocks the floor beneath it.
- **[major] 08_ch1_staff_exit_collapse.png** (geometry): The collapse is one clean tilted concrete box (x 530-745, y 300-450) with straight edges, even texture and two rebar rods behind it, on a spotless floor. There is no rubble, broken ceiling grid, fallen tiles, hanging cable or duct, and no dust, so a key story beat reads as a placeholder.
  - Likely cause: Zone1_VisitorCenterBuilder.cs line 685: CreateBox 'FallenSlab' (2.8 x 2.3 x 3.6 m) sits over the debris_pile model placed at the same spot on line 684 and hides it.
  - Suggested fix: Remove the box, or shrink it to a hidden collider so the route stays blocked, and let the debris_pile mesh be the visible mass. Add fractured slab chunks, fallen ceiling tiles, a bent troffer hanging from the hole, dangling cables, a fan of dust decal on the floor and dust in the beam.
- **[major] corridor_dark_noflash.png** (lighting): With the flashlight off, the near walls are crushed: the right wall is about RGB 2/5/5, the left about luma 11, and 46% of the frame is below 6/255. The warm battery pools down the hall sit at luma 83-94 as islands with nothing around them. The right half of the frame is a featureless black-brown gradient, which fails both 'dark but readable' and 'nothing crushed to black'.
  - Likely cause: LookLights.cs line 217 gives a lamp a Bounce only if it has no colour filter and casts shadows. The _Batt rule (LookLights.Zone1.cs line 38) is Shadow.None, so battery pools bounce nothing back onto the walls. Indoor ambient is 0.42 x an almost black storm sky.
  - Suggested fix: Give the warm _Batt units a bounce of about 0.2-0.25 share, and keep the red beacons without one, so walls 4-6 m from a pool sit at about 3-6 lux. With the flashlight off, near walls should stay above about luma 15.
- **[minor] corridor_dark_noflash.png** (lighting): The warm blackout pools start from bare wall. At their bright apex (about x 695, y 312 and x 580, y 335) there is no lamp body and no glowing lens, so the light comes from nowhere. The warm washes on the upper-left wall in office_dark and the left wall in records_dark have the same hard cone edge and no visible unit.
  - Likely cause: Battery units are straight-down 150 deg spots (the _Batt rule with Throw.Down). In the corridor, LookFixtures relies on a builder-placed beacon body, and nothing is visible at the light.
  - Suggested fix: Put a twin-head emergency unit with a glowing lens at each pool's apex, and aim the heads along the corridor instead of straight down.
- **[minor] corridor_on.png** (lighting): Lit fixtures clip into featureless shapes. The nearest corridor fixture is a round white orb with a halo (x 620-660, y 235-275) instead of a rectangular lens. The troffer lenses in office_on, lockers_on and 03 are 81-99% at 255 with cyan fringes, and their louvres do not read.
  - Likely cause: The 25-nit lens (LookFixtures.cs line 804) is about 1.3-2 x scene white at EV 4 to 3.3. Bloom threshold is 0, indoor ceiling lamps keep a volumetric dimmer of 0.12-0.15 only 7 cm under their own lens, and the lens has the cool tint.
  - Suggested fix: Lower the lens to 12-18 nits or raise the bloom threshold to about 1. Set volumetric to 0 on indoor ceiling lamps. Derive the lens colour from the lamp's kelvin.
- **[minor] 08_ch1_staff_exit_collapse.png** (colour): Under the beam the staff exit becomes a lime-green tunnel: the green walls are saturated (RGB 68/83/52 on the left), and the crack and rust pattern is oversized and cartoonish. The dado is green on the left wall and blue-grey on the right.
  - Likely cause: n/a
  - Suggested fix: Desaturate the PlasterPeeling90s tint by about 30% toward an institutional sage, tile the cracks at real scale, and paint the dado the same on both walls.
- **[minor] 12_ch3_feed_prep.png** (geometry): Both doorways are flat black rectangles cut into the tile (x 272-362 and 903-993; luma about 5), with no frame, no door leaf and no depth cue.
  - Likely cause: n/a
  - Suggested fix: Add architraves and a door leaf standing ajar, plus a faint light or a visible surface beyond, so they read as openings.
- **[minor] lobby_on.png** (geometry): A blank white-and-blue box stands between the T. rex's feet (x 622-655, y 303-335), right at the hero prop's focal point. It reads as a placeholder.
  - Likely cause: n/a
  - Suggested fix: Replace it with a real exhibit plaque with text and a graphic, or remove it.
- **[minor] breakroom_dark.png** (weather): Bright dust specks hang in unlit air near the dark ceiling (for example x 583, y 130 and x 780, y 130; more in office_dark and corridor_dark). They read as stars or snow, not dust in a beam.
  - Likely cause: n/a
  - Suggested fix: Make motes visible only where light hits them (fade by beam or volumetric light), so they glitter in the flashlight and vanish in the dark.
