# Review 1 (Nov 20, before the lead's look fixes in 07fd73d) — segment intro

Images: Screenshots/review1/ (same camera positions as Tools/lookdev/capture_set.sh and route_test.py).

### Critic segment `intro` (score 3/10)

No. This capture can't show an amazing intro: all 20 frames are covered by a page-2 Evacuation Manual reader that the capture script left open, and that reader also blocks every title card. Around its edges the daylight looks natural (blue sky, green jungle hills, no white-out from day to dusk, amber dusk light with teal-leaning shade). But the dusk-to-night beat drops into two near-black frames: 16 is the sun-to-moon dip in the middle of a shot, and 17, the '23:04' window push-in, renders black. The first gameplay room also has a blue ceiling over warm walls.

- **[blocker] intro_01.png (every frame 01-20)** (ui): Every intro frame is covered by the '1996 PRESERVE EVACUATION MANUAL' reader on DOCUMENT · PAGE 2, with a CONTINUE [E] button. The paper scan (x 112-604) and the reading column (x 643-1166, y 45-675) hide about 75 % of each frame. The reader's 0.86 backdrop plus vignette darkens the rest by about 3 stops and draws over the HOLD SPACE Skip Intro box (its text reaches only 81/255); the column's bottom-right corner overlaps that box. All four title cards are suppressed: 'CRETACEA PARK / Isla Tormenta | 5:42 PM', 'Closing time', '23:04' and '23:05 Night Shift'. So composition, crowd, dinosaurs and title cards cannot be judged, and as captured the owner's first minute is a spoiler page (Master Key, route to E-0).
  - Likely cause: The capture harness plus a missing reset in the game. In Tools/lookdev/capture_set.sh the 'ui' part opens doc_manual and calls MenuController.CloseDocumentModal() once (line 62). CloseDocumentModal runs the onClosed callback (MenuController.cs:322), which is DocumentPickup.ShowPageRecursive(pageIndex + 1) (DocumentPickup.cs:146), so page 2 opens and stays open. The 'intro' part then runs jump_chapter Intro. Neither CretaceaWorldAssembler.JumpChapterInPlayMode nor the 'P0-5' reset in StoryDirector.StartFromCheckpoint (StoryDirector.cs:214) closes the document modal. HudController.OnTitleCardRequested returns early while _suppressedByModal is set (HudController.cs:968).
  - Suggested fix: In capture_set.sh, after the document_reader shot, call CloseDocumentModal in a loop until GameState.Instance.IsDocumentOpen is false (or capture 'intro' first, or restart Play mode before it). In the game, make StoryDirector.StartFromCheckpoint close any open reader without running its page chain: clear MenuController._docModalOnClosed, hide _documentModalRoot, call SetDocumentOpen(false) and SetModalSuppressed(false). Recapture at the real cadence: sleep 2.4 s plus the capture comes to about 2.75 s per frame, so the segment labels are one shot late from 07 to 15. Then review composition, crowd, dinosaurs and the four title cards again.
- **[blocker] intro_17.png** (exposure): The start of shot 6, the '23:04' push-in on the Security Office west window, is a black frame. Every visible pixel is black, including the centre column (x 604-643) where the 3 m lit window should sit (about x 575-775 at 14 m with FOV 50). With the reader's darkening removed, the frame sits about 6 stops below mid-grey (mean 0.002, p99 0.005 linear). intro_18, one capture later, shows the lit office at a normal level. Nothing in shot 6 fades the screen (no RaiseScreenFade), so this black is not a scripted fade.
  - Likely cause: Most likely the lens starts behind or inside jungle. The shot-6 line (-86, 3.2, 27) to (-73.8, 1.85, 28.4) (StoryDirector.cs:545) is missing from LookVegetation.CameraLines (LookVegetation.cs:24, which lists only shots 1-5 and the dawn ending). It also lies outside every Footprint: the 'Visitor Center wings' rectangle starts at x -74 (line 43). So hill jungle and ground cover can stand between the camera and the window. The west facade also has no exterior lamp, so moonlight is the only light on it.
  - Suggested fix: Add (new Vector2(-89f, 27f), new Vector2(-72f, 28.5f), 7f) to CameraLines, or a footprint x -92..-74, z 22..34, and rebuild. Put a warm wall-pack (about 2700 K, with rain visible in its cone) over the window through a LookLights rule, so the push-in has a key light and a readable silhouette. Check frames at about 46.5 s and 48 s: the window must be in shot from the first frame.
- **[major] intro_16.png** (exposure): In the middle of the dusk-to-night aerial (shot 5b, where the park lights come on) the image dips to near-black. It is about 5 stops darker than intro_15 and intro_18 (compensated mean 0.005 against 0.08-0.09 linear). Plaza, roofs and jungle sink into black and only one warm lamp pool reads. The beat should show blue hour with lamps popping on; instead it plays as a black hole in the cinematic.
  - Likely cause: In AtmosphereController.BlendRoutine, DuskTransition to NightCalm swaps sun for moon, so dip is true (line 569). dimmer = SmoothStep(|k-0.5|*2) (line 599) then takes keyDirectionalLight.intensity to about 0 at the midpoint (line 651), and the sky and clouds take their brightness from that light. StoryDirector starts the 4.5 s NightCalm blend (line 534) together with the 4.5 s shot-5b glide, so the dip bottom lands 2.25 s into the shot. At that moment the blended exposure floor is still about EV 5.15, halfway between the dusk and night evMin of 7.0 and 3.3 in LookAtmosphereProfiles.
  - Suggested fix: Hide the swap. Either start the NightCalm blend about 2.25 s before the 5b-to-6 cut so the dip bottom sits on the cut (or make it a deliberate 0.3-0.5 s dip to black on that cut), or keep a floor on the dimmer (at least 0.25) and hold the sky at a blue-hour level through the swap. During the dip, let the exposure open to about EV 2.5 so the lamps switching on and the silhouettes read.
- **[major] intro_20.png** (lighting): In the first gameplay frame (also 19) the office ceiling reads blue: raw sRGB about (28, 37, 44) between the troffers, above warm beige walls at about (38, 33, 24). Night-sky ambient lights the ceiling instead of the room's 4000 K fluorescents, so the first room the player stands in mixes moonlight into a lit interior. The troffers sit on it as flat, clipped white slabs. This breaks the bible's rule that artificial light is warm or neutral indoors and only nature is blue.
  - Likely cause: The office troffers are Throw.Down spots (LookLights.Zone1.cs lines 28-29: 950-1000 cd, 4000 K), so the ceiling gets no direct light. What reaches it is the blue sky ambient or probe, and the bounce from the main lamp is too weak to warm it.
  - Suggested fix: Indoors, lower the indirect diffuse that comes from the sky (an indoor Volume override for the rooms), and give the main troffer a warm-neutral up-light or a stronger LampBounce (about 10-15 % of its intensity). The ceiling should sit about 1-1.5 stops under the walls in a neutral 4000 K tone. Also check that the 'vc_offices' reflection probe was not captured with the blue exterior.
- **[minor] intro_07.png** (colour): The plaza glide (shot 2, also intro_05) leans sepia. Shade is warm-yellow (shade R:G:B about 1.19:1:0.60 in 07 and 1.0:1:0.72 in 05), the sky near the horizon is grey-lavender, and the hill behind the plaza (left edge) is pale tan with flat dark tree cards. Next to shot 1's blue sky and green hills this cut reads dusty, the opposite of GoldenDay's 'blue shade, saturated tropical greens'. Only the edge strips are visible, so confirm on a clean capture.
  - Likely cause: Likely backlighting into the 27° sun with weak, warm sky fill in the shade. Possibly also the far-hill impostors: LookVegetationImpostors builds crossed quads that carry albedo only, so they would light flat and tan when backlit.
  - Suggested fix: In the GoldenDay profile (LookAtmosphereProfiles), raise the sky/ambient fill so shade goes blue-teal, or set the white balance 300-500 K cooler for this backlit direction. Give the impostors a translucency or normal term, or draw mesh trees out to this distance, so the backlit hill reads green rather than tan.
