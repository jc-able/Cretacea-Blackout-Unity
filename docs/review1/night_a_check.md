# Review 1 — second opinion on segment `night_a` (score 5/10)

A second, sceptical critic looked at the same captures: which findings hold (with what was measured), which do not, and what the first critic missed.

## Kept (confirmed)

- **[major] 13_ch3_loading_dock.png** (exposure): Downgraded from blocker. The dock is murky even with the flashlight on. Median 8.6/255, 48 % of pixels below 8 and 67 % below 16. The canopy underside is about 6/255 and the truck averages about 10/255. Only the moonlit doorway, the torch spot on the pillar and the dumpster read clearly; the floor in front of the player and the ceiling are black. It is the darkest route frame by far: the next darkest is 14, with a median of 20.7. It fails the bible's 'readable shadows' rule, but it is not a black screen.
  - Fix: Light the view the player arrives into. The only battery unit, BatteryLight 'Dock' at (8.6, 3.35, -13.6) in Zone1_VisitorCenterBuilder, is by the roller door behind the camera. Add a second Light_Dock_Batt unit on the east canopy column facing west (existing 520 cd rule in LookLights.Zone1), or run Light_Dock_Main at about 30 % emergency power. Aim for about 35 lux on the pad and the truck. Then check the meter: histogramPercentages (40, 88) with CenterWeighted metering (LookAtmosphereProfiles.BuildProfile) lets the bright doorway and the pillar hot spot in the centre set the exposure; try (40, 78) for the night presets. Target: under 10 % of pixels below 8/255 in this view.
  - Why it is real: I measured the same numbers. At 1:1 the truck is a dim grey-blue outline with its red stripe. Brightened 5x in linear, the whole set appears: truck, plank ceiling, wet asphalt, pallet, drum, jerrycan. So it is built, just unlit. The player can still find the way, because the exit doorway is the brightest area.
- **[major] NightStormBlackout_meadow_eye.png** (colour): The night reads as saturated teal, not moonlit blue. In dark and mid tones, red is only 17-27 % of blue: storm meadow 0.17, valley entry (17) 0.27, T. rex reveal (18) 0.23. Storm grass is (4,17,17), the storm sky (3,17,23), the valley-entry grass (5,17,17) and the aerial sea (2,30,44). Next to the sodium pools the palette turns teal-and-orange, and the meadow and jungle read as teal murk. This is the owner's 'colours are off' again, in a new hue.
  - Fix: The cause is in code: several settings each add green or remove red. Night tint -1 and storm tint -2 in LookAtmosphereProfiles push toward green (negative tint means green in Unity's white balance). smhShadows (0.90,1,1.12) and (0.86,1,1.14) strip red from the shadows. The moon filter in AtmosphereController.GetKeyLook, (0.80,0.90,1.0) and (0.76,0.88,1.0), has more green than red. The night horizonTint (0.70,0.90,1.0) is cyan. The first critic's values fit: tint +2 to +4, shadows about (0.95,0.97,1.08), moon filter about (0.86,0.89,1.0) at about 6500 K, sky tints about (0.80,0.86,1.0), storm saturation about -10. Target: red at 40-60 % of blue in the dark tones.
  - Why it is real: I measured the frame: storm meadow grass is (3.7,17.2,17.0), and red sits near zero across the dark tones. On screen, the storm meadow and the aerial read as a teal wash. Frames 17 and 18 show the same cast in the grass and the sky.
- **[minor] NightStormBlackout_plaza_eye.png** (weather): Downgraded from major. In the free-camera storm frames the storm barely shows. Below the skyline, the storm plaza differs from the calm plaza by only 4.9/255 on average, and the far hill is just as crisp. The sky is a flat dark gradient (3,14,20) with one cloud rim lit electric blue (6,33,57) that looks like a hole. The storm meadow and valley share that sky. In play, though, heavy rain and valley mist sell the storm (frames 17 and 18). So this matters for long views, such as the plaza seen from the lobby, more than for the route.
  - Fix: The haze stays dark because fog colorMode is SkyColor with tint x3 (LookAtmosphereProfiles). Under a near-black storm sky, the shorter meanFreePath (110 m) adds a dark veil, not a glowing one. For the storm, use a constant dim blue-grey fog colour (or a storm-only higher tint), and raise fogDepthExtent from 150 m toward about 400 m, so the far hill and treeline stand as silhouettes in front of haze. Lower cloudSunDimmer from 1.0 to about 0.4 and desaturate cloudScatterTint, so the moon stops lighting one cloud rim electric blue.
  - Why it is real: I diffed the two plaza captures pixel by pixel. A crop of the storm meadow sky shows one blue-rimmed wisp on an otherwise featureless sky. Frames 17 and 18 have dense rain streaks and softened far trees.
- **[major] NightCalm_plaza_eye.png** (materials): The plaza paving reads bone dry in both night presets (wetness 0.75 calm, 1.0 storm): matte tiles, no puddles, and no reflection of any lamp, bollard or the lit sign. The service paths in 14-17 do look wet. The cause is in code, so the plaza is dry in play too, including on the plaza CCTV feed and through the lobby glass.
  - Fix: Two causes. (1) LookWeather.IsPuddleSurface only accepts material names containing Asphalt, ConcreteCracked, ConcreteExterior, PavingStone, GravelGround, MudGround or ConcreteFloorWet. The plaza material is MAT_Z1_PlazaPaving, so the plaza gets no puddle decals at all: add 'PlazaPaving' or rename the material. (2) MAT_Z1_PlazaPaving has SmoothnessRemap 0.25-0.86 (Zone1_VisitorCenterBuilder). WetnessDriver raises the remap max only to max(0.86, wetSmoothnessMax 0.7), which changes nothing, and lifts the min by just wetSmoothnessMinRise 0.05. So wet looks the same as dry. For up-facing slabs, raise the rough end by about 0.25-0.35. Once the lamp lenses glow (next finding), screen-space reflections will have something to streak.
  - Why it is real: In both plaza frames the tiles under the pools are uniformly matte: the variation is the tile pattern, not sheen. No lamp head, bollard or sign is mirrored anywhere on the paving.
- **[major] NightCalm_plaza_eye.png** (lighting): Every plaza lamp head is dark (the hero head's underside is about (9,15,20)). The light's volumetric cone starts in mid-air under the arm, about 0.8 m from the pole. The pole just below the arm shows hot orange flecks (mean (95,83,62), peaks near white). In the storm plaza the same cone fans across the sky like a searchlight. One correction to the first critic: the hero pool does fall off, from about 130 luma at centre-right to about 50 at the left edge. Only the pools of the far posts merge into one lit strip.
  - Fix: LookFixtures.Build sends only ^Light_PathLamp through PathLamp() (which adds a lens and moves the light under the head). ^Light_PlazaLamp matches no family, so the light stays where the builder put it, at (lx, 4.25, lz): the lamp_post FBX pivot. That pivot is about 0.7 m from the pole (pole at mesh local (-0.06,-0.69), head at (0.11,3.55,0.66)). Route Light_PlazaLamp through PathLamp(): it finds the head through the post's own transform, so it should work at the plaza's 4.6 m scale. Narrow the 120-degree cone in LookLights.Zone1 to about 90-100 degrees.
  - Why it is real: A zoomed crop of the hero lamp shows a dark diffuser, the cone's apex between the pole and the head, and lit flecks on the pole. A crop of the far plaza shows every other post head just as dark.
- **[major] NightCalm_valley_eye.png** (vegetation): On the moon side, the tall grass and gravel go pale silver-cyan: the brightest 5 % of the right-half grass is (47,72,77), and every blade has a white tip. The grass reads as hoar-frost, not wet tropical grass. The left half is dark teal-green (7,19,17). The storm valley shows the same split, and so does play frame 18, where the objective tells the player to hide in this grass.
  - Fix: The terrain grass details use MAT_grass_bermuda_01. LookMaterials gives it the PlantCards smoothness remap of 0.10-0.62 ('waxy tropical leaves, wet'), and the moon is placed low ahead of the route (azimuth 115, elevation 29-31) so it glints toward the camera. Give the grass cards their own SmoothnessRemapMax of about 0.30-0.35, with specular occlusion on and green transmission. Keep 0.62 for broad leaves and puddles only. Optionally raise night indirectDiffuse from 1.5 to about 2.0 to lift the left half.
  - Why it is real: A crop of the right-half grass shows silver highlights on every blade and pale pebbles in the gravel. In 18 the grass on the right reads silver-blue.
- **[major] NightCalm_meadow_eye.png** (vegetation): The lower 45 % of the frame is a flat dark-green carpet, (7.9,22.1,18.2) with a spread of only about 5-6 levels, and a handful of tufts. It reads as a golf-course lawn. The storm version is the same, with 44 % of that frame below 16/255. Nobody walks the meadow at night, so this is mostly a problem for the day prologue, but it is the vista from the plaza rail in every preset.
  - Fix: Likely cause: LookVegetationDetails.Paint fades every grass detail out between 40 and 90 m outside the play area (1 - SS(40, 90, outside), and cells beyond 90 m are skipped). That leaves the meadow as bare terrain texture. Extend cheap tufts and clumps to the part seen from the rail (billboards are fine beyond 55 m). Add large-scale hue and brightness noise, darker clump patches, mud and moss blends, and an animal trail.
  - Why it is real: I measured the spread and can see a uniform lawn texture below the treeline: about five tufts and one rock in the whole lower half.

- **[minor] 15_ch3_path_raptor_crossing.png** (water): The junction puddle is a milky blob (148,141,131), brighter than the wet slab around it (95-101). It does mirror the torch-lit wall, but so blurred that it reads as spilt paint. The puddle in 16 is the same (132,129,125).

## Dropped (not real, overstated, or a capture artefact)

- Finding 16: the '[THUNDER CRACKS OVERHEAD]' caption shows over an unchanged sky.
  - Why not: This is correct behaviour. AtmosphereController.LightningFlashRoutine raises the caption 0.25-0.85 s after the flash ends, because thunder follows lightning. TrexStreamRevealRoutine fires its flashes at the trigger, and the shot comes 2.2 s later.
- Finding 1 as a blocker ('the player reaches the Dock checkpoint and sees a black screen'; truck and canopy 'invisible').
  - Why not: At 1:1 the truck outline and red stripe, the pillars, the dumpster and the moonlit exit doorway all read, so the player can navigate. Kept as a major exposure flaw.
- Finding 5's sub-claim that the hero pool has 'no hot spot or falloff'.
  - Why not: Row profiles across the foreground fall from about 130 to about 50 luma toward the left edge. The merging is real only between the far posts.
- Finding 3 as a major ('the storm does not read as a storm').
  - Why not: In the Play-mode storm frames 17 and 18, heavy rain streaks and valley-floor mist read clearly. The free-camera frames cannot show rain, and they show plaza lamps that the blackout turns off in play. Kept as a minor for the flat sky and the missing long-range haze.

## Missed by the first critic

- **[minor] 18_ch4_trex_reveal.png** (vegetation): The broadleaf trees on the Tyrant Valley skyline are one model repeated. jungle_tree is the only broadleaf in LookVegetation.TreeStems: a bare straight trunk with a flat umbrella crown. Five or six near-identical crowns line the right side of the NightCalm valley, the storm valley and 18 (also the right edge of the NightCalm meadow), standing in grassland. In silhouette they read as a baobab or acacia savanna, not a rainforest, which goes against 'jungle reads as jungle' in a play frame.
  - Fix: Vary scale (about 0.7-1.6) and rotation more, and never put two identical crowns side by side on the skyline. Wrap the jungle_tree trunks with hanging_vines and epiphytes, and plant tree_fern, banana_plant and pachira_aquatica_01 at their feet so the bare trunks don't read. Mix palm_tree and palm_tree_b into the valley skyline.
