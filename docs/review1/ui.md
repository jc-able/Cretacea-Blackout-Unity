# Review 1 (Nov 20, before the lead's look fixes in 07fd73d) — segment ui

Images: Screenshots/review1/ (same camera positions as Tools/lookdev/capture_set.sh and route_test.py).

### Critic segment `ui` (score 6/10)

The UI holds together and is easy to read, but it does not look amazing yet. One warm-black and amber style with real fonts (Barlow, Barlow Condensed, Courier Prime) runs through the HUD, pause, settings, field tablet, document reader and end card, nothing clips or overlaps at 1280x720, and the document reader page could ship in an AAA game. The field tablet lets it down: the Documents tab is a flat beige placeholder page with debug-style '[UNCOLLECTED - ...]' rows, and the route map is one static diagram that gives away what the manual is meant to reveal yet cannot show where the current objective is. On top of that, the blackout HUD keeps three amber elements on screen all the time.

- **[major] journal_tab2.png** (ui): Documents tab: the right 'paper' card (x 530-1128, y 178-617) is a single flat beige fill (#D8C9AB) with zero pixel variation across 580x340 px. It is the largest and brightest thing on screen (about 80% value against a frame average of about 26%) and nearly empty, so it reads as a placeholder. It also contradicts the textured aged paper the document reader uses (document_reader.png, std-dev about 14). Its body text, Courier Prime Regular in dark brown, renders as thin grey strokes at 720p.
  - Likely cause: JournalController.cs BuildDocumentsPage: CreatePanel(page, "DocDetail", new Color(0.86f, 0.80f, 0.68f, 0.97f)) with no sprite; the body uses UiFont.Mono (Regular) at 22f, colour (0.14, 0.12, 0.10).
  - Suggested fix: Use the reader's paper texture (9-sliced, with some edge wear). For an uncollected slot, show a dark PanelCard with a faint page outline and the hint in muted text so the screen stays dark; for a collected one, show the real page. Set paper body text in CourierPrime-Bold at 24-26 px.
- **[major] journal_tab2.png** (ui): All five list rows read like debug output: '1. [UNCOLLECTED - Security Office Desk]', '2. [UNCOLLECTED - Records Annex - East End]' and so on, in brackets with hyphens. The detail card says 'UNCOLLECTED INTEL / Locate this document at: Security Office Desk'. Every row has the same shape, so the list has no hierarchy and looks unfinished.
  - Likely cause: JournalController.cs:743 $"{i + 1}. [UNCOLLECTED - {ArchiveDocs[i].Location}]"; :767 "UNCOLLECTED INTEL"; :779 "Locate this document at: ...".
  - Suggested fix: Make each row two lines: a muted 'NOT FOUND' (or a redacted bar) with a lock or page icon, then a small TextMuted location hint. Change the detail to 'NOT YET RECOVERED' plus one hint line. Keep brackets out of UI copy.
- **[major] journal_tab1.png** (story): These frames come before the manual is read: the objective is 'Search the Records Annex for the 1996 Evacuation Manual.', MASTER KEY shows NOT FOUND and DOCUMENTS 0/5. Yet the tablet already shows 'ROUTE TO E-0 - FOLLOW SERVICE ROAD 3 EAST', 'SUBSTATION 3 / RESTORE CIRCUIT B' and the full six-step Circuit B startup sequence, which is exactly what the manual is supposed to reveal. After reading it, the toast says 'Route map added to Journal'. The chapter goal looks pointless and the toast is untrue.
  - Likely cause: JournalController.cs: BuildMapObjectivesPage, BuildSystemsChecklistPage and RefreshMapTab are not gated on the 'manual_read' flag (only GetNextObjectiveWorldX checks it). The toast comes from StoryDirector.cs OnManualUnlockedRoute.
  - Suggested fix: Until manual_read is set, show only a Visitor Center floor plan with the Records Annex marked, and set the Systems tab to 'NO DATA - locate the 1996 evacuation manual'. Reveal the route, E-0 and the checklist when the manual is read, so the toast is true.
- **[major] journal_tab0.png** (ui): A new player cannot tell where to go. The objective 'Search the Records Annex for the 1996 Evacuation Manual.' says what to do but not where, and it wraps so 'Manual.' sits alone on line 2. The map the HUD points to (TAB: Journal & route map) has no Records Annex at all. Markers follow the route by world X only, so the pulsing objective ring sits on top of the YOU dot on the Visitor Center outline (around x 305-320, y 450).
  - Likely cause: StoryDirector.cs:938 (the checkpoint path in SetupChapter2Records) drops the location given at :915 ('(east end of Staff Corridor) ... evacuation binder') and renames the item from binder to Manual. So a player who dies to the wing raptor and restarts gets the vaguer line. JournalController.cs UpdateLiveMapMarkers maps only world X onto the route.
  - Suggested fix: Use one string in both paths, for example 'Find the 1996 evacuation manual in the Records Annex, east end of the Staff Corridor.' Add a Visitor Center inset (Lobby, Control Room, Staff Corridor, Records Annex) for chapters 1-2 and put the beacon on the room. Widen or balance the objective plate so a single word never sits alone on a line.
- **[minor] journal_tab0.png** (ui): The route map looks like a programmer's diagram next to the document prop. The dashed boundary cuts through the 'Y' of 'TYRANT VALLEY' (x about 645, y about 578). 'PADDOCK 4' touches the cropped bottom edge (y 603 against the edge at 606). The 'YOU' label straddles the Visitor Center outline. The river is a perfect sine wave, and there is no compass, legend or scale. Its cool slate ground (#0C0F0F) with saturated blue and green reads as a different UI from the warm cards around it.
  - Likely cause: The map is a baked 1024x1024 raster from the earlier build (Assets/Resources/UI/Maps/hud_minimap_route.png, Nov 19), tightly cropped by JournalController.cs CropRouteMap.
  - Suggested fix: Redraw it as a 1990s park-service map in the UI palette: warm dark ground, amber route, muted blue water following the terrain, green only for E-0. Keep labels clear of linework, leave a 24 px margin inside the crop, and add a compass and scale bar. Drop the YOU label below the dot when the dot sits on a building edge.
- **[minor] journal_tab1.png** (ui): Rows 1-3 are all marked 'NEXT' in amber (x about 1077, y 253/298/343), so three steps claim to be next. The step text includes tuning values, '(Hold 4.0s)' and '(Hold 2.2s)', inside what is meant to be an in-world procedure, and 'Throw Main TRANSFER Lever' mixes capitalisation.
  - Likely cause: JournalController.cs steps[] (around lines 452-459) and line 711: activeNext ? "NEXT".
  - Suggested fix: Label parallel tasks 'TO DO' and put a counter in the header (BREAKERS 0/3). Reserve NEXT for a single row. Write '(hold)' without seconds, since the hold ring already shows the duration.
- **[minor] 11_ch2_manual_open.png** (story): The right column says the same thing three times: summary paragraph, transcript, and the page itself. The 'transcript' also does not match the page. The page's 7.2 says 'Maintenance Yard (Warehouse B), east sector'; the transcript's 7.2 says 'east perimeter of the Old Maintenance Yard beyond Paddock 4 (Tyrant Valley)'; and the transcript lists 7.4-7.5 steps that are not on the page. Numbered lines wrap without a hanging indent ('ignition.' sits flush left at x 678, y 458). The CONTINUE key chip sits at the right end of its button, while every other prompt puts the key first.
  - Likely cause: MenuController.cs document modal (around lines 784-872): summary, TRANSCRIPT and page content are authored separately.
  - Suggested fix: Make TRANSCRIPT a word-for-word copy of the visible page, or rename it 'FIELD NOTES'. Cut the summary to one line or drop it. Use TMP <indent=2em> for numbered lines. Put the key first: 'E  CONTINUE'.
- **[minor] settings.png** (ui): Two elements look selected at once. The unfocused SAVE & BACK button (#492D0D fill, brown border, x 874-1066, y 567-604) looks as lit as the focused Brightness row (#4A3416 fill, #BD8736 border). A gamepad user cannot tell where focus is. The CONTINUE button in the document reader has the same problem.
  - Likely cause: CretaceaUiFactory.ButtonAccent (0.30, 0.185, 0.06, 0.95) is almost the same as the focus fill.
  - Suggested fix: Unfocused accent buttons: ButtonNormal fill with a 1 px amber border. Focus: the #6E4415 fill with an amber border, plus a small amber chevron. Only one element on a screen should look lit.
- **[minor] settings.png** (ui): Brightness shows '0.0 EV', which is photographic jargon. The hint 'raise it until the treeline is just visible against the sky' cannot be followed from an indoor pause, and there is no reference image. There are no subtitle size or subtitle background options next to the Subtitles toggle, which The Last of Us Part I treats as standard.
  - Likely cause: MenuController.cs around lines 630-656 (formatter appends " EV"; BrightnessNote text).
  - Suggested fix: Show a -5 to +5 or 0-100 scale. Add a calibration panel (a dark logo on near-black plus a night treeline thumbnail rendered from the game), as RE7 and TLOU do. Add Subtitle Size and Subtitle Background rows.
- **[minor] 02_ch1_flashlight_in_hand.png** (ui): The top-right toast 'F  Toggle Tactical Flashlight' (Barlow Medium, mixed case, x 1027-1255, y 22-62) teaches the same key at the same moment as the bottom-left chip 'F  LIGHT ON' (Barlow Condensed caps). That is two prompt styles for one action.
  - Likely cause: FlashlightPickup.cs:133 raises the toast; HudController.cs BuildToast sets the toast text to UiFont.BodyMedium.
  - Suggested fix: Drop the toast and pulse the F chip amber once on pickup, or set toasts in the Label caps style ('F  TOGGLE FLASHLIGHT') so every prompt shares one style.
- **[minor] 06_ch1_blackout_talon_breakout.png** (ui): Three amber elements never leave the screen. In every blackout frame the top-centre eye reads 'EXPOSED' (x 579-699, y 20-72), even in an empty corridor, because it is tied to the flashlight being on. The objective banner never fades, and the LIGHT ON chip is permanent. Alien: Isolation and RE7 keep the screen clean so dread can build; here the meter becomes noise.
  - Likely cause: HudController.cs UpdateStealthEyeVisual shows EXPOSED when visibility > 0.95 at alpha 0.78 whenever the blackout flag is set; the objective banner drops only to 0.86 after its 4.5 s highlight.
  - Suggested fix: Show the eye only while a predator can hear or see the player, or for 3 s after a state change. Fade the objective banner out after about 8 s and bring it back when the objective changes or on TAB. Fade the light chip a few seconds after each toggle.
- **[minor] 06_ch1_blackout_talon_breakout.png** (ui): The centre reticle disappears on bright surfaces. It is 7 px at 1080p (about 4.7 px here), off-white at 78% alpha, with a halo of at most 0.33 alpha. Over the lit window glass in 06, and over the lamp in 02, it cannot be picked out at (640, 360).
  - Likely cause: HudController.cs BuildCenterReticleAndPrompt: size (7f, 7f), colour (1, 0.97, 0.9, 0.78); Assets/Resources/UI/HUD/crosshair_dot.png is 16x16 with only a faint dark edge.
  - Suggested fix: Give the dot a 2 px dark ring (black at about 55%) and make it 6-8 px at 1080p, so it reads on both glass and black.
- **[minor] journal_tab0.png** (ui): The smallest text, measured at 720p: the HUD 'TAB' chip glyphs and the 'JOURNAL & ROUTE MAP' hint are 7 px tall (about 10 px at 1080p); the tablet footer chips and NEXT/LOCKED are 8 px. Readable on a desktop monitor, faint on a TV or handheld.
  - Likely cause: Hint and footer labels use 18-19 reference px; CretaceaUiFactory key-chip text is height * 0.5.
  - Suggested fix: Raise hints, chips and state labels to at least 14 px cap height at 1080p (20-22 reference px, chip glyph at least 60% of the chip height).
- **[minor] 29_end_screen.png** (colour): Every inner card (map frame, objective side card, document list, systems page, end-screen stat tiles) renders #15150D, and the route-status box #0F0F03. That is a khaki/olive cast, while the tablet and pause cards are neutral #0D0D0D and the factory comment promises a warm near-black. Next to the amber accent, the cards look dirty green.
  - Likely cause: CretaceaUiFactory.PanelCard (0.078, 0.071, 0.066, 0.92) should render about #141211. Something in the CretaceaPanelGraphic fill and border layering shifts it; I could not pin it down in the code.
  - Suggested fix: Render a PanelCard over pure black and sample it to find the tint. Then target warm values: about #16120F for cards and #0E0B09 for modals.
- **[minor] 29_end_screen.png** (ui): The end card floats on a flat #000000 background. The dawn coast, the game's payoff image, never appears behind it. The credits are 19 px muted body text (about 6 px x-height at 720p) under a lot of empty black.
  - Likely cause: MenuController.cs BuildEndingOverlay: a 0.88 backdrop over a full black screen fade.
  - Suggested fix: Keep the dawn-coast camera running behind the card (blurred, dimmed to 50-60%) or slowly panning. Set credits at about 22 px.
- **[minor] pause.png** (ui): In every modal capture (pause, settings, the three tablet tabs, the document reader, the end screen), the right-most pixel column (x=1279) shows the scene undimmed and changes between frames. On the black end screen it is a full-height tan line (about #A69879).
  - Likely cause: The overlay UI does not draw the last column at 1280x720. Even the backdrop vignette, which extends 80 px past the edges (MenuController.cs CreateBackdrop), misses it, so the overlay surface is probably 1 px narrower than the capture (Game view size or the capture path).
  - Suggested fix: Check in a 1920x1080 player build. If it reproduces there, extend the root canvas or screen-fade image 2 px past the edges; if not, fix the capture size.
