Gemini 4 Neon ported it in Antigravity (Nov 19-20 2026), and Claude (Fable 5.2, then Opus 5.5, in Claude Code) gave it its look, motion and fixes (Nov 20-21). ### Textures, HDRIs, plants and rocks
- **Poly Haven** (https://polyhaven.com), CC0 1.0 public domain: PBR texture sets (`Assets/Art/Textures/`), 5 HDRIs (`Assets/Art/HDRIs/`), plant models (`Assets/Art/Foliage/PolyHaven/`) and rock models (`Assets/Art/Foliage/Rocks/`). Some were recoloured, and the terrain textures in `Assets/Art/Textures/terrain_jungle/` were derived from them by `Assets/Art/Terrain/Source~/derive_terrain_textures.py`.
- Decals, rain and puddle textures, the flashlight cookie and the star cubemap were made procedurally for this project.

### 3D models
- **Tripo AI** (https://www.tripo3d.ai), Tripo v3.1 text-to-model and auto-rigging: props, vegetation, vehicles, structures, the dinosaurs and the visitor characters (`Assets/Art/`). Generated by the creator on his own account, and Tripo's terms for generated output apply. They were re-exported and adjusted in Blender for Unity; the first-person flashlight hand was rebuilt in Blender from a Tripo model. Eight models are new Tripo v3.1 text-to-model generations made by the Gemini agent during the port: `wrecked_kiosk` (task 6ca4b4ad-bdb9-45c1-b4d6-c501726183e4), `diesel_fuel_tank_pump` (f9e08350-f575-4efc-b518-1baebcf88687), `generator_starter_panel` (f77b37b0-9dcb-4111-97ec-ced9c2d9e829), `industrial_valve_wheel` (58b8eaa9-df06-47ae-ae17-035ee8d47804), `power_transfer_switch` (57c9b46e-93de-4f76-b377-2da68c3ffbe3), the gloved arm in `flashlight_fps_rig.fbx` (a8ce6e1e-23e7-4dbb-9cc0-e06c83e52ccd), and new versions of `control_console` (8b4685c8-d010-4b68-9941-051179effedd) and `gate_control_box` (5bce4dd0-33d8-4072-aaa2-568659be54d6) that replace the Godot models of the same name. All other Tripo models come from the Godot version; their task ids are in the Godot repository's `docs/ASSET_SOURCES.md` and `assets/models/creatures_manifest.json` (https://github.com/jc-able/Cretacea-Blackout-Godot).
- The buildings, terrain and coast-guard boat are built by the project's editor scripts (`Assets/Editor/WorldBuilder/`, `Assets/Editor/LookPass/`).

### Audio
- **VARCO Sound** by NC AI (https://varco.ai): sound effects, ambience and music, generated by the creator on his own account and processed for the game. VARCO's terms for generated output apply. The VARCO Sound Unity plugin is an Asset Store product and is **not included**. To generate new sounds, install it from the Unity Asset Store yourself; `Assets/Audio/varco_sound_brief.json` has the prompts.
- **Fish Audio** text-to-speech (https://fish.audio), model s2.1-pro-free: 17 voice lines using the library voices Hannah and Robotic System Voice (Fish Audio) and the community voices jijri and Soldier. No voices of real people were cloned. Fish Audio's terms apply.
- The interior room tone (`amb_interior_hum_loop.wav`), the fuel-pump hiss (`fuel_pump_primed.ogg`) and the terminal beep (`ui_terminal_beep.ogg`) were synthesized for this project with NumPy; they are covered by the CC0 LICENSE.
- Using the VARCO sounds, the Fish Audio voices or the Tripo models above commercially is subject to those services' terms. Check those terms, or replace the files, before you sell a game built on them.
