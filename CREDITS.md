# Credits

Cretacea: Blackout (Unity version) is a port of the Godot game. Gemini 4 Neon ported it in Antigravity (Nov 19-20 2026), and Claude (Fable 5.2, then Opus 5.5, in Claude Code) gave it its look, motion and fixes (Nov 20-21). Everything was built by AI coding agents for the YouTube creator JC. The repository LICENSE covers the game code and the art made for this project. Third-party content listed below keeps its own licence.

### Engine and packages
- **Unity 6** (6000.6.9f1) with the **High Definition Render Pipeline 17.6**, under the Unity terms of service.
- Packages are fetched by the Unity Package Manager from Unity's registry (see `Packages/manifest.json`); none are stored in this repo: Input System, Cinemachine, AI Navigation, glTFast (Apache-2.0), uGUI / TextMesh Pro, Unity Pipeline, IDE integrations.
- **TextMesh Pro Essential Resources** (`Assets/TextMesh Pro/`), Unity Technologies, Unity Companion License. https://unity.com/legal/licenses/unity-companion-license
- HDRP default settings assets in `Assets/Settings/`, generated by the HDRP package (Unity Companion License).

### Textures, HDRIs, plants and rocks
- **Poly Haven** (https://polyhaven.com), CC0 1.0 public domain: PBR texture sets (`Assets/Art/Textures/`), 5 HDRIs (`Assets/Art/HDRIs/`), plant models (`Assets/Art/Foliage/PolyHaven/`) and rock models (`Assets/Art/Foliage/Rocks/`). Some were recoloured, and the terrain textures were derived from them (`Assets/Art/Terrain/Source~/`).
- Decals, rain and puddle textures, the flashlight cookie and the star cubemap were made procedurally for this project.

### 3D models
- **Tripo AI** (https://www.tripo3d.ai), Tripo v3.1 text-to-model and auto-rigging: props, vegetation, vehicles, structures, the dinosaurs and the visitor characters (`Assets/Art/`). Generated by the creator on his own account, and Tripo's terms for generated output apply. They were re-exported and adjusted in Blender for Unity; the first-person flashlight hand was rebuilt in Blender from a Tripo model. Four props (`diesel_fuel_tank_pump`, `generator_starter_panel`, `industrial_valve_wheel`, `power_transfer_switch`) were remade during the Gemini port from the Godot version's raw Tripo models; their exact generation step was not recorded.
- The buildings, terrain and coast-guard boat are built by the project's editor scripts (`Assets/Editor/WorldBuilder/`, `Assets/Editor/LookPass/`).

### Audio
- **VARCO Sound** by NC AI (https://varco.ai): sound effects, ambience and music, generated by the creator on his own account and processed for the game. VARCO's terms for generated output apply. The VARCO Sound Unity plugin is an Asset Store product and is **not included**. To generate new sounds, install it from the Unity Asset Store yourself; `Assets/Audio/varco_sound_brief.json` has the prompts.
- **Fish Audio** text-to-speech (https://fish.audio), model s2.1-pro-free: 17 voice lines using the library voices Hannah and Robotic System Voice (Fish Audio) and the community voices jijri and Soldier. No voices of real people were cloned. Fish Audio's terms apply.
- Using these generated sounds and voices commercially is subject to the VARCO and Fish Audio terms. Check those terms, or replace the files, before you sell a game built on them.
